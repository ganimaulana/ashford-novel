## 1. CORE STORY ARCHITECTURE

### Overall Structure

The story progresses through three major movements:

**Movement 1 — THE BELLWEATHER CYCLE (Ch 1–47)**
- Leon arrives in Alderwick, inherits No. 13 Bellweather Lane.
- Discovers the annual 13–21 November disturbance, the symbol (circle+3 lines+dot), and the Bellweather estate's hidden history.
- Encounters the Bell (tolls at 00:00 on specific nights), the lamp, the mill-yard gate, and the vanished bell.
- Learns of Aurelius Ashcroft's will, the partial inheritance, and the Agreement.
- Meets key characters: Helen, Calloway, Margaret, Arthur, Daniel, the clerk, and the mysterious stranger.
- Establishes the supernatural rules: 00:00 anomalies, the Gear, the Door, the reappearing house, the Order of Severence.
- Ends with Vale's death and Leon becoming the inheritor of the Door's responsibility.

**Movement 2 — THE KEEPER'S DUTY (Ch 48–86)**
- Leon begins guarding the Door, exploring the passage, the chamber, the reappearing house.
- Discovers the two marks (Symbols A and B), the nineteenth sign, the eastern marker.
- Conflicts with the Order of Severence; learns of factions.
- Reads the map, the Agreement, and begins keeping the account.
- Finds the second medallion, the bell, the key, and the lock.
- Realizes the Door is not a door but a memory, and the Keeper's duty is to keep the account.
- Passes the keeping to Daniel Wren.
- Leaves Alderwick.

**Movement 3 — THE HUNTER (Ch 87–137)**
- Leon becomes a hunter of Arc Items and information.
- Explores the marsh, the house on the hill, the crossroads, the river, the sea.
- Discovers the third medallion and the full tally (count of keepers).
- Learns that the count is not a count of years but a count of keepers.
- Meets the keeper of the tally.
- Ends at the sea, understanding that the calling is about the journey, not the destination.
