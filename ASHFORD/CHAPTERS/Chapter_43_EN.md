CHAPTER 43 — THE FIRST TEST

My first assignment arrived without warning.

A man had been moving the boundary stones.

The Order called him a rogue.

I found him near the old mill.

He was younger than I expected.

He did not run.

"You've come to stop me."

"That depends."

"On what?"

"Whether you're actually breaking anything."

He laughed.

"You are learning."

He showed me the stone he had moved.

It was not an original boundary stone.

It had been placed recently.

A test.

Not of the agreement.

Of me.

"Who sent you?"

"No one."

"Then why?"

He looked toward the mill.

"Because everyone here thinks the same thing."

"What thing?"

"That the keeper should obey."

I looked at him.

"And you?"

"I want to know whether you can choose."

He walked away.

I did not stop him.

When I reported to Calloway, he asked:

"Did you complete your assignment?"

"No."

"Why?"

"Because I don't know whether the assignment was legitimate."

Calloway nodded.

"Good."

It was the first time he had praised me.
