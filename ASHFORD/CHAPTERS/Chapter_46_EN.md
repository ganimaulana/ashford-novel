CHAPTER 46 — THE RENEWAL

The old constable was dying.

He told me plainly.

"I have kept the agreement for fifty years."

"And now?"

"Now you will."

He gave me the gear.

I held it.

It was warm.

"What happens when I renew it?"

"You will know that you have done it."

That was not an answer.

But it was the only one he gave.

At the mill, I placed the gear into the stone.

This time it turned.

The door opened.

Not widely.

Only enough for darkness to appear beyond it.

I heard something breathing.

I stopped.

The gear remained in my hand.

I could have opened the door farther.

I did not.

Instead, I turned the gear back.

The door closed.

The ground became still.

I had renewed the agreement.

But I had not learned what was behind it.

And somehow, that frightened me more.
