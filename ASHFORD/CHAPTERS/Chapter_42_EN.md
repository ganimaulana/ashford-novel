CHAPTER 42 — THE WATCHMAN

The Order did not recruit me immediately.

They watched.

I watched them watching.

That lasted several weeks.

Eventually Crowe offered me a test.

A coin.

"Carry it."

"Why?"

"You will learn."

I took it.

Nothing happened.

For seven days, nothing happened.

That was the test.

I realised that the Order valued obedience more than curiosity.

That troubled me.

At the end of the seventh day, Eleanor met me beneath the mill.

"You passed."

"What was the test?"

"Whether you would obey without knowing why."

"And did I?"

"You did."

She handed me the coin again.

"Then you may choose a name."

I thought of the town.

The stones.

The watching.

"The Watchman."

She nodded.

"Then Watchman you shall be."

I accepted the name.

But I did not accept the Order.

Not completely.

That distinction would matter later.
