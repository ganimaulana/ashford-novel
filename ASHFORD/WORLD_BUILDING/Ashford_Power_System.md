# Ashford Power System

**Status:** 📗 CANON
**Version:** 1.2
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-01
**Canon Status:** CANON — conflict resolved per author decision (option b): Imprint exists as a supernatural mechanism; Order of Severance does NOT originate or control it.

## Purpose

This document defines the supernatural/power framework of the Ashford novel. It is a CANON REFERENCE.

## Guiding Principles

- Not an RPG leveling system.
- Abilities are personal, specific, and have limitations, conditions, and consequences.
- Strategy and understanding are more important than raw power.
- A character's knowledge of a supernatural system can matter as much as raw supernatural capability.
- Mystery and ambiguity are preserved where the canon has not yet provided an answer.
- "Power" and "knowledge" are conceptually separate.

## 1. Pneuma

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Self / living human |
| Learnable | Yes |
| Controllable | Yes |

Pneuma is the fundamental supernatural force accessible to living humans. It originates from within the self — the living human body and spirit. Pneuma can be learned, trained, and controlled over time.

### Nature
- Pneuma is personal — no two individuals manifest or control it identically.
- Pneuma is distinct from Arc Items, Agreement, Faith, Anomaly, and Imprint.
- Pneuma is not a "power level" system. Mastery, understanding, and application are more significant than raw capacity.

### Sub-techniques
- **TBD** — No sub-techniques have been canonically established. Specific techniques, if any, are to be developed in later chapters. Do not invent classifications (e.g., "categories," "types," "affinities") unless they emerge from canon prose.

### Consequence
- The use of Pneuma may carry costs, risks, or limitations that are not yet fully documented. These are to be revealed as the narrative requires, not pre-defined.

## 2. Arc Items

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Objects / phenomena |
| Learnable | Conditional |
| Controllable | Conditional |

Arc Items are supernatural objects or phenomena whose properties do not simply originate from the current wielder. An Arc Item may possess its own rules, history, conditions, or effects.

### Nature
- Arc Items are distinct from Pneuma. An Arc Item's power is not generated by the wielder's living energy.
- Arc Items may be studied, understood, and used under specific conditions, but they are not "learned" in the same way Pneuma is.
- Not every Arc Item needs to be combat-oriented or powerful. Some may be mundane, weak, or seemingly useless.
- Arc Items may have negative effects, conditions, or drawbacks.
- Arc Items may have a history — they may have been owned, used, or created by individuals in the past.

### Relationship to Other Systems
- An Arc Item may interact with Pneuma, Agreement, Faith, or Anomaly, but it is not reducible to any of them.
- An Arc Item is not an Imprint (see §6).

### Existing Canon
- The full design and staged reveal of Arc Items is documented in `WORLD_BIBLE/09_MYSTERY/ARC_Item_System_Leon.md`. The current document does not overwrite that file.
- Arc Items known to have appeared in canon include, but are not limited to: the gear (Symbol B, keeper's object), the medallion (Symbol A, first sign), the four clocks, the key, the iron box, the sounding device, and the compass (see `ARC_Item_System_Leon.md`).

## 3. Agreement

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Pact / agreement |
| Learnable | Yes |
| Controllable | Conditional |

Agreement is a supernatural mechanism in which a binding relationship, condition, promise, or exchange produces an effect.

### Nature
- Agreements are not generic "power-up contracts." They are specific, conditional, and often irreversible.
- The exact mechanics and limits of Agreement are consistent with existing Ashford canon (the Agreement signed by Aurelius Ashcroft, the town's agreement to see nothing, keeper-related agreements).
- An Agreement may be written, spoken, or implied. It may involve parties who are not human.
- An Agreement may produce effects that are supernatural in nature, but the Agreement itself is a binding relationship, not a "spell" or "technique."

### Known Canon Examples
- The Agreement signed by Aurelius Ashcroft, concerning the door beneath the mill and the boundary.
- The town's agreement to see nothing and to forget (the "agreement to see nothing" from Ch 9–35 canon).
- Keeper-related agreements, including the transmission of the keeper's duty from Vale to Leon.

## 4. Faith

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Relationship of belief / faith |
| Learnable | Conditional |
| Controllable | Conditional |

Faith is a supernatural mechanism that arises from a relationship of belief, devotion, or faith. It is conceptually distinct from Pneuma.

### Nature
- Religious/theological factions may interpret the source and nature of Faith differently. These interpretations may contradict each other.
- The existence, identity, or nature of a god/deity is NOT definitively established by this document. Ambiguity is preserved unless and until canon explicitly establishes otherwise.
- Faith may produce effects that resemble those of other systems, but its origin (belief relationship) is distinct.
- The conditions for learning, accessing, or controlling Faith are not fully defined. These are to be revealed as the narrative requires.

### Limitations
- Faith is not merely "Pneuma accessed through belief." The two systems are distinct.
- A character may have Faith without having Pneuma, and vice versa.

## 5. Anomaly

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Unknown |
| Learnable | No / Conditional |
| Controllable | No |

Anomaly represents supernatural phenomena whose underlying mechanism is unknown or cannot currently be explained by the established systems (Pneuma, Arc Item, Agreement, Faith, Imprint).

### Nature
- Anomaly must NOT automatically be classified as Pneuma, Arc Item, Agreement, Faith, or Imprint simply because it is unexplained.
- Anomaly is important for maintaining the mystery layer of Ashford. Not all phenomena must be categorized.
- Some Anomalies may later be reclassified as a known system once sufficient understanding is achieved. Others may remain permanently anomalous.
- Anomaly is not a "category" that can be learned or controlled — it is a designation for the currently unknown.

### Relationship to Other Systems
- If a phenomenon is later understood to be a form of Pneuma, Arc Item, Agreement, Faith, or Imprint, it should be reclassified accordingly.
- Until then, it remains Anomaly.

## 6. Imprint

**Status:** 📗 CANON

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Source | Remnants associated with the dead; transplantation |
| Learnable | No as a general system |
| Controllable | Conditional |

Imprint is a supernatural mechanism in which remnants associated with a deceased individual are separated from the dead and transplanted into a new living host. It is NOT a general power system that any person can learn — it depends on specific conditions, procedures, or circumstances that are not yet fully defined.

### Nature
- An Imprint may contain or preserve elements such as supernatural ability, instinct, memory, behavioral patterns, emotional residue, or other remnants of the deceased, depending on the established canon.
- Transplantation is not guaranteed to be perfect. Potential incompatibility, rejection, and residual effects may exist.
- The transplanted Imprint must remain distinct from the host's original Pneuma.
- Imprint is not simply "stolen power." The emphasis is on remnants being separated from the dead and transplanted into a new vessel.

### Relationship to the Order of Severance
Per Phase 3H D7 and `Order_of_Severence.md` §106, the Order of Severance does NOT originate, control, or possess supernatural abilities. The Order may:
- **Know** about Imprint phenomena
- **Investigate** cases of Imprint
- **Guard boundaries** related to Imprint
- **Deal with** the consequences of Imprint
- **Observe and record** Imprint instances

The Order does NOT have the ability to perform Imprint transplantation itself. The source of the transplantation process — who or what performs it — is **UNRESOLVED** and may be explored in future canon.

This distinction preserves `Order_of_Severence.md` §106 while allowing Imprint to exist as a supernatural phenomenon in the world.

### Relationship to Other Systems
- An Imprint is NOT an Arc Item. An Imprint originates from a deceased living being, not from an object or phenomenon.
- An Imprint is NOT Pneuma. Even after transplantation, the Imprint remains distinct from the host's original Pneuma.
- An Imprint may interact with other systems, but it is not reducible to any of them.

## 7. Canon Conflicts & Author Decisions

### 7.1 Imprint vs. Order of Severence (RESOLVED)

**Location:** `WORLD_BIBLE/08_UNDERGROUND/Order_of_Severence.md`, line 106.

**Conflict:** The initial draft of Imprint as a mechanism *controlled* by the Order of Severance conflicted with Order_of_Severence.md §106 ("No Power/Power-User content is canon").

**Resolution (option b, author-approved 2026-09-01):** Imprint exists as a supernatural phenomenon in the world, but the Order of Severance does NOT originate or control it. The Order may investigate, guard boundaries, or deal with Imprint cases, but does not possess the supernatural ability to perform transplantation. The source of the transplantation process remains UNRESOLVED.

**Status:** RESOLVED. No changes to Order_of_Severence.md were required.

### 7.2 Anomaly Terminology Overlap (CLARIFICATION RECOMMENDED)

**Location:** `WORLD_BIBLE/13_REFERENCE/Supernatural_System.md`, line 13.

**Issue:** The existing Supernatural_System.md lists "Anomaly" as one of seven Affinity categories (a temporary concept). This document defines "Anomaly" as a distinct power system. The two uses share a name.

**Assessment:** Not a hard contradiction (the existing file marks its concepts as temporary/internal), but may cause confusion in the future.

**Recommendation:** Author may wish to update Supernatural_System.md to rename or clarify its use of "Anomaly" when convenient.
