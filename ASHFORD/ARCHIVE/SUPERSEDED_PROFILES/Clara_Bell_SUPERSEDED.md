## Power: Regeneration
Minor regeneration. Small wounds heal within minutes. Serious wounds require longer.

## Background
One of the earliest Power Users to voluntarily donate tissue. She trusted Elias Mercer completely. Her tissue became a baseline for early transplantation research.

## Cost
Repeated tissue harvesting gradually weakens her own Power.

## Core Tragedy
She gives away her Power so ordinary humans can become equal to her while she gradually becomes ordinary herself.
