## Basic Identity

- **Age:** 47

- **Power:** None

## Background
Working-class Ashford origin. Became a physician and witnessed institutional exploitation surrounding Power Users.

He concluded that society could not become genuinely equal while Power remained a biological dividing line.

He discovered Residual Power in tissue from deceased Power Users and became the first successful researcher to transplant residual Power into an ordinary human.

## Personality
- Calm, intellectual, paternalistic
- Can discuss violent actions using detached medical language

## Core Conflict
He sincerely wants to eliminate Power inequality but becomes increasingly convinced that he can control the consequences of his discovery.
