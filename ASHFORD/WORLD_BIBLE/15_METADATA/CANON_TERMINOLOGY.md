## 2. Definitions

### Holder
A Holder is someone who possesses or has authority over something such as: an object, an access mechanism, a supernatural function, the Door, or another specific authority. "Holder" is NOT a power level. Not every occurrence of "keeper" in the manuscript is a "Holder" — each must be context-audited.

### Observer / Seeker (UNRESOLVED)
The old "Watcher" terminology is being replaced by either "Observer" (witness, monitor, perceive) or "Seeker" (search, investigate, pursue). The final choice depends on function. Do not use either term as a power level.

### Token vs Sign
- **SIGN** = The supernatural identity, symbol, or underlying concept. (e.g., the first sign, the keeper's sign, the Order's sign, the XIII Signs).
- **TOKEN** = The physical object associated with or representing a Sign. (e.g., the three medallions, the gear, the bell).

Do not refer to all Signs as Tokens. Do not reveal all XIII Signs merely because the terminology is canonized.

### Tier
The supernatural power hierarchy:

Tier 9 → Tier 8 → Tier 7 → Tier 6 → Tier 5 → Tier 4 → Tier 3 → Tier 2 → Tier 1

Tier 0 remains UNRESOLVED / POSSIBLE but not established. Do NOT invent Tier 0 abilities, entities, rituals, mechanics, or lore. The only thing established at this stage is the hierarchy terminology. Do NOT assume the entire nine-Tier system has already been designed. Do NOT force the Tier system into Chapters 1–137.

### Rank
General terminology for a person's position or status within the power hierarchy. Do not use "Sequence" as the name of the power system. Do not automatically replace every historical or quoted use of "sequence" in existing documents.

### Resonance
Replaces the conceptual role previously associated with Potion, Acting, and artificial power digestion. Resonance represents the process of establishing compatibility/alignment with supernatural power. The exact mechanics are NOT fully defined yet. Do NOT invent recipes, ingredients, rituals, numerical resonance values, fixed resonance thresholds, or guaranteed advancement procedures.

### Drift
Replaces the general concept previously represented by Sanity, Madness, and loss of humanity. Drift is NOT simply another word for insanity. It represents progressive deviation from the normal human condition caused by supernatural influence. Do NOT invent percentages, numerical meters, fixed thresholds, universal symptoms, or automatic madness rules.

### Anchors
Replace the previous concept of "Connection Slots." Anchors are points through which supernatural connections can occur. Do NOT define Anchors as chairs, seats, people, power levels, or Signs unless future canon explicitly establishes such a relationship. The physical appearance and exact mechanics of Anchors remain open.

### XIII Signs
The system contains thirteen Signs. However: DO NOT reveal all thirteen immediately. DO NOT create a complete definitive list unless existing canon already establishes it. DO NOT invent missing Signs simply to complete the list. The existence of XIII Signs can remain a mystery. Individual Signs may be discovered gradually through plot, evidence, objects, visions, documents, or encounters — never through exposition dumps.

### Component / Gear / Key
Establish the following conceptual hierarchy:

```
COMPONENT
├── Gear
├── Key
└── Other possible components — UNDEFINED
```

Gear is therefore ONE COMPONENT. It is NOT the entire system, the sole method of opening the Door, or the central object of every supernatural event. Other components may exist but must not be invented prematurely. Key remains "Key."

### 00:00 Marker
The relevant supernatural time marker is 00:00. It is associated with supernatural activation/access phenomena. Do NOT reduce it to an ordinary clock reference when the context clearly indicates supernatural significance. However: DO NOT explain the complete meaning of 00:00 prematurely. DO NOT create a complete mechanical explanation yet. The narrative must establish its importance gradually.

### Call / Bell
The Bell functions as a signal associated with a Call. A simplified conceptual relationship:

```
00:00
↓
possible activation window
↓
Bell / Call
↓
possible connection
```

This is NOT yet a fully locked mechanical sequence. Do not invent additional rules. The Bell should retain mystery. The absence of a Bell/Call may itself eventually have narrative meaning, but do not force this into existing chapters without evidence.

### The Door
Use "The Door" as the primary terminology. Do NOT replace it globally with "Threshold." "Threshold" may be used descriptively in normal English when appropriate, but the supernatural object itself should be called The Door. The Door may eventually be associated with Components, Key, 00:00, Call, and The Sanctuary, but these relationships must be revealed gradually.

### The Sanctuary
The former concept of "Room / Connection Space" is now "The Sanctuary." The Sanctuary is a supernatural space where multiple connections can occur. It is conceptually similar to a shared supernatural meeting environment, but DO NOT copy another work's exact structure, mechanics, terminology, or presentation. Do NOT define: exact number of Anchors, permanent occupants, complete architecture, origin, complete rules, or ultimate purpose unless supported by future canon. The Sanctuary should remain mysterious.

### Power Title Format
The power system may eventually use:
```
[DESCRIPTOR] + [SIGN IDENTITY]

Example:
Marked Knight
Bound Knight
Resonant Knight
Ascendant Knight
Crowned Knight
```

These are FORMAT EXAMPLES ONLY. Do NOT lock these five titles as the complete progression. Do NOT create all nine levels yet. The intended structure is: **Tier + Sign Identity + appropriate title/state**. For example: `[Title] [Sign]`. The exact terminology for each Tier will be designed later.
