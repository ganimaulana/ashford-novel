## 1. Overall Story Architecture

Alderwick is a slow-burn supernatural mystery following Leon Ashcroft from outsider to keeper.

**Core progression:**
```
ordinary life
→ strange incidents
→ investigation
→ personal involvement
→ responsibility
→ discovery
→ experimentation
→ understanding
→ mastery through intelligence rather than raw power.
```

**Setting:** Alderwick, 1816–1820+. Gothic English market town; Bellweather Lane; St. Bartholomew's; the common; the mill; the boundary wall; Bellweather House.

**Core conflict:** A keeper's duty to guard the "place beneath" Alderwick vs. forces (Order, stranger, watchers, the count itself) that would open or end the keeping.

## 5. Long-Term Roadmap

**Future arcs must emerge naturally:**
```
CAUSE → CONSEQUENCE → INVESTIGATION → DISCOVERY → DECISION → NEW CONSEQUENCE
```

**Avoid:** `NEW ARC → NEW SYSTEM → NEW EXPLANATION → NEW ARC`.

**Mystery principle:** Establish anomaly → let Leon notice it → allow an initial interpretation → introduce contradiction → escalate → reveal partial truth → create a larger question.

**Source:** `15_ARC_ROADMAP.md`

---

## 6. Structural Constraints

- **No RPG-style levels.** Power progression is through knowledge, experience, observation, preparation, equipment, combination, understanding, control.
- **No arbitrary numerical power ranking.**
- **Do NOT assign fixed chapter numbers to Arc 2–10.** Each future arc may contain 5, 10, 20, 50, or more chapters depending on what the story requires.
- **Do NOT compress an arc to reach the next planned arc.**
- **Do NOT extend an arc merely to hit a word/chapter target.**
- **Do NOT retroactively force later explanations into Arc 1.**
