## 1. Overall Story Architecture

Alderwick is a slow-burn supernatural mystery following Leon Ashcroft from outsider to keeper.

**Core progression:**
```
ordinary life
→ strange incidents
→ investigation
→ personal involvement
→ responsibility
→ discovery
→ experimentation
→ understanding
→ mastery through intelligence rather than raw power.
```

**Setting:** Alderwick, 1816–1820+. Gothic English market town; Bellweather Lane; St. Bartholomew's; the common; the mill; the boundary wall; Bellweather House.

**Core conflict:** A keeper's duty to guard the "place beneath" Alderwick vs. forces (Order, stranger, watchers, the count itself) that would open or end the keeping.

## 2. Arc Structure

### ARC 1 — BELLWEATHER (Ch 1–137; actual Ch 1–141 after renumbering)
- **Narrative function:** Establish Leon and Alderwick; establish Bellweather and the initial supernatural mystery; establish local history and major early mysteries; establish the first layer of the supernatural world.
- **Purpose:** establish atmosphere, the three signs, the boundary, the gear, the door, the Agreement, the keeper's duty, and the central mystery of the "place beneath."
- **Status:** ACTIVE CANON.

### Examination Arc — CH80–99 (within ARC 1)

**Overall purpose:** Expand the examination from a small group into a visibly larger cohort, testing candidates through social complexity, information asymmetry, and consequential decisions rather than isolated puzzles.

**Phases:**

**Phase I — Scale / Cohort Formation (CH80–84):**
- CH80 — THE CROWD: Expand the examination to a visibly larger cohort. Introduce many additional candidates; only ~5–6 recurring candidates become named/recognizable.
- CH81 — THE NAMES: Establish recurring candidates primarily through actions, behavior, dialogue, and reactions.
- CH82 — THE NEXT TEST: Move examination away from isolated room/object puzzles; introduce movement-based examination; competition emerges naturally.
- CH83 — THE DIVISION: Groups split and recombine; force candidates to work with different people.
- CH84 — THE OTHER SIDE: Demonstrate that Leon is not the best at everything; different candidates possess different forms of competence. Suspicion about Leon's "inside connection" remains ambiguous.

**Phase II — Social Game / Information Economy / Trust (CH85–89):**
- CH85 — THE BARGAIN: Information becomes socially valuable and tradable; information brokerage as social engine.
- CH86 — THE HUNTER: Introduce a highly perceptive candidate who watches other participants; preserve ambiguity around motive.
- CH87 — THE PRICE: A group decision produces a moderate but real consequence/loss; Leon noticed a potential risk but withheld it because he was uncertain; develops epistemic/moral complexity.
- CH88 — THE SEPARATION: Force separation/reassignment; demonstrate that relationships in the cohort are fluid.
- CH89 — THE STRANGER: Introduce a difficult-to-read recurring candidate; may help or hinder unpredictably; not immediately labeled as friend, enemy, villain, or ally.

**Phase III — Competition / Harder Choices (CH90–95):**
- CH90 — THE RULE: Introduce one new simple rule that changes candidate behavior; keep grounded; avoid elaborate scoring.
- CH91 — THE CHASE: Competitive pursuit of a shared objective; Leon wins through sound judgment rather than speed; outcome mixed.
- CH92 — THE CHOICE: Leon encounters someone who needs assistance; helping costs his group an advantage; decision not framed as obviously morally correct.
- CH93 — THE FALL: A highly competent candidate fails because a reasonable decision produces a bad consequence; reinforce competence ≠ certainty.
- CH94 — THE ALLIANCE: Establish a practical alliance based on mutual utility; avoid sentimental friendship framing; someone else may lead.
- CH95 — THE FALSE FRIEND: Avoid melodramatic betrayal; apparent ally has conflicting interest; Leon must decide whether to disclose information; preserve ambiguity.

**Phase IV — Reduction / Survivors / Payoff (CH96–99):**
- CH96 — THE SECOND FIELD: Change the examination environment using only confirmed World Bible locations.
- CH97 — THE HUNT: Group pursuit of a shared objective while other groups compete; allow deception, observation, cooperation; Leon notices something others missed.
- CH98 — THE LAST GROUP: Candidate numbers begin to fall; recurring characters receive meaningful payoff; unexpected traits revealed.
- CH99 — THE SURVIVORS: Establish remaining survivors (not final result); show how candidates have changed; Leon remains one survivor among others.

**Leon's progression through the cohort:**
- CH80: Leon = nobody
- CH84: Leon = noticeable
- CH88: Leon = someone people remember
- CH92: Leon = someone people make choices around
- CH96: Leon = someone with a reputation
- CH99: Leon = one of the survivors

**Core structural principles:**
1. The examination should feel like a large living cohort, not a chain of disconnected puzzles.
2. Increase difficulty through scale, social complexity, information complexity, uncertainty, and character complexity.
3. Leon survives through competence, not hidden supernatural strength.
4. Leon is not a chosen one, hidden powerhouse, or always right.
5. Other candidates must remain genuinely competent; do not make them stupid to elevate Leon.
6. Social dynamics and information exchange are major engines of the examination.
7. Avoid exposition dumps; world-building embedded through movement, conversation, buildings, routines, documents, and social interaction.
8. Preserve slow-burn mystery pacing and maintain ambiguity.
9. Do not prematurely reveal why the Constable office is interested in Leon, whether connected to Vale's belongings, or hidden institutional motives.
10. No Observer, Seeker, Class, Tier, or supernatural power progression unless already established by canon.

## 5. Long-Term Roadmap

**Future arcs must emerge naturally:**
```
CAUSE → CONSEQUENCE → INVESTIGATION → DISCOVERY → DECISION → NEW CONSEQUENCE
```

**Avoid:** `NEW ARC → NEW SYSTEM → NEW EXPLANATION → NEW ARC`.

**Mystery principle:** Establish anomaly → let Leon notice it → allow an initial interpretation → introduce contradiction → escalate → reveal partial truth → create a larger question.

**Source:** `15_ARC_ROADMAP.md`

---

## 6. Structural Constraints

- **No RPG-style levels.** Power progression is through knowledge, experience, observation, preparation, equipment, combination, understanding, control.
- **No arbitrary numerical power ranking.**
- **Do NOT assign fixed chapter numbers to Arc 2–10.** Each future arc may contain 5, 10, 20, 50, or more chapters depending on what the story requires.
- **Do NOT compress an arc to reach the next planned arc.**
- **Do NOT extend an arc merely to hit a word/chapter target.**
- **Do NOT retroactively force later explanations into Arc 1.**
