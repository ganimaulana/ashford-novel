**Source of truth:** The manuscript (Chapters 1–137). Where World Bible differs from manuscript, the manuscript wins.

## Narrative Frame

- **Narrator:** Leon Ashcroft, first person, retrospective notebook/journal prose.
- **Setting:** Ashford, 1816–1820+. Gothic English market town; Bellweather Lane; St. Bartholomew's; the common; the mill; the boundary wall; Bellweather House.
- **Core conflict:** A keeper's duty to guard the "place beneath" Ashford vs. forces (Order, stranger, watchers, the count itself) that would open or end the keeping.
