## 2. KEEPER → HOLDER: AUTHORIAL DECISION DOCUMENTATION

### 2.1 The Conflict

The manuscript uses "keeper" (1409 occurrences) in a deeply embedded semantic cluster:

| Usage | Count | Function | Migratable? |
|-------|-------|----------|-------------|
| "the keeping" (the act/state) | 644 | Central thematic creed: "the keeping is the thing, the keeper is not" | NO — "holding" loses the guardianship meaning |
| "first keeper" (historical title) | 173 | Refers to the specific historical figure who set the boundary and carved the first sign | NO — established title |
| "keeper's sign" (Sign name) | 141 | One of the three locked supernatural Signs (first sign, keeper's sign, Order's sign) | NO — this is a named Sign, not a role |
| "keeper who" / "keeper of" | 232 | Role description | PARTIAL — but role-only migration creates inconsistency with compunds |
| "keeper before" (Vale) | 75 | Historical title | NO — established title |
| "keeper's bell" | 38 | Named object | NO — compound named object |
| "keeper's key" / "keeper's house" / "keeper's man" | 29 | Named objects/roles | NO — compound names |

### 2.2 Why Blanket Migration Fails

A mechanical "keeper" → "Holder" replacement would:
1. **Break the "keeper's sign"** — This is a named Sign in the manuscript's three-sign system (first sign / keeper's sign / Order's sign). Renaming to "Holder's sign" would destroy the locked symbol identity and confuse readers.
2. **Break "the keeping is the thing, and the keeper is not"** — This appears in the first keeper's book, Ferrand's notebook, and Leon's own reflections. It is the novel's central philosophical creed. "The holding is the thing" loses the meaning of active guardianship.
3. **Break "first keeper"** — A historical title for the figure who set the boundary. "First Holder" reads awkwardly.
4. **Create hybrid inconsistency** — If only the role uses migrate but compounds keep "keeper" (e.g., "the Holder" uses "the keeper's sign"), the manuscript becomes inconsistent.

### 2.3 WORLD_BIBLE Guidance

The CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md (the source of truth) explicitly states:
- "Do not automatically replace every 'Keeper' occurrence with 'Holder.'"
- "Legacy terms still present in Chapters 1–137 (Keeper, Watcher, Medallion...) must later undergo a **CONTEXTUAL TERMINOLOGY AUDIT**. Never use blind global replacement."
- "Audit every occurrence of 'keeper' — determine 'Holder' (authority) vs 'keeper' (literal keeper of records, etc.)."

### 2.4 Recommendation

The manuscript's "keeper" is the established in-world term for the role of guardian/guardian of the place beneath. Under the new canon, "Holder" maps to the authority role, but the manuscript's "keeper" is inextricably tied to the compounds "keeper's sign" (a Sign name), "the keeping" (thematic creed), and historical titles. The most responsible action is:

**Option A (Recommended):** Keep "keeper" in Chapters 1–137 as the established Arc 1 terminology. The new "Holder" terminology should be introduced in ARC 2+ when the larger supernatural hierarchy becomes narratively relevant. This preserves the Gothic atmosphere, the locked sign names, and the thematic consistency.

**Option B:** A future contextual audit where each occurrence is individually evaluated, compound names are identified as exceptions, and the role-only usages are selectively migrated. This requires reading and evaluating each of the 1409 occurrences individually.

**Decision:** REQUIRES AUTHORIAL DECISION.
