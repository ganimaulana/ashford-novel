## 2. FINAL STRUCTURE
After the novel-wide audit, the manuscript is recognized as a coherent grounded Gothic keeper story with **eight arcs**:
- ARC 1: Bellweather (Ch1–47) — foundation, atmosphere, the gear, the door, the Agreement.
- ARC 2: First Keeping (Ch48–60) — keeper's sign, passage, the stranger, the Order.
- ARC 3: The First Sign (Ch61–75) — two houses, two medallions, the bell, the chamber.
- ARC 4: The Account (Ch76–86) — forgetting vs. remembering, the account kept.
- ARC 5: The Hunt (Ch87–99) — four clocks, key, lock, Aurelius's letter.
- ARC 6: The Society (Ch100–117) — three tests, first keeper's book, the tally.
- ARC 7: The Count of Keepers (Ch118–125) — tally revealed, watcher, passing to Daniel.
- ARC 8: Beyond Ashford (Ch126–137) — marsh ring, third medallion, sea, journey.

**The LOTM-derived mythology is NOT in the manuscript and is CANON-EXCLUDED.** The old MASTER_PLAN's "power 9→8," "connection slots," "call window," and "named entity" are replaced by the grounded sign/medallion/bell/count/kceper system.
