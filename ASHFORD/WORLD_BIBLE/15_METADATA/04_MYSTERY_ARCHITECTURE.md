## 2. Mystery Threads

### Thread A — The Keeper's Duty
- **Setup:** Agreement, gear, door, boundary stones, keeper's sign.
- **Payoff:** Leon becomes keeper (Ch51); passes keeping to Daniel (Ch125); count of keepers revealed (Ch121).
- **Remaining:** What lies beneath; what the count completes.

### Thread B — Aurelius's Legacy
- **Setup:** Will (Ch1); Agreement (Ch40); lock (Ch96); letter (Ch98).
- **Payoff:** Aurelius signed the Agreement; made the lock; died 1804; first keeper before him.
- **Remaining:** His full history; what he omitted.

### Thread C — The Three Signs
- **Setup:** First sign (symbol), keeper's sign (line + strokes), Order's sign (circle + short line).
- **Payoff:** Three signs named; boundary stones carry first sign at base, Order's sign on face; keeper's sign opens passage.
- **Remaining:** Relationship to thirteen rings; full meaning of maker's sign.

### Thread D — The Count
- **Setup:** Wall signs; first keeper's book; three tallies.
- **Payoff:** Count = keepers; thirteen rings; Leon's mark already in tally.
- **Remaining:** Destination; completed count.

- Veteran → constable → intelligence → counter-intelligence → demotion → Alderwick.
- Guarded the Door ~50 years.
- Kept the Gear; left it at the mill to be found.
- Did not understand the Door, Gear, or inscription.

**Vale's legacy:**
- Private box (personal effects, journal, map, letters, button, ring).
- Teaching: "What I saw. What I was told. What I concluded. Keep them apart."
- Hidden caches (intelligence material, not supernatural).
- Eleanor Shaw held his map for seven years.

- How he obtained the Gear; why he chose Leon.
- The road where the horse was destroyed (omitted in both spoken and written account).
- The name appearing four times in journal then stopping.
