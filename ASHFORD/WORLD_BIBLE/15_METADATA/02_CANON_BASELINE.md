## 3. Canon Locks

### Supernatural System (World Bible only, not in manuscript)
- **XIII Signs:** Knight, Seer, Hunter, Scholar, Warden, Weaver, Herald, Artificer, Medium, Alchemist, Judge, Pilgrim, Oracle. Do NOT reveal all at once.
- **Nine Ranks:** Rank 9 = Marked (lowest) → Rank 1 = Transcendent (highest). Rank 0 is UNRESOLVED.
- **Format:** `[RANK TITLE] + [SIGN]` (e.g., Marked Knight).
- **Sign ≠ Rank.** Sign = identity/path; Rank = level of progression.
- Do NOT assign detailed abilities, advancement mechanics, or lore prematurely.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md`

### Gear Hard Rules
- **THE GEAR DOES NOT BECOME WARM.** No supernatural temperature response.
- Gear's only meaningful response is **mechanical**, tied to the separate underground mechanism (tremor Ch40; rotation/clicks/one-inch opening Ch49).
- Gear is never "the keeper's key," never a universal key, and does not detect signs.
- **Keeper's Seal ≠ Gear. Keeper's Door ≠ Gear mechanism.** The Keeper's Door (Ch78) has no lock and no Gear depression; it answers the Tokens.
- Gear count: ONE confirmed (Ch36/46/125); nine-Gear model is PROPOSED only.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §GEAR HARD RULES; `03_CANON_SEMANTIC_TABLE.md`

### Token Hard Rules
- A Token is the physical manifestation of a Sign.
- Tokens **warm** — at thresholds, at the first sign, on approach to a kept place; warmth grows with proximity and can lean directionally.
- **A Token does not warm for anyone but the keeper** (Ch117: cold in Ysabel Nott's palm).
- Thirteen Tokens exist; three are found by Ch141; ten remain. Enumerations on page: Ch103 (plural), Ch130 (two named), Ch131 (two named), Ch138 (three found, ten remaining).

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md`; `01_ANONYMOUS_TOKEN_NETWORK.md` §N.6

### 00:00 Marker
- 00:00 is a supernatural time marker, possibly associated with activation/access.
- Its complete meaning and mechanics remain UNRESOLVED.
- Do NOT define its complete purpose or retrofit every clock reference.
- In manuscript: recurring motif (stopped clocks, lock condition, journey horizon).

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §00:00 MARKER

### Bell / Call
- The Bell functions as a signal indicating a Call is available.
- Conceptually: 00:00 → possible activation → Call → possible connection.
- Do NOT treat every Bell event as automatically being a Call.
- The keeper's bell rang once for Leon; it is kept, not rung. Its summons remains UNKNOWN.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §CALL; `07_MYSTERY_LEDGER.md`

### The Door
- The supernatural object is **The Door** (preferred canonical name). Do NOT replace with "Threshold" as the primary name.
- The Door is kept closed; it is "not a door" but a memory.
- Exact relationship to Components, Key, 00:00, Call, and The Sanctuary remains to be discovered gradually.
- The place beneath is beneath the chamber, not behind the door.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §THE DOOR; `06_CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md`

### The Sanctuary
- The former concept of "Room / Connection Space" is now **The Sanctuary** — a supernatural space in which multiple supernatural connections may occur.
- Do NOT define yet: complete architecture, exact number of Anchors, permanent occupants, complete rules, origin, or ultimate purpose.
- Not present in manuscript; correct for Arc 1.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §THE SANCTUARY

### Constable ≠ Order of Severence
- Established in World Bible. Not yet established in manuscript (Leon never explicitly separates them, but they function independently).
- Crowe is Order-insider/participant; denies formal membership label on the page (Ch42).
- Order infiltration inside Constable: NOT ESTABLISHED.

**Source:** `11_PRE_ARC2_FOUNDATION_AUDIT.md` §8; `19_CANON_LOG.md`

### Keeper ≠ Holder
- Keeper is an in-world/historical/temporal term (guardian, custodian, tradition).
- Holder is the formal supernatural-system term (authority over a Sign/Token/function).
- The manuscript's "keeper" NEVER means a formal supernatural "Holder." The Keeper concept is structurally anti-Holder.
- **Zero manuscript changes required** for Keeper→Holder migration.

**Source:** `02_CANON_TERMINOLOGY.md` §KEEPER/HOLDER TERMINOLOGY

## 5. Semantic Definitions

### ERC
- **E.R.C. = ESOTERIC RECORD CLASSIFICATION.** Archival identifier/framework for documented esoteric subjects.
- ERC identifies **records**, not subjects. A Gear is NOT automatically an ERC; the RECORD about a Gear may carry an ERC designation.
- Subject types: OBJECT, PHENOMENON, LOCATION, ENTITY, EVENT, DOCUMENT, RITUAL, MECHANISM, UNKNOWN.
- ERC is NOT a power system, NOT an artifact category, NOT a supernatural object.
- ERC numbering is archival, NOT power-based. ERC-100 is not automatically stronger than ERC-010.
- Anti-SCP: No containment classes, Foundation-style organization, sterile laboratory language. ERC should feel historical, esoteric, archival, fragmented, mysterious.

### Artifact Taxonomy
```
ARTIFACT
├── GEAR (specialized Artifact associated with the Door)
├── TOKEN (distinct supernatural Artifact/system element)
└── OTHER SUPERNATURAL ARTIFACTS
```

### Gear
- Specialized Artifact associated with the Door and the ancient organization mystery.
- NOT ERC; NOT Token.
- ONE confirmed; nine PROPOSED.
- Does NOT warm; mechanical response only.

### Token
- Distinct supernatural Artifact/system element (access/connection/organizational function).
- NOT ERC; NOT Gear.
- Warmth is keeper-specific.

### Sign
- Supernatural system element (XIII Signs).
- NOT ERC; NOT Artifact unless canon explicitly requires.
- Manuscript has three signs: first sign (maker's), keeper's sign, Order's sign.

### Rank
- Supernatural progression hierarchy (Rank 9 = Marked … Rank 1 = Transcendent; Rank 0 does not exist).
- NOT ERC; NOT artifact numbering.

### Door
- Supernatural threshold/mechanism. NOT ERC; NOT Artifact.

### Sanctuary
- Supernatural space/location associated with connections. NOT ERC; NOT Gear.

### Arc / ARC
- Narrative story structure (Arc 1, Arc 2…) — OR legacy anomalous-record terminology in historical development documents, depending on context. NOT automatically ERC; narrative Arc never migrates.
