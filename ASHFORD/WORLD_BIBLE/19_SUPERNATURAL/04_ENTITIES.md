## OBSERVED PHENOMENA (Ch 1–47) — WHAT EXISTS / HAPPENS

### The Bellweather Cycle (Ch 1–8, LOCKED)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] A disturbance recurs annually at Bellweather House from **13–21 November**, then ceases (recorded by Calloway's predecessor). SOURCE: Ch 8.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The house "keeps what was done in it, and for a few nights each year it lets the keeping show" (Calloway). SOURCE: Ch 8.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The boy in the dream: "Don't be late again" — begins on the 13th. SOURCE: Ch 1, Ch 8.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] No. 13 phenomena: bell tolls once at night (Helen: "hasn't rung in a long time"), footsteps overhead, latch tried, prints vanish by morning, bolt drawn back by no visible hand, silhouette at the window. SOURCE: Ch 1–8.

### The Lamp and the Sound (Ch 9–35)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The lamp burns at nine each evening in the upper window of the end house, then is "carried away along the rooms." SOURCE: Ch 9, 10, 12, 14, 16.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The bell/sound comes when the lamp does not — alternation observed across multiple nights; rule held most nights, broke on some. SOURCE: Ch 16, 18, 24.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The sound travels along the ground, by the lane's old drain line, from the house toward the corner — "out, not in." SOURCE: Ch 16.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] First full-silence night: 28 Nov — no lamp at nine; sound twice at the corner; no light and no sound in the lane. SOURCE: Ch 12.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The sound came at one on the lamp's night (14 Dec) after the gate opened at eleven — the alternation broke; "the bell and the lamp are not two hands but one hand, and the hand was at the gate first." SOURCE: Ch 24.
- [EVIDENCE: STRONGLY IMPLIED] The lamp and the bell may be controlled by one agency ("one hand") or by two that do not know each other. UNRESOLVED. SOURCE: Ch 16, 24.

### The Mill-Yard Gate (Ch 14, 18–24)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The mill-yard gate found unlatched with oiled hinges (Nov 30 round); later found open at 11 on the Saturday round (14 Dec) with padlock unhung. SOURCE: Ch 14, 24.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Four tellings of the gate: the round shut it; the yard man will say it shut itself; the constable will say the wind; the bench will not say. SOURCE: Ch 24.
- [EVIDENCE: UNKNOWN] Whether the hand came or went, and who oils the gate. SOURCE: Ch 14, 24.

### The Greyhound's Locked Room (Ch 36)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The locked room at the Greyhound changes when left unattended: chair moves, basin level changes, bedcover disturbed, ashes warm. The changes do not occur while Leon watches. A traveller died in the room long ago. SOURCE: Ch 36.
- [EVIDENCE: UNKNOWN] Cause and mechanism. Preserved as Anomaly.

### Mrs. Hart's Letters (Ch 36)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Letters arrive containing information about events before they occur (flour price rise, neighbour illness, rain). One postmark appears dated after the letter arrived. Her reply letter disappeared overnight. SOURCE: Ch 36.
- [EVIDENCE: UNKNOWN] How the letters predict events; who sends them. Preserved as Anomaly.

### The Mill Lane Clock (Ch 36)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] A large clock in an empty house on Mill Lane; three neighbours independently report it strikes thirteen at midnight. Leon observes three nights: it strikes twelve each time, never thirteen. One gear position is empty; the gear found beneath does not fit the mechanism. SOURCE: Ch 36.
- [EVIDENCE: CONTRADICTION] Reported (13 strikes) vs observed (12 strikes). Preserved as mystery.

### The Stone Door Beneath the Mill (Ch 38, 40, 46)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] A narrow stone door set into the ground, no handle/keyhole/hinge, marked with a circle crossed by a short line; a depression matches the gear's shape. The mill appears to have been built around something already there. SOURCE: Ch 38.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] When the gear is placed in the depression and turned, the door moves (opens ~one inch); darkness and a passage appear; cold air and a low movement from beyond. Leon closes it. SOURCE: Ch 46.

### The Reappearing House on the Common (Ch 39, 45)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Appears where the common meets the lower pasture. Witnesses disagree on colour: white (farmer), grey (woman), dark brick (third person). When gone, only flattened grass remains — no foundation. SOURCE: Ch 39.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Interior (later appearance): bare hallway, single clock stopped at **11:17**, three knocks from beneath the floor. Cellar hatch beneath the front mat (not opened). SOURCE: Ch 45.
- [EVIDENCE: CONTRADICTION] Colour of the house differs by witness — preserved as mystery, not resolved.
- [EVIDENCE: UNKNOWN] The nature of the house, the meaning of 11:17, the source of the knocking. SOURCE: Ch 45.

### The Boundary Stones (Ch 37, 38, 43, 45)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The old boundary is marked by stones; four counted by Leon along the old line. The eastern marker was displaced ~1 foot; ground recently disturbed. SOURCE: Ch 37, 43.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The stones crack in May (Ch 45): first stone (eastern marker) cracks with a too-straight line; second cracks two days later; third later. All three produce gear-warming response. SOURCE: Ch 45.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Stones observed "returning" to positions on an older map (Marlow). SOURCE: Ch 38.
- [EVIDENCE: IMPLIED] The stone that seemed to move beneath Leon's hand (vibration) — unconfirmed; may be pulse/cold/cart. SOURCE: Ch 37.

### The Gear (Ch 36–47)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Small dark circular metal object found beneath the Mill Lane clock. Warm when held, cold when set down. Warms near fractured boundary stones. Fits the stone door depression; turns the mechanism. Effect reproducible. SOURCE: Ch 36, 37, 38, 40, 45, 46.
- [EVIDENCE: CONTRADICTION] Gear warmth not always reproducible; Vale tests with a stone fragment — nothing happens. SOURCE: Ch 37, 40.
