## 2. AFFILIATION — CURRENT CANON

**She is not a member of the Order of Severence.**

- She **knows** the Order, moves in its rooms, and is treated by it as its archivist.
- She has **never** claimed membership on the page, and has twice distanced herself from it.
- Her cover — being taken for the Order's archivist — is the thing that gives her access. Removing the *membership* does not remove the *access*, and must not.
- Vale is the only man in Alderwick who ever noticed the difference (Ch61). **This is the seed of everything in §7.**

**What she is instead (CONFIRMED by function, PROPOSED as formal designation):**
An independent keeper of records who holds a position no organization controls. Her loyalty is to the *record*, not to a body. This is why the Order can use her and why Vale could use her against them, in the same seven years, without either knowing.

**Constable relationship:** none. She has never been a Constable officer, clerk, or retainer, and must not be made one. She is not a bridge between the Constable and the Order (see `../../08_UNDERGROUND/01_ANONYMOUS_TOKEN_NETWORK.md` §L).

## 6. ABILITY — BOUNDED (PROPOSED, OPTIONAL, AUTHOR-GATED)

Phase 3H D7 remains in force: **no Power/Power-User content is canon.** The following is a *proposal* and may be declined without cost — every story function she has works without it.

If an ability is ever granted, it is bounded as follows and no further:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Nature | **Recognition of a hand.** She can tell, by looking, whether two documents were written by the same hand — across decades, across deliberate disguise, across a copyist's imitation. Nothing more. |
| Trigger | Direct sight of both documents, unhurried, in good light. Cannot work from memory of one. |
| Effect | A binary judgement: same hand / not the same hand / cannot say. **"Cannot say" must be a frequent outcome.** |
| Range | Physical documents in front of her |
| Duration | Instantaneous; not repeatable on the same pair for a better answer |
| Limitation | Gives **no** name, no date, no place, no content, no intent. Cannot read what is not written. Cannot detect that a document is false — only that it is or is not in the hand it claims. |
| Cost | Not physical. **Institutional.** Her judgement is worth nothing to any authority: she cannot show her working, so every use spends credibility she cannot replace. |
| Failure condition | Fails on short samples, on copperplate, on any hand deliberately taught to a standard, and on anything she wants the answer to. |
| Detectability | Nothing to observe. She looks at paper for a long time. |
| Knowledge required | Twenty years of ledgers. She does not know it is unusual and would be insulted by the suggestion. |
| Possible misuse | She can be fed a forged comparison and reach a true judgement about a false pair |
| E.R.C. interaction | Her judgement can never rise above **OBSERVED** standing (§ `03_ESOTERIC_RECORD_CLASSIFICATION.md`), because there is no second witness for an act of recognition. This is the point. |

**Why this and not memory:** it is the smallest possible ability that serves the manuscript's actual obsession (hands — Ch111 identifies the first keeper *by hand*; Ch102 finds the marginal hand; Ch121 matches three tallies by hand). It cannot solve a mystery on its own, it cannot be weaponised, and it cannot be corroborated.

**Mundane fallback if declined:** she is simply the best-read records hand in Alderwick and reaches the same judgements more slowly, with more errors, and with an explicit margin of doubt. Nothing in her arc requires the ability.

## 7. SECRET CONTACT / TOKEN RELATIONSHIP — SUMMARY

**Status: PROPOSED — full architecture in `../../08_UNDERGROUND/01_ANONYMOUS_TOKEN_NETWORK.md` §N.**

Canon direction (author-approved 2026-09-03): Eleanor Shaw is designated as one of Leon's **secret contacts**, reachable by way of a **Token**, in an arrangement concealed from the ordinary Constable hierarchy *and* from the Order.

Summary of the locked constraints (the reasoning is in the Token file):
- Trust is **earned across the future arc**, from the Ch62/Ch80 low point. There is no instant loyalty.
- The Token is **not a communication device**. It carries no words. It authenticates, and its *movement between two agreed places* is the entire message.
- The Token will be **cold in her hand and will stay cold** — Ch117 established that a Token does not warm for anyone but the keeper. She holds something she is told is alive and experiences dead metal. That asymmetry is the price of the arrangement, not a flaw in it.
- The arrangement is **mutually incriminating**, which is what makes it hold: he has given away part of the keeping; she has accepted a thing from the keeper while the Order believes she is theirs. Neither can report the other without ending themselves.
- **Timing is DEFERRED.** Ch103 / Ch130 / Ch131 / Ch138 enumerate exactly which Tokens Leon holds and carries; no Token can be permanently given away inside that window without contradiction. See the Token file for the three candidate resolutions.
