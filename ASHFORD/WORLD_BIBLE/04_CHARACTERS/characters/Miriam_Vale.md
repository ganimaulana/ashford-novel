## Power: Cellular Echo
Can sense the biological history and condition of tissue through touch.

Can detect:
- Previous injuries
- Biological damage
- Approximate tissue age
- Whether tissue previously possessed Power
- Biological changes before death

Cannot read thoughts or memories.

## Background
Pathologist and early collaborator of Elias Mercer. She helped establish that Power leaves biological traces.
