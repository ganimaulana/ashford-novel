## Canon Facts (from Ch 36–47, now OFFICIAL living canon per Phase 3H D5)

- Former constable of Ashford, before Calloway (Ch 40: "I was a constable. ... Before Calloway.").
- Lives in a house at the end of Bellweather Lane.
- Holds the Agreement document signed by Aurelius Ashcroft (Ch 40: shows Leon a folded packet; Ch 47: Leon has the document).
- Keeper of the Agreement for fifty years (Ch 46: "I kept the agreement for fifty years.").
- Possessed a gear object in a wooden box; in Ch 46 he closes the box and says to Leon "You already have one," declining to hand over a second gear.
- Knows of the stones, the door beneath the mill, the object (gear), and the boundary.
- Dies before dawn at the end of Ch 47; Leon becomes Keeper upon his death.
