## ARC 2+ PROPOSAL — FIRST ANONYMOUS TOKEN RECIPIENT

**Status:** PROPOSED — PRIMARY CANDIDATE FOR FIRST ANONYMOUS TOKEN RECIPIENT (Arc 2 or later). NOT CONFIRMED. NOT implemented in manuscript.

### Character Function (Proposed)
Eleanor Shaw is the leading PROPOSED candidate to become the first anonymous recipient of a supernatural Token from Leon Ashcroft during Arc 2 or later.

She is NOT an experienced occultist, supernatural expert, or equivalent of Leon. She is an intelligent and curious person whose interest in local history, old manuscripts, folklore, antiquities, unexplained events, old records, strange inscriptions, historical correspondence, and unresolved local mysteries gradually brings her close to something genuinely supernatural.

Her attraction to mystery comes from CURIOSITY, not supernatural competence.

### Leon / Eleanor Distinction (Proposed)
| | Leon | Eleanor |
|---|------|---------|
| Type | Investigator, pattern-oriented, cautious | Curious researcher, historically minded |
| Exposure | Already exposed to supernatural phenomena | Less knowledgeable about supernatural reality |
| Danger awareness | Understands danger better | Capable of making incorrect assumptions |
| Action | Acts deliberately, conceals information | Does not initially understand what the Token represents |
| Agency | Independent | Genuine agency and independent motivations |

Eleanor must NOT become: "female Leon," an instant occult expert, an instant supernatural scholar, a flawless investigator, a passive recipient, or a direct copy of a LOTM-style character. The conceptual niche ("curious outsider drawn toward a hidden supernatural world") may be similar, but the character, psychology, history, motivations, relationships, and development must remain distinctly Ashford.

### Canon Tension — Prior Acquaintance (OPEN QUESTION)
**Existing canon establishes that Eleanor Shaw and Leon met face-to-face (Ch 57), when Leon sought the map and the keeper's sign.** Therefore the "anonymous recipient" framing requires careful handling:

- Leon may contact her without revealing his identity despite prior acquaintance, OR
- Leon may use an alias/pseudonym, OR
- The "anonymity" may mean the Token and letter bear no name or face even though Leon knows her, OR
- The proposal may require a separate authorial decision on how anonymity is maintained.

This is an OPEN design question requiring author approval. Do NOT silently resolve it.

### Knowledge Boundary (Proposed)
**Eleanor knows (records level, Ch 57 canon):**
- The keeper's sign exists and is older than the Order's circle
- Vale found a different sign (the nineteenth)
- A map older than the vestry survey existed
- The Order knows Leon has the gear
- People have died protecting things they could not explain

**Eleanor does NOT know (Proposed):**
- The complete supernatural system
- The XIII Symbols
- The full meaning of the Token
- The existence/structure of any anonymous network
- The sender's ultimate objective (if anonymous correspondence begins)

### Token Relationship (Proposed)
- Eleanor is the leading candidate to receive a Token anonymously.
- The Token must have a narrative function: a decision, an investigation, a discovery, a test, access to something, or a meaningful consequence.
- It must NOT become an item she carries indefinitely without purpose.
- Exact Token behavior remains PROPOSED until supported by the supernatural system canon.
