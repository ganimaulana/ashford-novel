## Story Layers

### Ordinary
Characters who are not aware of the Hidden Circle or the deeper mysteries. They may have some knowledge but are not part of the secret world.

### Interesting
Characters who have knowledge of or are connected to the hidden history, but may not be full members of the Circle.

### Hidden World
Characters who are deeply involved in the mystery, either as keepers of secrets or as part of the Circle.

---

## Knowledge Risk

**High:** Character knows significant secrets and is likely to withhold or reveal them at key moments.

**Moderate:** Character has some information but not the full picture.

**Low:** Character is not a source of deep secrets.
