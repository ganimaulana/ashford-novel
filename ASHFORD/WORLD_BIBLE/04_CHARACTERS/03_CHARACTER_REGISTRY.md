## Story Layers

### Ordinary
Characters who are not aware of the deeper mysteries. They may have some knowledge but are not part of the hidden world.

### Interesting
Characters who have knowledge of or are connected to the hidden history, but are not full participants.

### Hidden World
Characters deeply involved in the mystery, either as keepers of secrets or as participants in it.

**Note (2026-09-03):** the "Hidden Circle" organizational concept is SUPERSEDED. Leon's affiliation is CONSTABLE; see `../08_UNDERGROUND/09_ORGANIZATIONAL_AFFILIATION_LEDGER.md`.

---

## Knowledge Risk

**High:** Character knows significant secrets and is likely to withhold or reveal them at key moments.

**Moderate:** Character has some information but not the full picture.

**Low:** Character is not a source of deep secrets.
