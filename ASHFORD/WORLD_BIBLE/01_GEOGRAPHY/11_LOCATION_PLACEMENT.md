## LOCATION PLACEMENTS

### Arthur\'s Shop

**File:** `BUILDINGS/02_ARTHURS_SHOP.md`

**Geographic Placement:**

- **District:** Bellweather Lane Area (edge of Old Town)
- **Elevation Zone:** Low-Medium (30-45m approximate)
- **Street:** Bellweather Lane
- **Character:** Transitional area between Old Town commercial dan quieter areas

**Geographic Context:**

- **Position:** Off main High Street, narrow historic lane
- **Surroundings:** Mix of building ages, some pre-Victorian
- **Access:** Walking distance dari Market Square, Station
- **Topography:** Slight rise dari valley floor, gradual slope

**Atmospheric Characteristics:**

- **Fog behavior:** Moderate - kabut drifts in dari valley but not as dense as riverside
- **Light:** Shadowed by taller buildings in places
- **Sound:** Quieter than High Street, footsteps echo in lane
- **Wind:** Sheltered by buildings, occasional drafts through lane
- **Temperature:** Cooler than exposed areas, damp retained

**Visibility:**

- From Market Square: 5-10 min walk
- From Station: 10-15 min walk
- From Riverside: 5 min walk uphill
- From Heights: Down the hill into town

**Underground Network Context:**

- Shop location good for discreet meetings
- Not too visible dari main thoroughfares
- Easy access dari multiple areas
- Historic character supports antiques/curios cover
- Atmospheric lane supports mystery tone

### Riverside Walk/Areas

**Status:** Referenced, area rather than single location

**Geographic Placement:**

- **District:** Riverside (linear district along Great Stour)
- **Elevation Zone:** Very Low (15-25m approximate)
- **Position:** Along river course through town
- **Character:** Mix of recreational, historical, industrial

**Geographic Context:**

- **Position:** Lowest elevation areas in town
- **Surroundings:** Water, old mills, warehouses, some conversions, parks
- **Access:** Footpaths, some road access, bridges connect across
- **Topography:** Flat floodplain, immediately adjacent to water

**Atmospheric Characteristics:**

- **Fog behavior:** VERY THICK - coldest air, water vapor, sheltered valley
- **Light:** Limited streetlighting on paths, dark areas between lights
- **Sound:** Water flowing, sometimes birds, footsteps on towpath
- **Wind:** Usually calm (sheltered), but occasionally follows river
- **Temperature:** Coldest areas of town at night, damp always

**Key Features:**

- Riverside footpaths
- Old bridges (stone, iron)
- Mills (converted or derelict)
- Warehouses (some converted)
- Weirs
- Parks near water
- Benches, lighting (intermittent)

**Paranormal Potential:**

- Water phenomena (reflections, voices)
- Mills (industrial history, machinery)
- Bridges (crossing points, liminal)
- Fog (visibility, disorientation)

## FOG VISIBILITY MAP

**When valley fog is thick (common autumn/winter nights):**

- **Cannot see:** Riverside completely obscured, Industrial Quarter lost in fog
- **Barely see:** Old Town streets visibility 10-20m, Station platforms unclear
- **Can see somewhat:** Bellweather Lane, Market Square edges, 30-50m visibility
- **Reasonably clear:** Victorian residential areas, 100m+ visibility
- **Clear:** Heights completely clear, can see fog below like blanket
- **Very clear:** Downs, panoramic views, fog in valley visible as layer

**This creates dramatic visual contrasts.**

## INTEGRATION NOTES

### For New Locations

When creating new location files, include:

1. **District:** Which district/neighborhood
2. **Elevation Zone:** Approximate elevation category
3. **Geographic Context:** Surroundings, topography
4. **Atmospheric Characteristics:** Fog, light, sound, wind, temperature
5. **Access:** How to get there, walking times
6. **Visibility:** What can be seen dari there

### For Story Writing

When Leon moves between locations, consider:

1. **Elevation change:** Going up/down affects atmosphere
2. **Fog behavior:** Changes dramatically dengan elevation
3. **Temperature:** Noticeable differences
4. **Sound:** Carries differently in valley vs heights
5. **Time to travel:** Realistic journey times
6. **Views:** What can be seen dari different elevations
