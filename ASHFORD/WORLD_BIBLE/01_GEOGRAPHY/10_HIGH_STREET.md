## Karakteristik

**Atmosfer:** [BELUM DITETAPKAN]  
**Lebar jalan:** [BELUM DITETAPKAN]

### Detail Arsitektur

**Periode Dominan:**
- **Victorian commercial** - shopfronts dan commercial buildings
- **Georgian base** - bangunan lebih tua dengan Victorian/modern shopfronts
- **Edwardian additions** - beberapa bangunan awal 1900s
- **20th century modernizations** - fascia boards, modern signage

**Karakteristik Visual:**
- **Ground floor:** Modern/Victorian shopfronts (large windows, fascia boards, signage)
- **Upper floors:** Georgian/Victorian residential atau offices
- **Mixed heights** - 2-4 lantai
- **Continuous frontage** - bangunan berdampingan tanpa gap
- **Variety** - setiap bangunan berbeda tetapi cohesive

**Shopfronts:**
- **Victorian:** Large display windows, pilasters, cornices
- **Modern:** Aluminium frames, modern fascias
- **Mix:** Original Georgian/Victorian upper floors dengan modern ground floor
- **Signage:** Bervariasi dari traditional painted signs hingga modern backlit

**Kondisi:**
- **Well-maintained commercial** - toko aktif, terawat
- **Some vacant units** - boarded up atau neglected
- **Upper floors often neglected** - fokus pada ground floor commercial

**Prinsip:** High Street adalah palimpsest arsitektur - bangunan dari berbagai periode dengan modifikasi commercial. Ground floor berubah mengikuti zaman, upper floors mempertahankan karakter original.
