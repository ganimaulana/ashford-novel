## Distribusi Arsitektur

**Prinsip distribusi berdasarkan jarak dari center:**

**Inner residential (dekat center):**
- **Victorian terraces** - workers' housing, kelas menengah
- **Late Victorian/Edwardian** - expansion dari center
- **Continuous terraces** - berderet tanpa gap
- **Close to street** - no front gardens

**Outer residential (suburban):**
- **Edwardian semi-detached** - pairs of houses
- **1920s-1930s housing** - inter-war development
- **1960s-1980s estates** - post-war expansion
- **Modern infill** - 1990s-2000s
- **Front gardens** - setback dari jalan
- **More spacious** - larger plots

### Kondisi (1816)
- **Bervariasi widely** - dari immaculate hingga neglected
- **Renovations common** - PVC windows, extensions, loft conversions
- **Some original features** - pada houses yang well-maintained
- **Some disrepair** - especially older Victorian terraces

**Prinsip:** Residential areas menunjukkan pertumbuhan Alderwick dari Victorian center ke Edwardian suburbs ke 20th century expansion. Arsitektur berubah dengan jarak dari center.

## Potensi Lokasi Paranormal

**[POTENSI LOKASI PARANORMAL - Residential Areas]:**

Residential areas memiliki domestic history - families, births, deaths, making them naturally suited untuk certain paranormal activity.

**Karakteristik yang cocok:**
- **Old Victorian/Edwardian houses** - long histories, many occupants
- **Attics** - storage, forgotten items, sealed rooms
- **Basements** - cellars, coal storage, dark
- **Staircases** - transition spaces
- **Back gardens** - private, enclosed
- **Alleyways** - between terraces, dark
- **Empty houses** - for sale, between occupants
- **Houses with tragic histories** - deaths, family tragedies

**Residential-specific paranormal:**
- **Former residents** - still "living" in their homes
- **Family ghosts** - domestic scenes replaying
- **Children** - playful or tragic presences
- **Domestic sounds** - footsteps upstairs, doors, cooking smells
- **Window figures** - faces in windows
- **Garden encounters** - figures in gardens at dusk

**Timing:**
- **Late night** - Leon walking home, street quiet
- **Dusk** - between day and night, shadows
- **Empty houses** - Leon entering for some reason
- **Normal day** - sees something nobody else does

**Domestic vs paranormal:**
- Normal residential life continues
- Paranormal hidden among domestic normality
- Leon's own home potentially affected
- Neighbors might have experiences too

**PENTING:** Residential paranormal berbeda dari industrial - more intimate, more personal, domestic horror.
