## Karakteristik

**Arsitektur:** Mixed - depends on university age [BELUM DITETAPKAN]

### Detail Arsitektur

**Periode (depends on university age):**
- **Victorian conversions** - jika university mengambil alih bangunan lama
- **1960s-1970s campus buildings** - jika university expansion era ini
- **Modern additions** - 1990s-2000s new buildings
- **Victorian residential** - area sekitar campus

**Kemungkinan Scenarios:**

**Scenario A: Victorian buildings converted**
- **Former Victorian schools/institutions** - converted to university use
- **Victorian Gothic** - untuk older academic buildings
- **Large Victorian houses** - converted to departments/offices
- **Bata merah dengan stone dressings**

**Scenario B: Modern university campus**
- **1960s brutalist/modernist** - concrete, flat roofs, large windows
- **Functional design** - purpose-built lecture halls, labs
- **Contrast dengan surroundings** - modern vs Victorian residential

**Scenario C: Mixed (most likely)**
- **Victorian core** - oldest buildings
- **20th century expansions** - newer buildings added
- **Variety of architectural styles** - accumulated over time

**Student Accommodation:**
- **Converted Victorian houses** - HMOs (Houses in Multiple Occupation)
- **Purpose-built student halls** - 1960s-2000s
- **Victorian terraces subdivided** - bedsits, flats

**Area Around Campus:**
- **Victorian residential** - terraces, semi-detached
- **Edwardian houses** - larger family homes converted to student use
- **Mixed condition** - beberapa well-maintained, beberapa shabby
- **Student-focused commercial** - cafes, bars, takeaways

**Prinsip:** University District adalah campuran Victorian residential yang diconvert untuk student use, dengan possible purpose-built campus buildings. Arsitektur tergantung pada kapan university didirikan/berkembang.
