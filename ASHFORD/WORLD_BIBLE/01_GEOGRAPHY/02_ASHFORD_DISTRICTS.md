## GENERAL CONCEPT

Alderwick is a **small English market town** — not a city, not a village.
Population estimate: 3,000–5,000.

It exists somewhere in the English countryside. Its exact county is unimportant; what matters is its CHARACTER.

> "Alderwick is a town that exists. Nothing more, nothing less."

But beneath the surface, it carries layers of history that most residents never know about.

## DISTRICT 1: MARKET SQUARE (Town Center)

**Character:** The heartbeat of Alderwick. Where everyone converges.

**Key Locations:**
- **Market Square** — open space where the weekly market is held; normally a gathering place
- **Market Hall** — covered structure where stalls set up; also serves as town meeting space
- **Bell & Horse Inn** (Margaret's establishment) — central inn; travelers, diligence stop nearby, social hub
- **Alderwick Post Office** — where mail arrives with the diligence; letters collected here
- **Church** (St. Bartholomew's) — the dominant building; visible from every angle
- **Constable's watch post** — informal; no dedicated building
- **Coffee House / Assembly Room** — where gentry and professionals meet

**Atmosphere:** Bustling during market days; quiet on Sundays; gossip hub year-round.

---

## DISTRICT 2: HIGH STREET (Commercial Main Road)

**Character:** The main thoroughfare connecting Market Square to the edges of town.

**Key Locations:**
- **Arthur's Antiquarian Shop** — small shop; sells old books, curios, local artifacts
- **Bell's Bookshop** — new books, academic texts, some secondhand
- **Various shops** — butchers, bakers, grocers, draper, apothecary
- **Diligence stop** — where coaches arrive/depart; adjacent to Bell & Horse Inn
- **Row of older houses** — transitioning from commercial to residential

**Atmosphere:** Busy during the day; shops open early; foot traffic throughout.

---

## DISTRICT 3: OLD TOWN (Residential, Near Church)

**Character:** The oldest part of the town; narrow streets, old buildings, close to the church.

**Key Locations:**
- **St. Bartholomew's Church** — main church; graveyard; parish records
- **Vicarage** — vicar's residence, usually adjacent to church
- **Older family homes** — some in disrepair, some well-maintained
- **Alderwick Library** — subscription or charity-supported; small but respected
- **Solicitor's office** — handles local legal affairs

**Atmosphere:** Quiet, older, more formal; residents are long-established families.

---

## DISTRICT 4: ACADEMY QUARTER

**Character:** Academic and intellectual center.

**Key Locations:**
- **Alderwick Academy** — private school; where Leon studies
- **Teacher residences** — nearby houses for staff
- **Student lodging** — rooms rented to boarding students
- **Academic bookshop** — near or adjacent to academy
- **Study spaces** — gardens, reading rooms

**Atmosphere:** Younger population; lively during term; quiet during holidays.

---

## DISTRICT 5: BELLWEATHER LANE (Historic Residential)

**Character:** A quiet, older residential street. Not fashionable, not dangerous. Just... old.

**Key Locations:**
- **No. 13 — Leon's residence** — modest; inherited; slightly neglected
- **Bellweather House** — larger property nearby; older; possibly abandoned or modified
- **Few other houses** — not many numbers; this is a small lane
- **Gardens / small yards** — behind each house

**Atmosphere:** Still, slightly isolated. People walk past but don't linger. The houses feel older than the rest of town.

**Story Significance:** This is where the mystery begins. This is where Leon discovers that the house's history is not what it seems.

---

## DISTRICT 6: NORTH Alderwick (Rural Edge)

**Character:** Open countryside, estate lands, wooded areas. The edge of town gives way to fields and woodland.

**Key Locations (Future):**
- **Alderwick Manor** — estate of the local gentry
- **Old chapel or ruin** — possibly connected to Ashcroft family history
- **Woodland / forest** — walking, isolation, secrets
- **Cemetery / burial ground** — if not adjacent to church
- **Abandoned buildings** — old mills, disused structures
- **Hidden locations** — suitable for secret meetings, ritual sites

**Atmosphere:** Remote, quiet, atmospheric. Fog is common. This is where things get mysterious.

---

## DISTRICT 7: INDUSTRIAL / WORKSHOP AREA

**Character:** Working-class area; where tradespeople operate.

**Key Locations:**
- **Blacksmith** — essential to any town
- **Cartwright / wheelwright** — repair and build horse-drawn vehicles
- **Miller** — if near a river
- **Cooper / tanner** — craft trades
- **Laborer housing** — smaller, simpler dwellings

**Atmosphere:** Noisy, smoky, practical. Not a place Leon frequents unless necessary.

---

## TRANSITIONS & MOVEMENT

**From Bellweather Lane to Market Square:** 10-15 minute walk through older streets.

**From Academy to High Street:** 5-minute walk.

**From Market Square to Church:** visible from most of the Square; 2-minute walk.

**From Alderwick edge to North countryside:** immediate; the town ends and fields begin.
