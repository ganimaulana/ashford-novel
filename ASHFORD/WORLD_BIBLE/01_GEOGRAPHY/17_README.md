## Key Geographic Concepts

### Elevation Layers

```
NORTH DOWNS (150-180m) - Often clear, windswept
        ↓
Alderwick HEIGHTS (80-120m) - Above fog frequently
        ↓
RISING GROUND (40-70m) - Victorian residential, variable fog
        ↓
OLD TOWN CENTER (25-35m) - Valley floor, moderate fog
        ↓
RIVER VALLEY (15-25m) - Thickest fog, dampest, coldest
```

**This creates natural atmospheric variation.**

---

### Fog Distribution

Kabut TIDAK uniform - berbeda berdasarkan topografi:

- **THICKEST:** Riverside, Old Industrial Quarter
- **HEAVY:** Old Town center, Market Square, Station area
- **MODERATE:** Bellweather Lane, transitional zones
- **VARIABLE:** Victorian residential areas, South Alderwick
- **OFTEN CLEAR:** Alderwick Heights, woodland edges
- **USUALLY CLEAR:** North Downs ridge

**Elevation changes = atmosphere changes.**

---

### The Four Ashfords

Each zone has distinct character:

**1. Valley Alderwick (River & Old Town)**
- Damp, foggy, enclosed
- Historic center, commerce
- Sound carries strangely
- Water presence

**2. Victorian Alderwick (Rising Ground)**
- Residential, tree-lined
- Mix of fog and clear
- Suburban character
- Views down to valley

**3. High Alderwick (Heights)**
- Isolated, panoramic
- Wind-exposed, often clear
- Old estates, woodland
- Can see fog below

**4. Beyond Alderwick (Downs, Woods, Coast)**
- Rural, dramatic
- Ancient landscape
- Different atmosphere entirely
- Gateway to wider world

## Climate Character

**Temperate Maritime - English Lowland**

- Cold & damp (not extreme)
- Frequent kabut (autumn/winter)
- Grey skies common
- Rain/drizzle frequent
- Wind variable
- Frost occasional
- Snow rare

**Novel setting:** 1816, autumn/winter - emphasizes darker, foggier atmosphere.

## Geographic Relationships

### Alderwick Connections

| Feature | Direction | Distance | Time |
|---------|-----------|----------|------|
| Greyhaven (coast) | E/SE | 30-40 km | 40 min |
| North Downs ridge | N | 5-10 km | 20 min |
| Hollow Wood | W/SW | 3-6 km | 15 min |
| Alderwick Lake | SW | 3-5 km | 10 min |
| Canterbury | NE | ~25 km | 30 min |

### Within Town

- **Walking:** Most central locations 5-15 min apart
- **Elevation change:** Noticeable when moving up/down
- **Fog variation:** Dramatic between valley and heights
- **Temperature:** Can differ 2-3°C valley to heights

## Paranormal Implications

Geography naturally supports different phenomena:

**Water-based:**
- Rivers: reflections, voices, boundaries
- Lake: submerged history, depths
- Wetlands: isolation, sounds

**Elevation-based:**
- Heights: seeing things dari above, perspective shifts
- Fog layers: visibility changes, disorientation
- Valleys: sound effects, cold air drainage

**Woodland:**
- Spatial disorientation
- Ancient sites
- Boundary crossing
- Darkness even in day

**Urban:**
- Abandoned buildings: echoes, isolation
- Old town: layered history
- Industrial: machinery, accidents
- Transit spaces: liminal, time
