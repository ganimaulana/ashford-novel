## DOCUMENT STATUS SYSTEM

Setiap dokumen dalam World Bible memiliki status level:

### 📗 CANON
**Sudah pasti terjadi dalam cerita**
- Confirmed dalam published/finalized chapters
- Tidak boleh diubah tanpa major revision
- Reader-facing information

### 📘 WORKING CANON
**Keputusan world-building yang disepakati**
- Will be treated as true unless revised
- Foundation untuk writing
- Can evolve dengan story needs

### 📙 DRAFT
**Ide yang belum disetujui**
- Proposal atau brainstorming
- Needs review before becoming Working Canon
- May change significantly

### 📕 HIDDEN CANON
**Author-only knowledge**
- True information not yet revealed to readers
- Spoilers, twists, hidden truths
- Guides writing but kept secret

## NAVIGATION GUIDE

### To Write a Scene:
1. Check location in `01_GEOGRAPHY/Location_Placement.md`
2. Check building details in `03_BUILDINGS/`
3. Check time period details in `1816/`
4. Check atmospheric conditions in `11_ATMOSPHERE/Fog.md`
5. Check social context in `05_SOCIAL/City_Activity.md`

### To Create ARC Case:
1. Read `07_ANOMALIES/ARC_System.md`
2. Check draft examples in `07_ANOMALIES/Draft/`
3. Determine category dan risk level
4. Document in appropriate subfolder
5. Update `07_ANOMALIES/ARC_Master.md`

### To Track Mystery:
1. Log clues in `09_MYSTERY/Clues.md`
2. Update `09_MYSTERY/Reveal_Structure.md`
3. Check `12_NOVEL/Mystery_Pacing.md`
4. Verify consistency in `00_MASTER/Continuity_Master.md`

### To Check Consistency:
1. Review `00_MASTER/Continuity_Master.md`
2. Check `00_MASTER/Timeline_Master.md`
3. Verify against `00_MASTER/Canon_Rules.md`
4. Update `00_MASTER/Open_Questions.md` if needed
