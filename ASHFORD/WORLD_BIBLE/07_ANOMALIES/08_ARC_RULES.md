# ARC RULES

## Status

**Universal Rules** governing ARC design and documentation.
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-31

---

## Core Principle

> **The value of an ARC is not determined by its individual effect.**

An ARC that appears useless alone may become the foundation of a powerful combination.
ARC Synergy is the **core mechanic** of the ARC system.

---

## Creation Rules

1. Every ARC must have a distinctive rule
2. Every ARC must have meaningful limitations
3. Behavior must be internally consistent
4. Must fit the Alderwick world and 1816 setting
5. Must contribute to mystery, world-building, or characterization
6. Must avoid RPG-like design
7. Must avoid direct SCP imitation
8. Must have a clear narrative purpose
9. Power level must be appropriate
10. Must support long-term foreshadowing where possible

---

## The "Trash ARC" Rules

1. Leon collects ARC others consider useless
2. "Useless" ARC become valuable through understanding, context, and synergy
3. Examples: broken compass, returning coin, cracked mirror, stopped clock, old gloves, broken pen, empty bottle, small bell
4. Leon's collection is a toolkit, not an inventory of weapons
5. Some ARC remain genuinely useless — this is intentional
6. Do not force every ARC to become powerful

---

## Leon's Progression Rules

1. Leon begins weak — survives through intelligence, observation, preparation, ARC
2. Leon's primary advantage is understanding, not power
3. Leon's growth: ARC-dependent → ARC strategist → ARC architect → Reality exploiter
4. Leon must make mistakes — misinterpret ARC, combine incorrectly, fail
5. Leon's growth is not linear — he backtracks and learns from failure
6. Other characters: Power → Strategy. Leon: Strategy → Power

---

## Aurelius Rules

1. Aurelius is a "constructed powerhouse" — powerful through combination of many small ARC
2. Other characters perceive Aurelius as individually powerful
3. The true architecture of Aurelius's ARC toolkit is concealed
4. Leon uses misdirection, false attribution, overlapping effects, timing
5. No single character knows the complete architecture unless canon establishes it
6. Aurelius Ashcroft is not a powerful man with ARC — he is a man who became powerful through ARC

---

## Compatibility Rules

1. Not all ARC can be combined
2. Some ARC cancel each other or create dangerous feedback
3. Some ARC require specific conditions to coexist
4. Leon must learn which ARC are compatible through trial and error
5. Compatibility is discovered through narrative, not announced

---

## Numbering Rules

1. Sequential numbering: `01_ARC_001`, `02_ARC_002`, ...
2. Three-digit format
3. Numbers are identifiers, not rankings
4. Permanent once assigned
5. No renumbering established ARC

---

## Interaction Rules

1. ARC may interact with other ARC
2. Synergy must be tracked in `09_ARC_SYNERGY.md`
3. Suspected synergy is NOT confirmed synergy
4. Author-only planned synergy must be clearly marked
5. Never treat speculation as canon

---

## Cost Principles

1. Not every ARC must have a cost
2. Costs should vary between ARC
3. Costs may be physical, mental, social, temporal, spiritual, or informational
4. Some ARC may appear harmless until long-term consequences become apparent

---

## Limitation Principles

1. Every significant ARC must have limitations
2. Limitations should be discoverable through narrative
3. Limitations may be conditional
4. Limitations may be misunderstood by characters
5. Some limitations may remain unknown

---

## Knowledge-Layer Rules

1. Track multiple layers of knowledge for each ARC
2. Different groups may hold contradictory explanations
3. Earlier explanations may be wrong
4. Documents may be incomplete or altered
5. The WORLD_BIBLE contains author knowledge, not reader knowledge

---

## Mystery/Reveal Rules

1. Effect before explanation
2. Observation before understanding
3. Partial rules before complete rules
4. Application before deeper implication
5. Avoid exposition dumps
6. The reader should infer rules through events

---

## Canon Rules

1. Existing Alderwick canon always takes priority
2. Do not silently rewrite existing canon
3. Identify and record conflicts
4. Propose smallest possible corrections
5. Major canon changes require approval
6. Never silently convert Proposed → Canon

---

## Negation Rules

1. Leon is NOT immune to ARC
2. Leon's advantage is understanding ARC rules
3. Negation levels: Avoidance → Interruption → Suppression → Cancellation → Reversal
4. Cancellation must be rare — if too many ARC cancel each other, the system becomes too easy
5. Reversal is the most interesting negation — make ARC work against its source
6. "Useless" ARC can counter powerful ARC through understanding
7. Counters are not always 1:1 — ARC-A + condition + ARC-B = negation
8. The question is not "Which ARC is stronger?" but "Which ARC understands the rule of the other?"

---

## Counter System Rules

1. ARC can counter other ARC
2. Direct counter: ARC-A is specifically countered by ARC-B
3. Synergistic counter: ARC-A + condition + ARC-B = negation
4. Counters can be discovered through narrative, not announced
5. Leon's trash ARC collection enables unexpected counters
6. No single character should know all counters unless canon establishes it

---

## Utility vs Power Rules

1. An ARC can have high raw power but low utility
2. An ARC can have low raw power but high synergy
3. Weak ARC + High Synergy + High Precision can be more valuable than Powerful ARC + Low Control
4. Leon's collection prioritizes synergy and precision over raw power

---

## Documentation Rules

1. Use the standard ARC file template
2. Use `Unknown` for genuinely unavailable information
3. Do not fill unknowns with speculation
4. Track canon status explicitly
5. Update ownership and location promptly
6. Cross-reference related ARC
7. Track synergy separately
8. Maintain the index when adding new ARC
9. Identify which behavior patterns apply (see `02_ARC_BEHAVIOR_PATTERNS.md`)
10. Layer multiple patterns per ARC for depth
11. Document multi-axis classification (see `04_ARC_CLASSIFICATION_SYSTEM.md`)
12. Track negation potential and counter relationships
