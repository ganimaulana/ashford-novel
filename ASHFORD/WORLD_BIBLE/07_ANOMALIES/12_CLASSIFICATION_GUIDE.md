## Category Classifications

### OBJECT
**Definition:** Benda fisik dengan sifat abnormal.

**Examples:** Keys, Coins, Clocks, Photographs, Clothing, Jewelry, Tools, Furniture

**Identification:**
- Tangible
- Can be moved
- Has physical form
- Interacts with material world

### LOCATION
**Definition:** Tempat atau area dengan fenomena abnormal.

**Examples:** Rooms, Buildings, Streets, Alleyways, Specific addresses

**Identification:**
- Fixed geographic location
- Cannot be relocated
- Phenomenon occurs within defined space

### PERSON
**Definition:** Individu dengan atau mengalami kondisi abnormal.

**IMPORTANT:** Use with extreme care and respect.

### EVENT
**Definition:** Kejadian yang berulang dengan pola.

### PHENOMENON
**Definition:** Fenomena tanpa fixed object atau location.

### ENTITY
**Definition:** Presence atau keberadaan yang tidak fully explained.

**IMPORTANT:** Use sparingly.

### DOCUMENT
**Definition:** Written, recorded, atau visual media dengan abnormal properties.

## Risk Classifications

### R0 — Residual
**Risk Level:** Minimal to none
**Characteristics:** No longer active, only traces remain

### R1 — Passive
**Risk Level:** Low
**Characteristics:** Active but benign, no direct harm

### R2 — Reactive
**Risk Level:** Moderate
**Characteristics:** Responds to triggers, predictable when triggered

### R3 — Hazardous
**Risk Level:** Significant
**Characteristics:** Direct harm potential, requires serious precautions

### R4 — Critical
**Risk Level:** Severe
**Characteristics:** Wide-area impact, multiple casualties potential
**NOTE:** R4 cases are extremely rare.

### R5 — Unknown
**Risk Level:** Undetermined
**Characteristics:** Insufficient data, unpredictable behavior
**IMPORTANT:** R5 ≠ very dangerous. R5 = we do not know yet.

### NULL
**Risk Level:** Unclassifiable
**Characteristics:** Does not fit R0-R5, contradictory behavior
**NOTE:** NULL is very rare.
