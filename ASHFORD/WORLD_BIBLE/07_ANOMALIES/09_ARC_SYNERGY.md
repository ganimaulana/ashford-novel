## Synergy Design Principles

1. **The value of an ARC is not determined by its individual effect**
2. Some ARC are significantly more valuable in combination than individually
3. An ARC that appears useless alone may become powerful when combined
4. Not every ARC must have a known synergy
5. Synergy should be discovered through narrative, not announced
6. Leon's primary advantage is understanding ARC synergy
7. Other characters should not immediately recognize when Leon uses synergy
8. Compatibility matters — not all ARC can be combined
9. Some ARC cancel each other or create dangerous feedback
10. Leon must learn through trial, error, and failure

---

## Aurelius Synergy Principle

When Leon operates as **Aurelius Ashcroft**, he combines multiple ARC and techniques.

Other characters should perceive:
> Aurelius himself is extraordinarily powerful.

They should NOT understand:
> Aurelius possesses dozens of ARC.

The actual toolkit may contain far more components than observed effects.

### The Constructed Powerhouse

Aurelius is not powerful because of one strong ARC.
Aurelius is powerful because of dozens of small ARC working together.

Each ARC alone is weak.
Together, they create something that appears to be raw power.

## Synergy Examples (Design Reference)

### Example A — Detection Chain
- ARC-A: Detects something unusual
- ARC-B: Makes the invisible visible
- ARC-C: Allows physical interaction with what has been revealed

Individually: detection, observation, niche interaction.
Together: a powerful investigation toolkit.

### Example B — Probability Manipulation
- ARC-A: Changes probability
- ARC-B: Delays consequences
- ARC-C: Hides evidence

Leon combines them to create outcomes that appear impossible to observers.

### Example C — The Trash Toolkit
- Broken compass that doesn't point north
- Coin that always lands on the same side
- Cracked mirror
- Stopped clock
- Old gloves
- Broken pen
- Empty bottle
- Small bell

Each appears worthless alone.
Together, in the right hands, they become a constructed powerhouse.
