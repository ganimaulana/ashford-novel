## 1. OFFICIAL DESIGNATION

**ARC — Anomalous Reality Classification**

ARC is a classification system used to identify phenomena that appear to deviate from the known rules of reality.

An ARC is NOT necessarily:
- an artifact
- a magical item
- a monster
- a supernatural creature
- a curse

ARC is a broad classification.

An ARC may be:
- an object
- artifact
- person
- entity
- location
- event
- phenomenon
- ritual
- biological anomaly
- temporal anomaly
- spatial anomaly
- informational anomaly
- dream
- symbol
- conceptual anomaly
- or a combination of multiple phenomena

ARC numbers are identifiers, NOT power rankings.

**ARC-020 is not inherently stronger than ARC-007.**

---

## 2. ARC VS STORY ARC

### ARC (uppercase)
Refers to **Anomalous Reality Classification**.
Example: `ARC-017`

### Arc (lowercase)
Refers to **narrative/story arc**.
Example: `Arc 001`, `Arc 002`

Never use `ARC I`, `ARC II`, etc. for narrative arcs if it creates ambiguity with anomaly designations.

---

## 3. CORE PHILOSOPHY

ARC is NOT an RPG item system.

Do NOT document ARC primarily as:
```
Power:
Attack:
Defense:
Level:
Damage:
```

Instead document:
- observed behavior
- rules
- conditions
- limitations
- costs
- consequences
- history
- interpretations
- interactions
- unknown properties
- narrative relevance

An ARC should create mystery, not merely provide a supernatural ability.

The important questions are:
- What does it actually do?
- Under what conditions?
- Why does it behave this way?
- What are its limitations?
- What does it cost?
- What happens when its rules are misunderstood?
- Can it interact with another ARC?
- Does its apparent function differ from its true function?
- What assumptions do people make about it?
- What deeper mystery might it be connected to?

---

## 4. INFORMATION LAYERS

Every important ARC should distinguish between:

### Layer 1 — Public Understanding
What ordinary people believe.

### Layer 2 — Faction Understanding
What organizations, churches, scholars, occultists, authorities, or collectors believe.

### Layer 3 — Historical Record
What surviving records claim.

### Layer 4 — Character Knowledge
What individual characters know.

### Layer 5 — Operational Understanding
What experienced ARC users have discovered.

### Layer 6 — True Mechanism
How the ARC actually works.

### Layer 7 — Deeper Truth
Its relationship to deeper Ashford metaphysics, if applicable.

These layers may contradict one another. Never assume that the first recorded explanation is correct.

---

## 5. MYSTERY-FIRST PRINCIPLE

ARC information must be revealed gradually.

The reader should generally encounter:
```
Observation → Strange behavior → Pattern → Partial rule → Application → Deeper implication → Truth
```

Avoid exposition dumps. Do not expose the complete ARC mechanism merely because it is known in the WORLD_BIBLE.

The WORLD_BIBLE is author knowledge. It is not automatically reader knowledge.

---

## 6. ARC RULES

Every significant ARC must have internally consistent rules.

Document:
- activation conditions
- effects
- limitations
- exceptions
- duration
- range
- triggers
- required conditions
- possible loopholes
- known failure states

Rules should be discoverable through narrative events.

## 7. ARC COST

Document potential costs.

Costs may involve:
- body
- memory
- identity
- emotion
- perception
- sleep
- relationships
- information
- spiritual consequences
- psychological consequences
- temporal consequences
- future consequences

Do not force every ARC to have a cost. Do not make every cost identical.

---

## 8. ARC SYNERGY

ARC may interact with other ARC.

### Confirmed Synergy
Canonically demonstrated.

### Suspected Synergy
The possibility is theorized but not confirmed.

### Failed Synergy
The interaction has been tested and does not work.

### Dangerous Synergy
The combination produces severe consequences.

### Unknown Interaction
Insufficient information.

### Author-Only Planned Interaction
A future planned interaction that has not yet occurred in the story.

Never treat suspected or planned synergy as established canon.

---

## 9. ARC VALUE

Every ARC should be evaluated across multiple dimensions:

- **Individual Value** — How useful is it by itself?
- **Synergy Value** — How useful when combined with another ARC?
- **Contextual Value** — How useful under specific conditions?
- **Hidden Value** — Does it possess an undiscovered function?
- **Long-Term Value** — Can it become important much later?
- **Narrative Value** — Does it serve the story?
- **World-Building Value** — Does it enrich the world?

An ARC can have low individual value but high synergy value.
An ARC can be dangerous but practically useless.
An ARC can be harmless but critically important.
An ARC can genuinely be useless.

Do not artificially make every ARC secretly powerful.

## 10. LEON'S ARC RELATIONSHIP

### Core Identity: "Best User", Not "Strongest"

Leon begins as one of the weaker members of the secret organization in terms of direct supernatural power.

He does NOT possess:
- extraordinary combat ability
- terrifying blessings
- high-tier ARC
- special lineage
- ability to defeat elite members directly

Leon survives through:
- observation
- information
- preparation
- ARC
- improvisation

NOT through:
- power → bigger power → biggest power

### The "Trash ARC" Philosophy

Leon's signature is that he collects ARC others consider useless.

Examples of "useful trash":
- A broken compass that doesn't point north
- A coin that always lands on the same side
- A cracked mirror
- A stopped clock
- Old gloves
- A broken pen
- An empty bottle
- A small bell

Each appears worthless. Each becomes valuable through:
- deep understanding
- context
- synergy
- patience

### Leon's ARC Collection

Leon gradually accumulates ARC that appear:
- weak
- useless
- defective
- harmless
- inconvenient
- overly specific
- incomplete

Some become useful through context, preparation, knowledge, synergy, environmental conditions, or loopholes.

Others remain genuinely useless.

His collection functions as a **toolkit**, not an inventory of weapons.

### Leon's Power Progression

```
Early Leon: ARC-dependent
"I am weak. I need ARC to survive."
Relies on items, tricks, information, preparation.

Mid Leon: ARC strategist
"I do not need strong ARC. I need the right ARC."
Selects ARC according to context.

Late-mid Leon: ARC architect
"I need the right combination."
Creates ARC + ARC + environment + information + timing.

Late Leon: Reality exploiter
"I need to understand what the world allows."
Uses knowledge of history, religion, factions, rituals, ARC, metaphysics, loopholes, human behavior.

Endgame: Knowledge itself becomes more powerful than ARC.
```

### The Contrast

Other characters: Power → Strategy
Leon: Strategy → Power

Strong characters gain abilities then learn to use them.
Leon gains small abilities then learns why they exist.

This makes Leon's growth feel earned.

### Limitations and Compatibility

Not all ARC can be combined. Some ARC:
- cancel each other
- create dangerous feedback
- require specific conditions to coexist
- conflict with the user's nature
- conflict with each other's rules

Leon must learn:
- which ARC are compatible
- which combinations are safe
- which combinations are worth the risk
- when to use ARC alone
- when to combine
- when to discard

### Deliberately "Useless" ARC

Some ARC exist to be useless — for now.

They serve:
- foreshadowing
- world-building
- misdirection
- future payoff
- thematic resonance

Do not force every ARC to become powerful. Some remain mysteries. Some remain jokes. Some remain tragedies.

### Leon Makes Mistakes

Leon should:
- misinterpret ARC rules
- combine ARC incorrectly
- trust the wrong ARC
- fail to anticipate consequences
- learn from failure

His growth is not linear. He backtracks, misjudges, and pays costs.

This makes his eventual mastery feel earned.

## 11. AURELIUS ASHCROFT — CONSTRUCTED POWERHOUSE

When Leon operates under the identity of **Aurelius Ashcroft**, he combines multiple ARC and techniques.

Other characters perceive:
> Aurelius himself is extraordinarily powerful.

They should NOT understand that his apparent power comes from a carefully constructed combination of ARC.

### The Architecture

Aurelius is not powerful because of one strong ARC.
Aurelius is powerful because of dozens of small ARC working together.

Examples:
- A broken compass + a coin + a cracked mirror + a stopped clock + old gloves + a broken pen + an empty bottle + a small bell + ...

Each ARC alone is weak.
Together, they create something that appears to be raw power.

### The Illusion

Leon conceals the underlying mechanism through:
- misdirection
- false attribution
- overlapping effects
- timing
- prepared environments
- selective demonstrations
- concealed ARC
- contingency planning

No single character should know the complete architecture of Aurelius's ARC toolkit unless explicitly established by canon.

### The Principle

> **Aurelius Ashcroft is not a powerful man with ARC. He is a man who became powerful through ARC.**

This is the central conceit of the Aurelius identity.

## 16. ARC BEHAVIOR PATTERNS

ARC behavior is not limited to simple cause → effect.

The 12 behavior patterns framework is documented in `ARC_BEHAVIOR_PATTERNS.md`.

Every ARC should be analyzed for which patterns it employs:

| # | Pattern | Core |
|---|---------|------|
| 01 | Causality | A causes B |
| 02 | Correlation | A always appears with B, but does not necessarily cause it |
| 03 | Conditional | A + B + C required for D |
| 04 | Trigger | Specific condition activates phenomenon |
| 05 | Exchange | Gain X by giving Y |
| 06 | Transfer | Something moves from one state to another |
| 07 | Substitution | Something replaces something else |
| 08 | Recursion | Effect repeats or generates itself |
| 09 | Feedback | Effect strengthens/weakens its source |
| 10 | Accumulation | Effect builds over time |
| 11 | Threshold | Effect appears after specific point |
| 12 | Inversion | True effect is opposite of apparent effect |

**Key principle:** A single ARC may operate through multiple patterns simultaneously.
This layering creates depth, mystery, and discovery opportunities.

Leon's ARC Analysis Method — test each ARC:
1. What triggers it?
2. What changes?
3. What is exchanged?
4. What moves?
5. What is replaced?
6. What accumulates?
7. What happens at the threshold?
8. Does the effect repeat itself?
9. Does the effect strengthen itself?
10. Is the apparent effect actually the opposite of the real mechanism?

**Most important question:**
> **"What assumption am I making that the ARC itself never actually confirmed?"**

## 17. ARC CLASSIFICATION — MULTI-AXIS

ARC classification is NOT a power ranking (D/C/B/A/S).
Each ARC has **independent axes** — one ARC can be light but difficult to control, or very powerful but extremely limited.

Complete classification system: `ARC_CLASSIFICATION_SYSTEM.md`.

Each ARC has minimum 6 attributes:

| Axis | Options |
|------|---------|
| **Form** | Object, Entity, Location, Event, Phenomenon, Information, Ritual, Conceptual, Composite |
| **Persistence** | Permanent, Temporary, Conditional |
| **Use Type** | Reusable, Limited-use, Consumable |
| **Threat** | Harmless, Minor, Dangerous, Severe, Catastrophic |
| **Control** | Stable, Conditional, Unstable, Uncontrollable |
| **Temporal Constraint** | None, Time-limited, Periodic, Time-specific |

Additional dimensions:
- **Activation Conditions**: Physical, Environmental, Temporal, Behavioral, Mental, Emotional, Informational, Relational, Symbolic, Composite
- **Negation Level**: Avoidance, Interruption, Suppression, Cancellation, Reversal
- **Utility Dimensions**: Raw Power, Utility, Precision, Reliability, Synergy, Counter Potential

**Leon's Principle:**
> **"An ARC does not need to be powerful. It only needs to obey a rule."**

Once he knows the rule: Rule → Prediction → Manipulation → Counter → Synergy.

## 18. CANON STATUS SYSTEM

Every ARC must clearly indicate one of:

### Canon
Approved and established.

### Proposed
Concept exists but has not been approved.

### Unknown
The existence or details are intentionally uncertain.

### Retconned
Previously established information has been superseded.

Never silently convert Proposed → Canon without explicit approval.

## 20. QUALITY CONTROL FOR NEW ARC

Before adding a new ARC, verify:

1. Is the concept distinct?
2. Does it have a specific rule?
3. Does it have limitations?
4. Is the behavior internally consistent?
5. Does it fit Ashford?
6. Does it fit the 1816 setting?
7. Does it contribute to mystery?
8. Does it contribute to world-building?
9. Does it contribute to characterization?
10. Does it avoid RPG-like design?
11. Does it avoid direct SCP imitation?
12. Does it have narrative purpose?
13. Is its power level appropriate?
14. Can it interact naturally with existing ARC?
15. Is the potential synergy justified?
16. Is its true mechanism separated from observed behavior?
17. Is the reveal timing appropriate?
18. Can it support long-term foreshadowing?
19. Does it create meaningful consequences?
20. Does it strengthen Ashford rather than merely add lore?

If not, revise or reject it.

## 23. NO SILENT RETCON

If existing Ashford canon conflicts with this ARC framework:

1. Identify the conflict.
2. Record it.
3. Determine whether the ARC concept can be adjusted.
4. Preserve established canon whenever possible.
5. Report the conflict before making a major canon alteration.

## 24. READER KNOWLEDGE VS AUTHOR KNOWLEDGE

This distinction is mandatory.

The WORLD_BIBLE may contain:
- True Mechanism
- Future Reveal
- Planned Synergy
- Ultimate Purpose

The novel must NOT automatically reveal these facts.

Always track:
- What the author knows
- What Leon knows
- What other characters know
- What the reader knows

Mystery pacing depends on keeping these layers separate.

## 25. FINAL PRINCIPLE

The ARC system exists to support the story, not to replace it.

> **An ARC is valuable not because it is powerful, but because of what it allows the story, the world, and its characters to discover.**

For Leon specifically:
> **He does not need the strongest ARC. He needs to understand the ARC nobody else understands.**

And for Ashford as a whole:
> **The reader should eventually realize that the strange things scattered throughout the story were never random.**
