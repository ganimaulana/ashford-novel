## Key Design Principles

### 1. ARC ≠ SCP Foundation

**Scale:**
- SCP: Global organization
- ARC: Local informal network di Alderwick

**Tone:**
- SCP: Clinical, detached, institutional
- ARC: Personal, cautious, human-scale

**Resources:**
- SCP: Unlimited funding, technology, personnel
- ARC: Limited resources, normal people, improvisation

**Goals:**
- SCP: Secure, Contain, Protect
- ARC: Understand, Document, Preserve Normality

### 2. Underground = Informal Network

**NOT an organization:**
- No hierarchy
- No membership
- No headquarters
- No official structure

**IS a network:**
- Personal connections
- Shared information
- Loose cooperation
- Varying involvement levels

### 3. Rules = Guidelines, Not Laws

**The Ten Universal Rules:**
- Learned through experience
- Taught through stories
- Not rigidly enforced
- Sometimes broken (dengan consequences)
- Passed down informally

### 4. Local and Grounded

**Alderwick-specific:**
- Cases tied to local history
- Characters dengan normal lives
- Investigation fits into daily routines
- Maintaining normalcy important

### 5. Human Limitations

**Characters are NOT:**
- Supernatural experts dari awal
- Always right
- Omniscient
- Heroes atau fighters

**Characters ARE:**
- Learning as they go
- Making mistakes
- Cautious dan pragmatic
- Normal people dealing dengan unusual situations

## Classification System

### Categories

1. **OBJECT** - Physical items with anomalous properties
2. **LOCATION** - Places dengan unusual characteristics
3. **ENTITY** - Beings yang exhibit anomalous behavior
4. **EVENT** - Recurring phenomena dengan temporal patterns
5. **PATTERN** - Abstract interconnections
6. **PHENOMENON** - Environmental atau perceptual anomalies
7. **NULL** - Misidentified atau insufficient data

### Risk Levels

- **R0** - Safe (No Danger)
- **R1** - Minimal Risk
- **R2** - Predictable Danger
- **R3** - Conditional Danger
- **R4** - Significant Threat
- **R5** - Critical Threat
- **NULL** - Unknown / Unclassified

**Focus:** Risk about predictability, bukan hanya danger level.

---

## The Ten Universal Rules

1. **Never Assume Intent** - Anomalies tidak necessarily hostile atau helpful
2. **Never Test Without Reason** - Don\'t poke things unnecessarily
3. **Never Replicate** - Don\'t try to recreate anomalies
4. **Never Publicize** - Keep knowledge dari general public
5. **Record Before Interpretation** - Document facts first
6. **Never Remove an Anchor** - Don\'t disrupt binding elements
7. **Do Not Follow the Voice** - Resist compulsion to respond
8. **Two-Person Rule** - Never investigate alone
9. **No Heroics** - Survival over glory
10. **Preserve Normality** - Maintain appearance of normal life

## Writing Guidelines

### For Story Integration

**DO:**
- Show gradual discovery of network
- Include learning process untuk rules
- Show limitations dan challenges
- Make investigations feel organic
- Include mundane aspects
- Show disagreements dalam network
- Maintain human scale

**DON\'T:**
- Make underground feel like organization
- Give characters instant expertise
- Make everything supernatural
- Remove consequences
- Make it too efficient atau organized
- Forget public/underground separation

### For Creating New Cases

**Process:**
1. Determine category dan risk level
2. Document objective observations first
3. Note historical context
4. Describe behavior patterns
5. Identify triggers atau conditions
6. Record containment/avoidance methods
7. Note limitations dan unknowns

**Format:** See draft examples dalam `Draft/` folder
