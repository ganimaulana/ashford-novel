## 2. City-Level Atmosphere

From `01_GEOGRAPHY/01_Alderwick_CITY_OVERVIEW.md`:

**Karakteristik atmosfer:**
- Tua tetapi hidup (old but alive)
- Tenang tetapi tidak mati (calm but not dead)
- Normal tetapi berlapis sejarah (normal but layered with history)
- Sesekali sunyi, sesekali ramai (sometimes quiet, sometimes busy)
- Sedikit usang di beberapa bagian (slightly worn in places)

**Yang HARUS DIHINDARI:**
- Membuat Alderwick terasa seperti kota horor
- Menggunakan kata "misterius", "gelap", "angker" terlalu sering

**Prinsip:** Atmosfer muncul dari detail konkret dan kontras — not from labeling it with adjectives.

## 3. Architecture & Contrast as the Source of Atmosphere

From `02_ARCHITECTURE/02_ARCHITECTURE_MASTER.md`:

Atmosfer muncul dari **kontras**, bukan dari "semua bangunan tua dan gelap".

**Contoh kontras yang menghasilkan atmosfer:**
- Bangunan Victorian di sebelah bangunan modern
- Bangunan terawat di sebelah bangunan terbengkalai
- Toko renovasi di bangunan tua
- Fasad indah dengan bagian belakang terbengkalai
- Kota ramai dengan tempat-tempat sepi
- Normal city life dengan paranormal moments

**Alderwick harus tetap terasa:** Normal · Hidup · Dihuni · Tidak selalu gelap · Tidak selalu sepi

**Paranormal Activity:**
- Jangan menjadikan arsitektur tua sebagai bukti bahwa sesuatu supernatural sedang terjadi
- Bangunan tua hanya menyediakan **atmosfer**
- Paranormal tidak hanya terjadi di tempat sepi — dapat terjadi di tengah crowds
- Kota terus berjalan normal bahkan ketika paranormal terjadi

## 4. Per-Building Atmosphere

From `03_BUILDINGS/05_BUILDINGS.md` and related files:

| Building | Atmosphere |
|---|---|
| Margaret's Tearoom & Coffee Shop | Warm and friendly; community hub where people know each other and exchange news; smell of tea, coffee, baked goods |
| Bell's Antiquarian Shop | Overstuffed, cozy, slightly claustrophobic; time slows inside (old paper, dust, wood polish) |
| Bell's Bookshop | Better-lit and organized; reading area; academic |
| Alderwick Library | Quiet, formal, musty; sound of pages turning |
| St. Bartholomew's Church | Solemn, cool, dimly lit; smell of incense and old wood |
| No. 13, Bellweather Lane (Leon's residence) | Quiet; slightly isolated; feels older than rest of lane; Leon notices things others don't |
| Bellweather House | Older, partially abandoned/repurposed; shares history with No. 13 |
| Margarets Cafe | Hangat dan ramah; pusat komunitas tempat orang saling kenal dan bertukar kabar |
| Abandoned / Neglected buildings | Adds atmosphere **without** making the city feel dead; contrast between active and empty is effective — do NOT overuse |

**Prinsip bangunan kosong** (`03_BUILDINGS/01_ABANDONED_AND_NEGLECTED.md`):
- Empty buildings can add atmosfer **tanpa** membuat kota terasa mati
- Kontras antara bangunan aktif dan kosong bisa efektif
- JANGAN overuse
- Tanya saat menambah: benar-benar perlu? alasan realistis? proporsi? untuk plot atau hanya atmosfer?

## 8. Quick Author Checklist

When writing any scene in Alderwick, confirm:
- [ ] Does the scene show atmosphere through a **concrete detail or contrast**, not an adjective label?
- [ ] Is the city still **alive/normal/inhabited** (not a horror set)?
- [ ] If a building is old, does it provide *atmosphere* — not proof of the supernatural?
- [ ] Is paranormal activity allowed to happen **in crowds**, not only in quiet places?
- [ ] Is there a quiet undercurrent of **anxiety/political tension** beneath ordinary life?
- [ ] Are empty buildings used sparingly, for contrast, not as the default?
