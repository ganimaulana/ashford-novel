## III. Character Profiles

### Sir Edmund Harcourt

**Age:** 58
**Position:** Senior Magistrate, Alderwick district
**Class:** Landed gentry
**Alignment:** Crown Conservative
**Status:** APPROVED

**Background:**
Family has held land around Alderwick for generations.
Former militia officer.
Served during the Continental Wars in administrative/military capacity.
Now one of the most influential men in Alderwick.

**Personality:**
- Formal
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Dislikes violence
- Values order above all

**Core belief:**
> "Order is more important than justice."

**Narrative Function:**
Not a villain. A man who genuinely believes stability is the highest good.

Can value order over justice — creating moral complexity.

**Flaw:**
Can value order over justice.

## IV. Narrative Guidelines

### 1. No Political Purity
Every character has mixed motives.

### 2. Politics is Personal
Political beliefs come from personal experience.

### 3. Complexity Over Caricature
Avoid "evil aristocrat" or "noble poor" stereotypes.

### 4. Politics Serves Mystery
Political characters may provide:
- Motives for crimes
- Hidden alliances
- Suppressed information
- False leads
- Genuine clues

### 5. Not All Politics is Visible
Most characters do not think of themselves as "political."

## V. Expanded Character Profiles

### Three Truths System

For each character, there are three layers:

| Layer | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Public Identity** | What the world sees |
| **Private Motivation** | What they actually want |
| **Secret** | What they hide from everyone |

### Sir Edmund Harcourt

#### Public Identity
Respected Senior Magistrate of Alderwick. Formal, calm, intelligent. Known for fairness and dedication to order.

#### Political Belief
Crown Conservatives. Believes order prevents revolution. Supports the Church of the Crown and the Grain Protection Acts.

#### Private Motivation
Genuinely fears another war. Saw too much death during the Continental Wars. Will do anything to prevent chaos.

#### Secret
During the Continental Wars, Harcourt made a controversial decision that led to the deaths of several soldiers under his command. He was never charged, but he has lived with guilt ever since. He covers this not because he is corrupt, but because he believes revealing it would destroy the stability he has built.

#### Question
> Is he hiding his past because he is corrupt — or because he believes his actions saved Alderwick?

#### Potential Future Arc

### James Calloway

#### Public Identity
Local constable. Practical, non-political, loyal to duty. Known for his fairness and reliability.

#### Political Belief
Non-political. Believes in law and order, but not in political factions. Sees himself as serving the community.

#### Private Motivation
Wants to do his job well. Takes pride in being a good constable. Fears the political tensions will tear Alderwick apart.

#### Secret
Calloway has been reporting to multiple parties. Not because he is disloyal, but because different people have asked him for information. He does not fully understand the political implications of what he reports. He is an unwitting informant.

#### Hidden Trait
Beneath the ordinary constable is a latent sensitivity to Alderwick's old forces — a hereditary knack that lets him feel where something has lingered. He has used it silently for years on cases ordinary law cannot touch, and told no one. This is what lets him confirm the Bellweather phenomenon without needing to name it.
