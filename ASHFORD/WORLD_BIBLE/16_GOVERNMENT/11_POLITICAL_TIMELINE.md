## VI. Narrative Integration

### How to Use This Timeline

1. **Do not dump this information on the reader**
2. **Reveal events gradually through:**
   - Newspaper headlines
   - Tavern conversations
   - Public notices
   - Leon's observations
   - Other characters' comments
3. **Let the reader feel the pressure rising**
4. **When The Blackmere Assembly happens, it should feel inevitable**

### Leon's Awareness Progression

| Phase | Leon Knows | What He Sees |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| Early (Ch 1–10) | Personal mystery only | Small political details in background |
| Middle (Ch 11–25) | Something is wrong in Alderwick | Political tensions, investigations begin |
| Late (Ch 26–35) | Alderwick is heading toward crisis | Rising pressure, radical activity |
| Crisis (Ch 36–45) | The Blackmere Assembly is coming | Major event and aftermath |
| Resolution (Ch 46+) | How it all connects | Truth revealed |

## VIII. The Blackmere Assembly

### What It Is
A large reform gathering that escalates into confrontation between civilians and authorities.

### What It Is Not
- Not a copy of Peterloo
- Not a simple good-vs-evil event
- Not the end of the story
- Not the central mystery (but connected to it)

### What It Reveals
- The true nature of power in Alderwick
- Who is really pulling strings
- How far the government will go
- How far radicals will go
- What ordinary people are willing to endure
- How the central mystery intersects with politics

### Aftermath
- Political crisis
- Arrests and trials
- Public anger
- Unresolved tensions
- Seeds of future conflict
