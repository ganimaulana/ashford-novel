## V. Information Flow Integration

### How Information Moves Through All Systems

```
Event occurs
     ↓
Witnesses observe
     ↓
Information spreads through:
- Political channels (government informants)
- Criminal networks (paid information)
- Religious networks (confession, gossip)
- Media (newspapers, pamphlets)
- Word of mouth (taverns, markets)
     ↓
Information reaches:
- Political characters (who use it for power)
- Criminal characters (who use it for profit)
- Religious characters (who use it for influence)
- Leon (who uses it for investigation)
     ↓
Information is:
- Acted upon
- Sold
- Suppressed
- Distorted
- Forgotten
     ↓
Leon must piece together:
- What happened
- Why it happened
- Who benefits
- What is being hidden
- How it connects to the central mystery
```

## VIII. Narrative Integration Guidelines

### 1. No Lore Dumps
Information enters through action, dialogue, and observation.

### 2. Show Connections Gradually
Leon discovers connections over time, not all at once.

### 3. Every Element Serves Mystery
Politics, crime, and religion all connect to the central mystery.

### 4. No Faction is Pure
Every faction has good people, bad people, and complicated people.

### 5. Information is Power
Whoever controls information controls Alderwick.

### 6. The Past Explains the Present
Historical events have consequences that echo forward.

### 7. Mystery Pacing Controls Everything
Information is released according to mystery pacing, not world-building completeness.

## IX. Daniel Carter Integration

### Natural Bridge Between Past and Present

Daniel James Carter is a history student. He has a natural function:

- Knows historical facts
- Understands political context
- Can research old records
- Asks questions about the past

### Limitations

- Daniel is not an encyclopedia
- He may have facts but miss their significance
- He may draw wrong conclusions from correct facts
- He is focused on academic history, not current politics
- He doesn't know about ARC or the underground

### How Leon Uses Daniel

| What Daniel Knows | What Leon Learns |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Historical context | Why things are the way they are |
| Old events | Parallels to current situations |
| Political theory | Framework for understanding power |
| Research methods | How to find buried information |

## X. Arthur Edmund Bell Integration

### Natural Bridge to Information Economy

Arthur owns a bookshop/antiquities store. He has a natural function:

- Access to old books and documents
- Knowledge of where information is stored
- Connections to collectors and scholars
- Understanding of historical artifacts

### Limitations

- Arthur knows where information is, not necessarily what it means
- He may not recognize the significance of what he has
- He is a bookseller, not a detective
- He has his own secrets and agenda

### How Leon Uses Arthur

| What Arthur Knows | What Leon Learns |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Where records are stored | Location of specific documents |
| Historical context | Background for current events |
| Who collects what | Potential allies or information sources |
| Old maps and documents | Physical evidence of hidden history |

## XI. Integration Checklist

Before writing new content, verify:

- [ ] Does this connect to existing canon?
- [ ] Does it serve the mystery?
- [ ] Is it consistent with character motivations?
- [ ] Does it respect the Pressure Index level?
- [ ] Is the information source plausible?
- [ ] Are the Three Truths maintained?
- [ ] Does it avoid lore-dumping?
- [ ] Does it respect faction complexity?
- [ ] Does it create new questions while answering old ones?
- [ ] Is it discoverable through Leon's investigation?
