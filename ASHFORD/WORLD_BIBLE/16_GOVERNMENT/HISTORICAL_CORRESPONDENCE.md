## Core Principle

> **Ashford is NOT modern England with names replaced.**
> 
> Ashford is a fictional historical-inspired world whose primary inspiration is Britain circa 1816.

Real history is a **REFERENCE LAYER**, not the Ashford canon.

---

## AI Implementation Rules

When generating Ashford content:

1. Always use Ashford canon names.
2. Never accidentally substitute real-world proper nouns into narrative text.
3. Historical names may appear only in research/reference documentation unless explicitly requested.
4. Historical analogues are NOT canon.
5. Historical events are NOT canon until adapted and approved.
6. Preserve broad historical plausibility.
7. Adapt historical mechanisms rather than copying historical scenes.
8. Existing Ashford canon overrides newly generated ideas.
9. If two canon documents conflict, flag the conflict rather than silently choosing one.
10. Do not create real historical figures as Ashford characters unless explicitly requested.
11. Do not create modern institutions using modern terminology unless justified by the period.
12. Do not assume a fictional entity is identical to its historical analogue.
13. Preserve the distinction between inspiration and canon.

## Canon Status System

| Status | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **DRAFT** | Proposed but not approved. |
| **APPROVED** | Accepted for use but still open to refinement. |
| **LOCKED** | Canon. Do not alter without explicit instruction. |
| **HISTORICAL REFERENCE** | Real-world information used only for research. |
| **DEPRECATED** | Previously proposed but no longer valid. |

Never silently upgrade DRAFT → LOCKED.

### Analogue Rules for Royal Figures

**King Alaric III**

Historical function:
A long-reigning British monarch whose declining ability to personally govern contributes to the creation of a regency.

Ashford canon:
King Alaric III is the monarch of Albion.

Canon rule:
Alaric III is NOT George III.

Do NOT automatically import:
- George III's exact biography
- George III's family members
- George III's personal relationships
- George III's medical history
- George III's quotations
- George III's private events
- real royal scandals

Only the broad political function may be used unless explicitly incorporated into Ashford canon.

## Usage Guide for AI Writers

### How to Use This Document

1. **Identify the historical mechanism** you need to adapt.
2. **Find the Ashford canon equivalent** in the tables above.
3. **Use only the Ashford canon name** in narrative text.
4. **Research the historical analogue** for plausibility and context.
5. **Adapt the mechanism**, not the specific details.
6. **Mark new adaptations as DRAFT** until approved.

### Example Workflow

```
Need: A political faction that supports the Crown and social order.
↓
Look up: Crown Conservatives (analogue: Tory Party)
↓
Use in narrative: "The Crown Conservatives argued..."
↓
Research: Tory Party history for plausibility
↓
Adapt: Create Ashford-specific Conservative faction
↓
Status: DRAFT until approved
```
