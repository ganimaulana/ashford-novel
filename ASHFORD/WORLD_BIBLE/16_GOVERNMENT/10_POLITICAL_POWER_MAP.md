## Core Principle

> **Power in Alderwick flows through three layers: official, unofficial, and hidden.**

Each layer influences the others. None is fully aware of the others.

## V. Pressure Points

### Where Power is Fragile

| Pressure Point | What Happens If Broken |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Magistrate legitimacy | People stop respecting the law |
| Church moral authority | Social cohesion collapses |
| Merchant credit | Local economy stalls |
| Aristocratic land | Employment disappears |
| Informant network | Government becomes blind |
| Postal system | Information stops flowing |

### Who Exploits These

| Faction | Target Pressure Point |
|---------|----------------------|
| Radicals | Magistrate legitimacy, Church authority |
| Criminals | Merchant credit, Informant network |
| Reform Coalition | Aristocratic land, Magistrate decisions |
| Unknown Network | All of the above |

## VII. Principles

### 1. Power is Never Absolute
Every node in the power structure has limits.

### 2. Information is Power
Whoever controls information controls Alderwick.

### 3. Money Follows Power
Economic power reinforces political power.

### 4. Legitimacy is Fragile
Once people stop believing in authority, it crumbles.

### 5. The Hidden Controls the Visible
What people don't see shapes what they do.
