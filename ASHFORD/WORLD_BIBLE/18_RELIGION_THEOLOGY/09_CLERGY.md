## Established Clergy & Offices (from Chapters 1–47)

### The Rector
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Preaches at St. Bartholomew's. Sermons observed: on keeping / the steward found faithful (Luke) (Ch 15, 20); on the new thing and the old thing (Ch 33). SOURCE: Ch 15, 20, 33.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Has tea with Mr. Hare, the draper / rector's warden. SOURCE: Ch 34.
- [EVIDENCE: IMPLIED] Name not given in Ch 1–47. UNKNOWN.

### The Clerk (parish clerk)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The present clerk is the son of T. Wren, who was clerk before him. Keeps the church books. Holds one of the two tower keys. Does not change from year to year (unlike churchwardens). SOURCE: Ch 13, 18, 21.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] The clerk's entry-doctrine: the reckoning records what was sold, not what became of it. SOURCE: Ch 18.

### T. Wren (the elder clerk)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Former clerk; father of the present clerk. Entered the bell sale (1809). Gave the Academy copy into keeping (1806) with a letter directing it be shown to any that should ask. Wrote the marginal note in the London probate copy ("occupant not to be named at law"). SOURCE: Ch 13, 20, 21.

### E. Hodge (keeper of the keys)
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Keeper of the keys before the clerk's father. Reported dead by the verger. The name "E. Hodge" appears filled in the tenure blank of the Academy copy (1806). Hodge's second ring was never given up, in no man's knowledge. SOURCE: Ch 18, 21.

### The Verger
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Keeps the tower door and the church door. Has kept one of the two tower keys for eleven years. Never went up the tower at night. Carries a lantern low. SOURCE: Ch 18, 19, 20, 24, 25.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Reported the frost evidence at the tower door (two comings, one going) and the four tellings of the gate. SOURCE: Ch 19, 24.

### The Churchwardens
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] Two wardens: one chosen by the parish (Farmer Goding, elected at Easter), one given by the rector (Mr. Hare the draper). Change every year. Stand over the parish's goods and books. SOURCE: Ch 18, 34.
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] They carried the rate at the vestry meeting (Dec 1816) at the figure the bench named. SOURCE: Ch 25.

### Hodge's second key-ring
- [EVIDENCE: CONFIRMED] A second key-ring existed, was "laid by" (put aside) by Hodge, never given up, in no man's knowledge. SOURCE: Ch 13, 18, 32.

## Clergy Knowledge of the Supernatural
- [EVIDENCE: STRONGLY IMPLIED] The verger knows more than the town (the Thursday of the bell; the frost evidence; the gate). SOURCE: Ch 19, 24, 25.
- [EVIDENCE: IMPLIED] The rector's sermons on "keeping" reflect the town's own doctrine about itself, suggesting the church and the keeping are intertwined. Not confirmed as explicit supernatural knowledge. SOURCE: Ch 20.
