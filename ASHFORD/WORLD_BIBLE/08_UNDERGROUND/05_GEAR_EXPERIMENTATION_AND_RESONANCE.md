## 2. FIRST EXPERIMENT (CONFIRMED, Ch40; escalation CONFIRMED, Ch49)

**Structure as it already stands:**

```
proximity        → nothing, at every distance, every site         [Ch36–47]
contact          → silence, then a single tremor through stone    [Ch40]
applied pressure → rotation, clicks deep in the stone             [Ch49]
rotation         → one inch of movement, cold air, a faint sound  [Ch49]
withdrawal       → everything stops; no trace left                [Ch40, Ch49]
```

**What it accomplishes (already in canon):** the Gear has a physical relationship with the mechanism; the relationship is more than a lock (the teeth do not fit the clock; the depression "accepted it" but proved nothing); vibration through stone is observable; and the inscription acquires a second layer by proximity to a thing that clicks and answers. The Gear itself signals nothing — every observable response is located in the stone/mechanism, never in the metal.

**What it withholds:** whether the sound beyond the door was wind, water, stone, or something else. Leon writes that he could not tell. This ambiguity is CONFIRMED and must not be retroactively resolved.

**PROPOSED future experiments (Arc 2+, not written):**
- Stone touched with a bare hand, Gear absent → expected: weak or no response (isolates the Gear as initiator).
- Gear installed, second hand flat on the surrounding stone → expected: stronger or clearer perception (isolates contact as the perceptual channel).
- Same procedure repeated at the **second** door beneath the common → tests whether the two doors behave identically.
- Same procedure with the Token present → tests interaction between the two artifact systems. **Do not resolve.**
- Repetition after an interval → tests whether the mechanism has states (see Experiment 7).

**Rule:** not every experiment yields a supernatural result. Null results are recorded, not skipped. Leon learns by **comparison and failure**, never by revelation.

## 4. RESONANCE MODEL (PROPOSED — explanatory bridge only)

The chain, never stated outright in prose:

```
PHYSICAL CONTACT → MECHANICAL RESPONSE → RESONANCE → PERCEPTION → SUPERNATURAL RESPONSE
```

- **Gear** = the medium that initiates.
- **Stone/mechanism** = the structure that carries.
- **Resonance** = the bridge (PROPOSED; the word is author-side and should appear in prose sparingly if at all).
- **Perception** = how Leon experiences it — tremor, pressure, an unclassifiable sound (never a temperature change in the Gear; warmth-perception belongs to the Token system only).
- **Supernatural response** = the consequence, always partial.

Resonance may be affected by contact, position, material, timing, the keeper's sign, the perceiver, and the mechanism's state. **No numerical system. No levels, tiers, percentages, charges, or cooldowns. No RPG terminology anywhere, author-side or in prose.** Everything remains diegetic: Leon can only report what he felt and whether it repeated.

## 9. WHY THE DOOR DOES NOT OPEN

**CONFIRMED reason:** it partially did, and Leon closed it (Ch49), then chose repeatedly not to repeat the act (Ch49 "That was precisely why I did not," Ch57, Ch63, Ch64). The Door stays closed because the keeper keeps it closed. This is a **character** answer, and it is stronger than a mechanical one.

**PROPOSED mechanical reason (do not confirm):** the Gear is one component of a larger mechanism and can establish contact without satisfying the conditions for passage. Candidate functions, none selected: identify a compatible bearer · wake a dormant mechanism · transmit a signal · permit perception · prepare a later interaction · serve as one required physical component among several. Canon leans toward *kept sign/memory* over *key* — Ch57 (Leon refuses to classify it), Ch60 ("the gear was the marker"), Ch64 ("made to be kept — kept as a sign, kept as a memory"). The Gear is explicitly **not** the wrong key; it is not established as a key at all. Whether the mechanism responded *to* the Gear, *recognized* it, or merely required its shape is UNKNOWN — protected.

## 11. HARD CONTINUITY RULES

1. The first Gear experiment (Ch40) does **not** open the Door.
2. The Ch49 partial opening — one inch, then closed — is CONFIRMED and must not be erased or expanded retroactively.
3. The Gear is not a conventional key, is never called "the keeper's key," and is not a universal key. The keeper's sign is associated with the key function.
4. **The Gear has no supernatural temperature response. It never becomes warm.** Warmth belongs to the Token/Object system exclusively. Any future prose giving the Gear warmth, glow, or an obvious supernatural signal violates canon.
5. The Keeper's Door (the third door, beneath Bellweather House, Ch78) has **no depression for the Gear and no lock**; it answers the Tokens. Keeper's Seal ≠ Gear. Keeper's Door ≠ Gear mechanism. The Keeper's Door does not require the Gear.
6. The Gear is not itself a voice and does not speak.
7. Inscription meaning and function remain UNRESOLVED.
8. Resonance is never a stat, level, tier, charge, or percentage.
9. Physical mechanics and supernatural effect must always have a causal bridge; no effect without contact or proximity.
10. Leon learns through observation, comparison, and failed hypotheses — never revelation.
11. Do not reveal the complete Gear system prematurely; the nine-Gear model stays PROPOSED.
12. Do not introduce a second confirmed Gear.
13. Never use "Gear A/B/I/II" or any numbering in-world.
14. Gear ≠ ERC. An ERC record may document the Gear; the Gear is not an ERC.
15. Gear ≠ Token. They may interact; they are not merged.
16. Do not relocate the Door. It is beneath the mill; the second door lies in the passage beneath the common; the chamber is beneath both houses.
17. Do not establish the ritual as confirmed.
18. After Ch129 the confirmed Gear is with **Daniel**. Any Arc 2+ Gear experiment must account for Leon not having it.
