# ANONYMOUS TOKEN NETWORK — DESIGN FILE

**Status:** PROPOSED — ARC 2+ (not CONFIRMED, not implemented in manuscript)
**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Nature:** Design/preparation document. All concepts herein are PROPOSED unless an existing canon statement confirms them.

---

## A. STATUS

**PROPOSED — ARC 2+**

This document describes a future-narrative possibility: a decentralized, anonymous network of Token recipients connected by a hidden common source. It is a design framework only.

---

## B. CHARACTER ROLE — ELEANOR SHAW

**Proposed role:** First Anonymous Token Recipient — PRIMARY CANDIDATE.

See `04_CHARACTERS/characters/Eleanor_Shaw.md` (ARC 2+ PROPOSAL section) for the full character proposal.

Key points:
- Eleanor is an intelligent, curious person drawn to local history, old manuscripts, folklore, antiquities, records, inscriptions, and unexplained events.
- She is NOT an occultist or supernatural expert.
- Her curiosity, not supernatural competence, draws her close to something genuinely supernatural.
- She must NOT become "female Leon," an instant expert, or a passive recipient.
- She retains full independent agency.

---

## C. MOTIVATION (Proposed)

Eleanor's motivation develops from curiosity and intellectual adventure:
1. She investigates mysteries believing most have rational/historical explanations.
2. She encounters the Token and cannot fully rationalize it.
3. Her curiosity becomes personal.
4. She must decide whether the risk is worth continuing.

---

## D. PERSONALITY (Proposed)

- Curious, historically minded, intellectually adventurous
- Capable of incorrect assumptions
- Less knowledgeable about supernatural reality than she believes
- Independent, with genuine agency
- Not flawless; makes mistakes

---

## E. KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY (Proposed)

**Eleanor knows (after contact):**
- She received a Token
- Someone intentionally contacted her
- The sender knows something about her investigation

**Eleanor does NOT know:**
- Leon's identity
- The complete supernatural system
- The XIII Symbols
- The full meaning of the Token
- The existence/structure of any secret organization or network
- The sender's ultimate objective

---

## F. TOKEN RELATIONSHIP (Proposed)

A Token given to Eleanor must have a narrative function:
- access, identification, testing, communication, triggering a phenomenon, interacting with a supernatural mechanism, selecting/recognizing a potential recipient, or establishing controlled contact

It must lead to: a decision, an investigation, a discovery, a test, access, or a meaningful consequence.

It must NOT be an inventory object carried indefinitely without purpose.

Specific Token behavior remains PROPOSED until supported by the supernatural system canon.

---

## G. ANONYMOUS CORRESPONDENCE (Proposed)

Leon does NOT personally introduce himself, openly recruit, or initially meet her face-to-face (see OPEN QUESTION in Eleanor's profile re: prior Ch 57 acquaintance).

Historically plausible 1816 delivery methods (no modern drops):
- Letter slot
- Envelope beneath a door
- Discreetly placed letter
- Delivery through a servant or intermediary

The package may contain:
1. A Token
2. A short anonymous letter
3. Possibly a simple instruction

The first message must NOT explain: Leon's identity, the full supernatural system, the XIII Symbols, the Rank system, the existence of an organization, or the identity of other recipients.

**Mutual uncertainty:**
- Eleanor knows "someone knows something I do not."
- Eleanor does NOT know who/where the sender is, how they know about her, whether they are trustworthy, whether others are involved, whether the Token is benevolent or dangerous.
- Leon does not have perfect knowledge of her either.

---

## H. LEON'S RELATIONSHIP TO ELEANOR (Proposed)

Leon does NOT distribute Tokens because he "needs members for his organization." His motivation develops from necessity:

1. He encounters a problem he cannot solve alone.
2. He recognizes Eleanor's useful perspective, skill, or disposition.
3. He investigates her from a distance.
4. He determines whether she can be trusted.
5. He gives her controlled access to a Token.
6. He observes what she does with it.
7. Only later does he consider whether she could become part of a larger network.

He recruits because a person's existence/perspective becomes relevant to the mystery — not because he is building a conventional secret society from the beginning.

---

## I. FUTURE NETWORK POTENTIAL (Proposed)

Eleanor may eventually be one of several people who independently receive Tokens or instructions from the same hidden source.

- Recipients do NOT immediately know one another's identities.
- They do NOT know the complete structure, Leon's identity, the organization's true purpose, or the total number of recipients.

Conceptual model:

```
                 UNKNOWN SOURCE
                      |
          +-----------+-----------+
          |           |           |
       Person A    Eleanor     Person C
          |           |           |
       unknown     unknown     unknown
       to B/C       to A/C      to A/B
```

Leon may initially be the hidden common connection — PROPOSED only.

Members should NOT sit around a table together.

**Defining principle:** "People connected by a mystery before they are connected to one another."

---

## J. INFORMATION ASYMMETRY (Proposed)

| Party | Knows | Does NOT know |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Eleanor | Received a Token; someone contacted her; sender knows something about her investigation | Leon's identity; full supernatural system; XIII Symbols; Token's full meaning; organization existence; sender's objective |
| Leon | Eleanor may be useful; what he observed; why he selected her | How she will react; whether she remains trustworthy; what she may discover independently; whether other actors watch her |

Keep this asymmetric.

---

## K. RISKS (Proposed)

- Eleanor may refuse, destroy the letter, investigate the sender, or betray trust.
- Leon cannot control her.
- The Order of Severence (Eleanor's existing affiliation) may notice her behavior.
- Other unknown factions may observe her.
- The Token may be lost, stolen, or misunderstood.
- Correspondence may be intercepted (1816 — letters can be read by others).

---

## L. CANON CONSTRAINTS (PROPOSED — must not violate)

- Constable ≠ Order of Severence.
- Leon's primary organization = Constable.
- Crowe and Graves = Order of Severence.
- Eleanor's existing canon affiliation = Order of Severence (The Archivist). This is NOT removed or contradicted by this proposal.
- Leon does not automatically know every hidden organizational relationship.
- Keeper ≠ Holder.
- Sign ≠ Token.
- Rank 1 = Transcendent; Rank 9 = Marked.
- Rank 0 does not currently exist.
- XIII Symbols remain mysterious.
- Do not expose supernatural information prematurely.
- Do NOT make Eleanor automatically part of Constable, Order of Severence, or any existing secret organization — beyond her existing Order affiliation.
- Do NOT use her as a bridge to connect Constable and Order.
- Do NOT imply Leon's actions represent official Constable activity (they are personal/independent).
- The future network must NOT copy the LOTM Tarot Club: no formal gathering room, fixed seats, theatrical aliases, weekly meetings, card-based hierarchy, immediate identity disclosure, or central leader openly recruiting.
