# ORGANIZATIONAL AFFILIATION LEDGER — Alderwick

**Status:** 📗 CANON — authoritative organizational reference
**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Purpose:** Establish the clear organizational structure separating **CONSTABLE** (Leon's organization) from the **ORDER OF SEVERENCE** (a separate secret organization).

---

## Core Canon Decision (locked 2026-09-02)

**CONSTABLE** = Leon's organization / group.
Leon is associated with and operates within the Constable organization (the constabulary / watch network of Alderwick).

**ORDER OF SEVERENCE** = A completely separate secret organization.
The Order of Severence is NOT Leon's organization.
Do not describe the Order of Severence as "Leon's organization."
Do not merge the two organizations.

**Constable ≠ Order of Severence.**

---

## Relationship Model

```
CONSTABLE
    │
    │ Leon belongs / operates here
    │
    ├── Leon Ashcroft
    ├── James Calloway (constable)
    └── Other confirmed Constable members

         ↑
         │  possible infiltration
         │
         ↓

ORDER OF SEVERENCE
    │
    ├── Silas Crowe
    ├── Nathaniel Graves
    └── Other confirmed Order members
```

The Order of Severence may have agents, informants, or infiltrators inside the Constable organization.

**INFILTRATION ≠ MEMBERSHIP.**

A person working inside Constable for the Order retains:
- Primary Affiliation: Order of Severence
- Operational Position: Constable
- Hidden Affiliation: Order of Severence

Do NOT automatically classify such a person as a full member of both organizations.

## Organization Definitions

### CONSTABLE
A legitimate, recognized investigative / law-enforcement-associated organization within Alderwick's supernatural framework — the constabulary and its watch network. Its exact institutional structure remains consistent with existing canon. Leon belongs here. Constable is NOT a secret cult (unless existing canon explicitly establishes otherwise).

### ORDER OF SEVERENCE
A separate secret organization concerned with supernatural phenomena (the boundary, the Agreement, keeper-related matters) and its own agenda. Its relationship with Constable is NOT inherently allied, hostile, subordinate, or identical. The relationship must remain nuanced unless canon establishes otherwise. Potential infiltration is allowed.

---

## Narrative Rule

The distinction must remain clear:
- **Leon:** CONSTABLE → his organization.
- **Graves:** ORDER OF SEVERENCE → his organization.
- **Crowe:** Order-adjacent. He denies membership on the page; treat him as informed by and present at the Order, never as its officer.
- **Shaw:** unaffiliated. Taken for the Order's archivist; not one.

If Crowe interacts with Constable, interaction does NOT equal membership. If an Order agent is inside Constable, that person is an infiltrator/agent, not automatically a normal Constable member. This distinction must be preserved throughout future arcs.

## Terminology Caution (added 2026-09-03)

**"The Constable" as a capitalised organisation name is a World-Bible construct. It appears nowhere in Chapters 1–141.**

The manuscript's institutional vocabulary is lower-case and period-plausible: *the constable*, *the constabulary*, *the watch*, *the round*, *the watch-house*, *the bench*, *the vestry*, *the office of constable*. The only capitalised uses in the manuscript are vocative or titular ("Constable Calloway", "Constable.").

Consequence for future writing: **do not introduce "the Constable" as a spoken proper noun into prose.** Use it only in World Bible documents, as this ledger does. Characters say "the constable", "the office", "the round", "the bench".

See `../17_ORGANIZATIONS/05_GOVERNMENT.md` for the institutional architecture and `../04_CHARACTERS/06_CONSTABLE_TEAM_ARCHITECTURE.md` for personnel.
