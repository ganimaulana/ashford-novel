# ORGANIZATIONAL AFFILIATION LEDGER — ASHFORD

**Status:** 📗 CANON — authoritative organizational reference
**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Purpose:** Establish the clear organizational structure separating **CONSTABLE** (Leon's organization) from the **ORDER OF SEVERENCE** (a separate secret organization).

---

## Core Canon Decision (locked 2026-09-02)

**CONSTABLE** = Leon's organization / group.
Leon is associated with and operates within the Constable organization (the constabulary / watch network of Ashford).

**ORDER OF SEVERENCE** = A completely separate secret organization.
The Order of Severence is NOT Leon's organization.
Do not describe the Order of Severence as "Leon's organization."
Do not merge the two organizations.

**Constable ≠ Order of Severence.**

Do NOT automatically classify such a person as a full member of both organizations.

## Organization Definitions

### CONSTABLE
A legitimate, recognized investigative / law-enforcement-associated organization within Ashford's supernatural framework — the constabulary and its watch network. Its exact institutional structure remains consistent with existing canon. Leon belongs here. Constable is NOT a secret cult (unless existing canon explicitly establishes otherwise).

### ORDER OF SEVERENCE
A separate secret organization concerned with supernatural phenomena (the boundary, the Agreement, keeper-related matters) and its own agenda. Its relationship with Constable is NOT inherently allied, hostile, subordinate, or identical. The relationship must remain nuanced unless canon establishes otherwise. Potential infiltration is allowed.

---

## Narrative Rule

The distinction must remain clear:
- **Leon:** CONSTABLE → his organization.
- **Crowe:** ORDER OF SEVERENCE → his organization.

If Crowe interacts with Constable, interaction does NOT equal membership. If an Order agent is inside Constable, that person is an infiltrator/agent, not automatically a normal Constable member. This distinction must be preserved throughout future arcs.
