# Secret Organization — The Hidden Circle (Working Title)

**Status:** 📘 WORKING CANON
**Version:** 2.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-28

---

## NATURE

- **Age:** Old — predates the current century by generations, possibly centuries
- **Nature:** Independent network of individuals connected by knowledge, bloodlines, or shared purpose
- **Not a formal institution** — no headquarters, no membership cards, no uniform structure
- **Not a single monolith** — has factions, conflicts, disagreements about purpose

---

## PURPOSE

- **Unknown to most members.** Even senior members disagree about what the organization ultimately seeks.
- Some believe it preserves knowledge of the supernatural
- Some believe it controls or contains supernatural entities
- Some believe it seeks power or advantage
- Some believe it serves a purpose none of them fully understand

**This is intentional.** The organization has evolved beyond any single founder's intent. It has become its own entity.

---

## MEMBERSHIP

### Who Joins
- **Descendants** of old families (Ashcroft, and others)
- **Talented outsiders** selected for unusual abilities or knowledge
- **People with unusual bloodlines** — supernatural heritage they may not know about
- **Powerful individuals** — scholars, wealthy patrons, people with influence
- **People selected because of potential** — young people identified before they understand what they are

### How One Joins
- **Invitation** — through a trusted contact (not cold recruitment)
- **Gradual introduction** — not sudden; people learn slowly
- **Testing** — not formal, but implicit; the organization observes
- **Voluntary** — no one is forced; members choose to stay (or leave, with consequences)

---

## STRUCTURE (OR LACK THEREOF)

### Important: This is NOT a perfect organization

- It has **senior members** and **junior members**
- It has **factions** — groups with different views on purpose and methods
- It has **conflicts** — members who disagree, even hate each other
- It has **failures** — members who disappeared, betrayed, or were eliminated
- It has **weak members** — not everyone is powerful
- It has **extremely strong members** — but they are the exception
- Some members do not even know each other
- Some members do not know the full scope of what the organization does

### Communication Methods (1816-era)
- **Correspondence network** — letters sent through trusted couriers
- **Dead drops** — messages left in specific locations
- **Symbol systems** — marks, symbols, coded language in letters
- **Innkeeper network** — trusted innkeepers who relay messages
- **Personal meetings** — the primary method; face-to-face
- **No instant communication** — a letter from London takes days; a message from Ashford to London takes the same time
- **Some members have faster channels** — but these are special, not universal

### Membership Tiers (Loose, Not Official) — Aligned with Leon Layer System

1. **Observers** — people who know of the organization but are not members (e.g., Daniel, Margaret — they don't even know the organization exists)
2. **Correspondents** — people who exchange information but have limited involvement (e.g., Arthur — partial knowledge only)
3. **Participants** — active members who attend meetings, follow directives (Leon at Layer 3)
4. **Inner Circle** — senior members with significant knowledge and influence (know Leon at Layer 3, partial Layer 4)
5. **Founders' Legacy** — people who knew the original members; possibly living or dead (know Layer 4, Aurelius's true nature)

**Critical:** Daniel and Margaret are PERMANENT OBSERVERS in the sense that they will never advance beyond Layer 1. Arthur is a permanent CORRESPONDENT — he will never become a full Participant. The layer system prevents accidental information leaks.

## LEON'S ENTRANCE — LAYER SYSTEM

### Leon operates on FOUR COMPARTMENTALIZED LAYERS

See `LEON_LAYER_ARCHITECTURE.md` for the complete specification.

**Summary:**
1. **Layer 1 — Ordinary Citizen:** Public persona. Known to everyone, including Daniel and Margaret.
2. **Layer 2 — Paranormal Investigator:** Surface hidden activity. Known only to Arthur (partial).
3. **Layer 3 — Leon Ashcroft in the Secret Organization:** Hidden identity. Known to Circle members, unknown to surface contacts.
4. **Layer 4 — Aurelius Ashcroft:** Full legacy. Known only to the oldest Inner Circle members.

### Critical Rules

**Daniel and Margaret are PERMANENT SURFACE CONTACTS.**
- They know only Layer 1.
- They will never be initiated.
- Their ignorance is their protection.
- If they discover Layer 2, they become liabilities.

**Arthur is a BRIDGE CHARACTER.**
- He knows Layer 1 and partial Layer 2.
- He knows the Circle exists but not its structure.
- He will NOT introduce Leon to the Circle.
- He will NOT reveal Circle secrets to Daniel or Margaret.

**Circle Members know only up to Layer 3.**
- They know Leon is an Ashcroft member.
- They do NOT know he is being positioned as Aurelius's successor.
- They do NOT know Layer 4 details.

### How Leon Enters (Layer 2 → Layer 3)

1. **He is misidentified** — someone assumes he is connected to Aurelius
2. **He is observed** — the organization notices the letter's arrival and Leon's investigation
3. **He is tested** — through cases, challenges, and observations
4. **He is invited** — not as a member, but as a potential asset
5. **He discovers** — through his own investigation, he stumbles into contact with members

### Leon's Advantage at Each Layer

- **Layer 1:** Looks like an ordinary researcher. No one suspects him.
- **Layer 2:** His observational skills make him dangerous. The Circle notices.
- **Layer 3:** Members overestimate him because his conclusions are too accurate. He uses their assumptions to gather information.
- **Layer 4:** He is the heir. He must decide what to do with the legacy.

### Layer Transition Triggers

- **1→2:** Leon finds the symbol in Arthur's book and decides to investigate (Ch 2)
- **2→3:** Leon is contacted by Circle members, tested, and accepted (Ch 9+)
- **3→4:** Leon accesses Bellweather basement, reads Aurelius's journals, accepts the legacy (Endgame)

### Compartmentalization Rules

1. **Physical Separation:** No Circle documents in Layer 1 locations.
2. **Temporal Separation:** Hidden activities happen during "research" time slots.
3. **Social Separation:** Layer 2/3/4 contacts never meet Layer 1 contacts.
4. **Information Separation:** Layer 2/3/4 information never shared with Layer 1 contacts.
5. **Emotional Separation:** Leon's emotional investment differs by layer.

## POWER SYSTEM — PRINCIPLES ONLY

- Power exists
- Not everyone has it
- Not everyone knows about it
- Abilities vary by bloodline, training, artifacts, supernatural heritage
- Leon is NOT strong at the start
- Leon's progression: Observation > Deduction > Survival > Knowledge > Technique > Power

## GUIDELINES FOR WRITING ORGANIZATION SCENES

### DO:
- Make the organization feel lived-in and imperfect
- Show factional disagreements
- Include members who are uncomfortable, weak, or uncertain
- Use 1816-era communication (letters, couriers, in-person)
- Keep the organization mysterious — members disagree about its purpose
- Show the weight of history — this organization is OLD

### DON'T:
- Make the organization a monolith with clear rules
- Reveal the full scope too early
- Make all members powerful or competent
- Introduce organization members before Ch 9
- Make Leon instantly understand what's happening
- Explain the organization's true purpose
