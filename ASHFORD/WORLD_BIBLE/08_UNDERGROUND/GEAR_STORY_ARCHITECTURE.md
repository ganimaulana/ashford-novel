## 1. CORE PRINCIPLE (restated)

GEAR IS NOT ERC. Gear = specialized Artifact. An ERC record may document a Gear; the Gear never becomes an ERC. Gear ≠ Token. Token remains its own system (PROPOSED common ancient origin: UNRESOLVED).

## 2. THE ENGINE LOOP

```
DISCOVERY → RECOGNITION → INTERPRETATION → ACQUISITION → EXPERIMENTATION
→ DOOR INTERACTION → NEW CAPABILITY → NEW CLUE → NEW LOCATION → NEW PERSON
→ NEW CONFLICT → DEEPER MYSTERY
```

Neither Leon nor the reader initially knows: how many Gear exist, who created them, why they were separated, why the Door exists, why the ancient organization disappeared, what completion causes.

## 3. GEAR COUNT

**NINE = PROPOSED, NOT CONFIRMED.** Compatibility check: canon establishes one confirmed gear + one Vale-box gear; it never states a total. Nine is compatible IF the confirmed gear is one of the nine (candidate: the first / recognition position) — UNRESOLVED. If author rejects nine, the architecture degrades gracefully to any count (positions are functional, not numbered).

## 4. DOOR PROGRESSION (PROPOSED — design framework)

| Stage | Effect | Mystery consequence |
|-------|--------|---------------------|
| INITIAL | Door exists, inactive/inaccessible | What is it? |
| FIRST GEAR | Limited interaction — recognition; the Door answers presence (candidate: confirmed keeper's gear) | It is not stone |
| SECOND | One-way auditory transmission | Something hears |
| THIRD | Two-way communication | Something answers |
| FOURTH | Limited visual perception | Something is there |
| FIFTH | Deeper perception / environmental sensing | The other side has a shape |
| SIXTH | Limited interaction with something beyond | It can be touched — at cost |
| SEVENTH | Partial crossing / controlled access | Crossing changes the crosser |
| EIGHTH | Persistent/deeper access — revelation | The Door was never only a door |
| NINTH | UNKNOWN — deliberately unresolved | Revision of all prior assumptions |

Rules: NOT "+1 ability" ladder. Every activation must change Leon's understanding of the Door, carry cost/limitation/risk, and generate a new question. The Door must never become a puzzle: collect-X-open-door is forbidden.

## 5. VISUAL LANGUAGE

Shared subtle design language: a faint recurring maker's motif (candidate: echoes of the first sign — PROPOSED), comparable metalworking "grammar", inscriptions in an unidentified hand. Each Gear differs in silhouette, material, coloration, markings, wear, geometry. The common origin should be recognizable only gradually.

Forbidden: modern machinery, sci-fi tech, magical crystals, videogame artifacts, unjustified glow, in-fiction labels (GEAR A/B/1/2 — internal identifiers only), obvious numbered markings.

## 6. CRYPTIC INSCRIPTIONS — THREE-LAYER RULE

Every Gear bears writing with at least three interpretive layers:
- LAYER 1: what an ordinary observer believes (philosophy, doctrine, memorial text)
- LAYER 2: what Leon suspects (a clue to function)
- LAYER 3: what it actually means within the ancient-organization mystery

Inscriptions may reference: absence, thresholds, memory, witnesses, separation, return, names, something that no longer exists. Never "THIS OPENS THE DOOR." Layers are revealed at different times; inscriptions reward re-reading.

## 7. DISCOVERY EXPERIENCE (STORY EVENT RULE)

Every discovery must render: location, reason it was there, appearance, what made it unusual, Leon's initial misreading, the revealing clue, the acquisition obstacle, Leon's reaction, the new question created.

Acquisition mechanisms (use with variety, never uniformly): auction · antiquarian shop · private collection · abandoned building · old estate · archaeological material · inheritance · another investigator · criminal collection · faction-controlled artifact · accidental discovery · trade · negotiation · debt · favor · an unknowing ordinary owner.

## 8. GEOGRAPHIC EXPANSION ENGINE

Gear may be located beyond Ashford: other cities, scholars' hands, hidden inside other objects, auction lots, faction vaults, unknowing owners, old buildings, sites near other phenomena. Travel rule: Leon travels because the investigation demands it, never because a new setting is wanted. Each destination must independently sustain: new characters, local history, local mystery, faction texture, potential member candidates. Loop:

```
Gear lead → new location → local story → acquisition → return → Door experiment
→ new clue → next destination
```

## 9. ORGANIZATION MEMBER INVOLVEMENT (PROPOSED)

Leon does NOT personally acquire every Gear. Once the anonymous network develops, members may investigate/acquire under partial information. Consequences designed for: member agency, fragmented information, mistakes, divergent interpretation, trust stakes, meaningful transactions, betrayal potential. Members are never disposable assistants; each needs independent motivation. Leon must have a reason (possibly incomplete/wrong) for assigning a lead. Leon is not omniscient.

## 10. GEAR-FINDING ARTIFACT — "THE PALE COMPASS" (PROPOSED)

An Artifact — NOT Gear, NOT Token, NOT ERC. Name PROPOSED only. Provides directional/proximity information concerning Gear, imperfectly:

Limitations: distance ceiling · interference near strong thresholds (candidate: near the chamber) · false positives (adjacent-era relics) · inability to distinguish between Gear · temporary readings · environmental distortion · readings that clarify only under specific conditions (candidate: only when a Token is present — UNRESOLVED).

Acquisition scenario (PROPOSED): an ordinary person/member obtains it unknowing; Leon recognizes significance; a meaningful transaction follows (offer of another artifact / information / favor / purchase concealing purpose / arranged unrelated trade / solving the owner's problem / allowing a harmless false belief). Principle: the owner does not fully understand; Leon does — but Leon's understanding is still incomplete. Final mechanism undecided.

## 11. FINAL ASSEMBLY — FIVE POSSIBILITIES (all PROPOSED; none confirmed)

**A. THE DOOR REMEMBERS.** Completion does not open the Door — it completes the account: the system records/reveals what the keepers' count was always tallying. Advantages: honors "not opened but remembered"; recontextualizes Arc 1. Disadvantages: risks feeling passive. Mystery potential: high. Canon compatibility: strong (memory motif). Impact on Leon: he becomes witness, not opener.

**B. THE DOOR OPENS BOTH WAYS.** Completion makes the threshold traversable — and something on the far side has been waiting for exactly this. Advantages: escalation, danger, Arc-3 engine. Disadvantages: risks conventional "opened door" payoff. Mystery potential: high. Canon compatibility: moderate ("opens from both sides" is canon). Impact: Leon must decide whether completion was a mistake.

**C. THE GEAR WERE A SEAL, NOT A KEY.** Assembly does not unlock — it re-locks something that has been loosening since the organization's failure. The ancient organization separated them deliberately because keeping them apart WAS the safeguard. Advantages: inverts the quest; separation becomes sacred. Disadvantages: must be seeded early to avoid feeling like a trick. Mystery potential: very high. Canon compatibility: strong (separateness/keeping theme). Impact: Leon's entire collection arc becomes a moral problem.

**D. THE SUMMONING COUNT.** Completion synchronizes with the count of keepers and 00:00 — the Gear system and the tally were two halves of one mechanism. The bell's call and the Door's opening are one event. Advantages: unifies confirmed mysteries (count, bell, 00:00, Door). Disadvantages: risks over-resolution; must leave residue. Mystery potential: high. Canon compatibility: strong (all elements already canon). Impact: Leon realizes the keepers were maintaining a countdown he has been accelerating.

**E. LEON DOES NOT KNOW — THE RECOMMENDED CORE.** Whatever the mechanism, Leon assembles the system because the evidence leads him there, not because he knows the reward. The outcome is something he did not expect. Advantages: anti-videogame; preserves agency-through-investigation; any of A–D can be the hidden truth. Disadvantages: requires disciplined withholding. Mystery potential: maximum. Canon compatibility: strong. Impact: defines Leon's character — he completes what he cannot predict.

**Design recommendation:** E as narrative posture; C and D as the strongest hidden-truth candidates; A as tonal fallback. Author decision REQUIRED — do not preselect.

## 12. WHY WERE THE GEAR SEPARATED? (all PROPOSED)

Deliberate separation · emergency dispersal · destruction of the original organization · protection against misuse · failure of the Door · internal conflict · betrayal · ritual failure · deliberate concealment · the Gear themselves became dangerous. NONE confirmed. Seed multiple; let evidence conflict.

## 13. ANCIENT ORGANIZATION MYSTERY

Gear = strongest clue-path toward the vanished organization. Factions hold fragments only: the Order of Severence may classify Gear as boundary relics without knowing the nine (their exact knowledge remains UNRESOLVED — do not grant completeness); the nameless society preserves histories/objects (confirmed interest in the tally — a potential bridge, UNRESOLVED); others may own Gear unknowingly. No faction is wholly incompetent; the mystery emerges from fragmented information. The organization's fate remains UNKNOWN (hypotheses A–G in `DOOR_GEAR_SYSTEM.md` §XXX preserved).

## 14. LEON KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY (per-Gear template, enforced)

For every Gear document: WHAT LEON KNOWS · WHAT LEON SUSPECTS · WHAT LEON DOES NOT KNOW · WHAT LEON BELIEVES INCORRECTLY · WHAT THE READER MAY SUSPECT · WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE. Leon solves by intelligence/deduction; some truths remain beyond him. Accidental omniscience is a defect.

**Arc 2 opening state (canon-consistent):** Leon no longer holds the keeper's gear (passed to Daniel, Ch 129). Leon carries Tokens and the journey's knowledge. Any Gear quest must account for Daniel's possession of the confirmed gear — a built-in complication, not an error.

## 15. INFORMATION REVEAL STRUCTURE

EARLY: Gear appears mysterious → MIDDLE: connected to the Door → LATER: part of a larger system → LATER: system tied to an ancient organization → LATER: the organization discovered something → LATE: the Gear may not have been designed merely to open the Door → FINAL: true purpose substantially clearer (never fully, where canon reserves it). Layered foreshadowing only.

## 16. NO POWER CREEP

Gear provides ACCESS / INFORMATION / INTERACTION — never raw power that invalidates Rank, XIII Signs, Tokens, or established limitations. Any strong capability requires: cost, limitation, risk, uncertainty, consequence.

## 17. READER SATISFACTION RULE

Every Gear encounter grants a discovery reward before full understanding: memorable design, cryptic text, historical clue, unusual behavior, connection to a prior mystery, relationship to another Gear, character/location revelation, a new question, or partial answer to an old one. The reader must feel "that object matters" without knowing exactly why.
