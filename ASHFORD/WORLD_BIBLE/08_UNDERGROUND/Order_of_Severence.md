## Organization Overview

**Name:** Order of Severence
**Type:** Secret medical/research organization
**Origin:** Investigations into Residual Power in biological tissue belonging to deceased Power Users

### Historical Nature
A secret medical/research organization that originated from investigations into Residual Power in biological tissue belonging to deceased Power Users.

### Original Objective
Reduce the inequality and oppression caused by Power being concentrated among Power Users by developing a method through which ordinary humans could gain access to Power.

### Historical Progression
medical research → Residual Power discovery → ethical transplantation → liberation movement → political radicalization → revolutionary activity → extremist practices → ideological fragmentation

The organization is NOT inherently evil from its foundation. Its original motivation is rooted in:
- Segregation
- Exploitation
- Oppression
- Unequal treatment
- Abuse of Power Users
- Unequal access to social power

Its later methods become increasingly morally questionable.

## Internal Ideological Currents

These are ideological currents, camps, circles, or tendencies within the organization. They are not necessarily formal factions unless later canon establishes them as such.

### Reformists
**Goal:** Controlled and ethical transplantation under strict consent, medical standards, and safeguards.

### Liberators
**Goal:** Use transplantation to free ordinary humans and oppressed groups from systems dominated by Power Users.

### Revolutionaries
**Goal:** Destroy political and social structures that allow Power Users to maintain systemic privilege.

### Extremists
**Goal:** Eliminate Power inequality regardless of individual consent when they believe it is necessary.

### Purists
**Goal:** Preserve the original principles and limits of Severence. They oppose uncontrolled experimentation and believe excessive transplantation corrupts the human body and original mission.

### Pragmatists
**Goal:** Preserve the organization's survival and effectiveness. They are willing to cooperate with governments, Power Users, criminals, other organizations, or enemies if useful.

## Relationship with Leon's Organization

[CANDIDATE] — Do not define Order of Severence as automatically allied with or hostile toward Leon's organization. Their relationship should remain nuanced.

Possible areas:
- Ideological disagreement
- Temporary cooperation
- Intelligence exchange
- Protection of dissidents
- Infiltration
- Manipulation
- Disagreement over bodily autonomy
- Disagreement over Power transplantation

Rose Whitmore can serve as a potential narrative bridge.

Leon may recognize that Order of Severence originated from legitimate oppression while rejecting its increasingly exploitative methods.

Do not turn this into a simplistic hero-villain relationship.

## Power System Notes

Residual Power remains rare, difficult, medically dangerous, and partially understood unless existing canon establishes otherwise.

Do not establish that:
- Every Power can be transplanted
- Every transplant succeeds
- Every transplant transfers memories
- Every transplant transfers personality
- Every donor has identical biological characteristics
- Elias fully understands the phenomenon
- Every failure is fatal

Where phenomena are unexplained, label them as [UNRESOLVED] or [CANDIDATE].
