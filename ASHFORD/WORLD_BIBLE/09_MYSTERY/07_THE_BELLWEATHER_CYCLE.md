# The Bellweather Cycle — Phenomenon Design (PROPOSAL)

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED in Ch7–Ch8 (2026-08-31). Canon-consistent.
**Inconsistencies 1–5 below have been reconciled.**
**Scope:** The recurring supernatural phenomenon behind the "logical" intrusions Leon logs in Ch2–8, and how Constable James Calloway investigates it.

---

## 1. Summary

A recurring supernatural phenomenon centered on Bellweather Lane / No. 13, anchored to the boy in Helen's portrait (the Aurelius Ashcroft line) and to Leon's recurring dream. It begins the night of **13 November 1816** and follows a **cyclic pattern** documented in the Alderwick constabulary archives.

The small, logically-explainable events Leon records (back-door latch tried, vanishing footprints, window silhouette, bolt disturbed) are the phenomenon's *early surface* — Leon rationalizes them until the constable reads them otherwise.

---

## 2. Onset

- Begins **night of 13 November 1816** — the date on the portrait inscription: *"He would return on 13 November 1816."*
- First manifestations are the Ch2–Ch7 intrusions Leon logs. They are real disturbances, not imagination, but each is individually explainable; only in aggregate (and to a sensitive reader) do they read as one phenomenon.

---

## 3. Anchors to established elements

- **Helen's portrait** — her changed expression when viewing it (Ch1) is the first human "reading" of the phenomenon's source. The phenomenon is anchored to this boy / the Ashcroft line.
- **Leon's recurring dream** (Ch1: boy in dark coat, *"Don't be late again"*) recurs on a fixed date within the cycle; the dream is the phenomenon touching Leon directly.
- **The empty house at the end of Bellweather Lane (Bellweather House)** is the phenomenon's focus.

---

## 4. The Constable thread (James Calloway)

1. **Daniel notices a change** in Leon. Leon asks — without explaining details — where one reports if something is threatening them. Daniel advises the nearest constable.
2. **Leon reports** to Constable James Calloway. At the office, Calloway does **NOT** explain the supernatural; he deduces from Leon's log and concludes he must examine the place.
3. **House visit.** Calloway possesses a latent ability to sense Alderwick's old supernatural forces; at No. 13 / Bellweather Lane he confirms a presence.
4. **Archive search.** Unable to resolve it, Calloway searches the constabulary records and finds the phenomenon occurred on dates **X–Y** in past years, after which it ceases and life returns to normal — a cycle.

---

## 5. Cyclic dates (IMPLEMENTED)

- Onset 1816: **13 November** (night) — matches the portrait inscription "He would return on 13 November 1816" and Leon's first logged disturbance (Ch2).
- **Active window: 13–21 November**, recurring year after year (per constabulary archives Calloway finds).
- **Resolution:** after the 21st the phenomenon ceases and Alderwick returns to normal; no one is harmed who waits it out and does not go seeking it.
- Leon's dream recurs within the window, strongest on the 13th.
- Note: the Aurelius Ashcroft death/disappearance timeline ("a few decades ago," Ch7) remains the anchor for *why* the cycle exists, but the window dates are now fixed independent of that open question — no loophole.
