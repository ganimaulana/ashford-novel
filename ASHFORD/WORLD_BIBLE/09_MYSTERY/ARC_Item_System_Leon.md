## 3. ARC Item behavior (observed, not rules)

- **Sounding device (small bell):** inert **inside** the sealed house. Once taken **outside**, it is active **every day**. It gives a faint sounding when an anomaly is present/imminent within its radius (~3 km). Near the sealed house, anomalies are *rare* (the house's seal suppresses them), so it seldom sounds — which is why Leon initially takes it for a dead trinket. It sounded now **by coincidence**: the 13–21 Nov cycle has an active anomaly in the area. If the item is removed from the house and carried out, it can be active and used again the next day.
- **Compass:** also primes only **outside** the house. When primed it abandons north and settles on the **bearing of the active anomaly's epicenter**; one bearing per priming, then inert until next priming (still usable daily once outside).
- **Negative effects (logged by Leon):** chills, fatigue, brief blurring of short-term memory after handling; stronger for "heavier" items. This is why he relies on *light* items and why the dangerous bunker items are left sealed.
- **Levels:** Light (safe to handle: the bell, the compass) → Moderate → Dangerous (sealed in the **bunker** beneath No. 13, unknown to Leon). Risk tiers mirror `ARC_CLASSIFICATION` risk levels.

## 6. Leon's layer stack (LoTM reference — revised 2026-08-31)

Modeled on *Lord of the Mysteries*, where the protagonist holds parallel identities (civilian → official agency → deep-cover in a rival order → faction-uniting club). Leon's stack:

- **Layer 1 — Ordinary civilian (the "public" Leon):** what **Daniel and Margaret** see — the new writer/resident of Bellweather Lane. No secret knowledge. (Master outline already mandates Daniel & Margaret stay Layer 1 only; Arthur = Layer 1+2 bridge.)
- **Layer 2 — Core secret society (Leon's home org):** a secret society that recruits across the establishments — **military, church, etc.** (fits 1816: Napoleonic veterans, St. Bartholomew's, gentry). This is the *large, faction-connecting* body; Leon is its **Heir / weakest member**; the **ARC items** originate here. Entry = **irregular / freelance** (recruited by Calloway to resolve anomalous phenomena); promotion to **permanent** → assigned to **infiltrate The Severance**.
  - LoTM parallel: the Church / official Beyonder order + the "Tarot Club" role (connecting factions).
- **Layer 3 — Infiltration: The Severance:** Leon is placed inside **The Severance** as a **spy (disusupi)** by Layer 2. The Severance is a *separate* secret organization — the **target**, not Leon's own. He reports up to Layer 2.
  - LoTM parallel: deep-cover inside a rival order (e.g., Aurora Order) under an alias.
- **Layer 4+ — further infiltrations:** as needed (e.g., the criminal underworld, other factions reached via The Severance's sphere), the stack can grow past **5 layers**. Leon can hold multiple covers.
