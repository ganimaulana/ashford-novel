## MARGARET'S TEAROOM & COFFEE SHOP
**Location:** Market Square / High Street area, near town center
**Owner:** Margaret Eleanor Whitmore
**Period:** Small building; sign above door reads "TEAROOM & COFFEE SHOP" (old lettering from previous generation)
**Description:** Small establishment with six or seven tables; warm interior; row of small tables; counter at back; front windows; modest but welcoming
**Atmosphere:** Warm and friendly; community hub where people know each other and exchange news; smell of tea, coffee, baked goods
**Key feature:** Social center where Margaret knows everyone's business; gathering place for locals, students, workers

---

## BELL'S ANTIQUIARIAN SHOP (Arthur's)
**Location:** High Street
**Owner:** Arthur Edmund Bell
**Description:** Two stories; cluttered ground floor; narrow staircase to upper storage; window display of curios; smell of old paper, dust, wood polish; shelves floor to ceiling; narrow aisles
**Arthur's role:** Antiquarian knowledge; connected to local families through artifacts
**Key feature:** Private collection upstairs — items not shown to casual visitors
**Atmosphere:** Overstuffed, cozy, slightly claustrophobic; time slows inside

---

## BELL'S BOOKSHOP
**Location:** High Street
**Description:** Two stories; better-lit and organized; new books and academic texts; some secondhand; window display of recent publications; reading area
**Key feature:** Subscription library connection; academic texts not available elsewhere

## Alderwick ACADEMY
**Location:** Academy Quarter
**Description:** Large building; classrooms ground floor; dormitories and study rooms above; library; common room; courtyard; garden
**Students:** Boarding and day students, ages ~12-18
**Staff:** Headmaster, teachers (Daniel James Carter), servants
**Leon's role:** Student; assigned room and class
**Key feature:** Academy library and records separate from public library

## ST. BARTHOLOMEW'S CHURCH
**Location:** Market Square
**Description:** Stone construction; tall spire visible from most of town; graveyard surrounds building; family monuments; old stained glass; worn pews
**Vicar:** [Name TBD] — keeper of parish records
**Records:** Baptism, marriage, burial — primary vital records in 1816 England
**Archives:** Old documents, wills, parish orders, old maps

## VICARAGE
**Location:** Adjacent to Church
**Description:** Two-story house; garden; study filled with books
**Key feature:** Vicar's personal collection — genealogies, local history, family records

## Alderwick POST OFFICE
**Location:** Market Square or High Street (possibly inside a shop)
**Description:** Small room or counter; mail collected and distributed; diligence brings mail from London
**Key feature:** Leon's Pembroke & Clarke letter arrives here. The postmaster may note that a letter to "No. 13, Bellweather Lane" is unusual.

---

## SOLICITOR'S OFFICE
**Location:** Old Town
**Description:** Office ground floor; records room above; desk, files, ledgers, safe
**Documents:** Wills, property deeds, contracts, estate records, court documents
**Key feature:** Leon finds Ashcroft property records, Pembroke & Clarke will, documents related to No. 13
**Messen:** Professional, precise; gatekeepers of property and legal history

---

## NO. 13, BELLWEATHER LANE (Leon's Residence)
**Location:** Bellweather Lane
**Description:** 2-3 stories; modest; inherited; slightly neglected; not grand but not dilapidated; rooms with fireplaces; some furniture from previous occupants; feels lived-in but unused
**Previous occupant:** Unclear; records suggest Ashcroft connection, last known resident unknown
**Key feature:** Traces of history — architectural details, objects, possibly locked room or hidden space
**Atmosphere:** Quiet; slightly isolated; feels older than rest of lane; Leon notices things others don't

---

## BELLWEATHER HOUSE
**Location:** Bellweather Lane (near No. 13)
**Description:** Larger than No. 13; older; possibly modified over centuries; partially abandoned or repurposed; more historical artifacts
**Connection:** Shares history with No. 13 — same original property later divided, or one was main house and one was outbuilding
