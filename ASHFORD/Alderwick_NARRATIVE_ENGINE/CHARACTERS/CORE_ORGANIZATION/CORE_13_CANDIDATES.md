# CORE ORGANIZATION -- 13 PROPOSED CANDIDATES

**Status:** ALL PROPOSED -- NONE ARE CANON
**Created:** 2026-08-29
**Canon Reference:** Alderwick Chapters 001-014 (1816)
**Purpose:** Design pool for a potential CORE ORGANIZATION (successor network to Aurelius's Hidden Circle)

---

## DESIGN NOTES

These 13 candidates represent a **proposed living network** -- people connected to, inherited from, or transformed by Aurelius Ashcroft's original Hidden Circle. They are NOT Aurelius's original 1792 circle (that was M.R., J.T., Thomas Wren, and Aurelius himself).

The organization, if approved, would be the **surviving structure** that outlived Aurelius -- some members continuing his work, others sabotaging it, others unaware they were part of it.

# CORE PILLAR 1 -- THE ARCHIVIST

### candidate_id: CORE-ORG-01
### proposed_name: Silas Hawthorne
### age_range: 58-65
### gender: Male
### occupation/social_role: Parish clerk / unofficial keeper of town records
### organization_role: **The Archivist** -- Maintains physical records, copies, and hidden documents that keep the organization's knowledge alive after Thomas Wren's death. Controls access to parish records, property deeds, and copied documents.

### public_persona
An unremarkable elderly man who spends his days at St. Bartholomew's. To most of Alderwick, he is the quiet parish clerk who files documents and answers routine inquiries. He speaks little, looks down often, and is easily overlooked. He is the kind of man people forget he was in the room.

### private_self
Possesses an almost obsessive memory for documents. Remembers every entry he has ever processed, every name, every date, every discrepancy. Kept a private copy of everything Aurelius ever gave Thomas Wren. Believes truth belongs in records, not in people. Has spent decades making sure the truth survives even if the people die. Not loyal to Aurelius -- loyal to accuracy. If Aurelius was wrong, Silas will record that too.

### primary_goal
To preserve every document related to Bellweather House and the Ashcroft network in a hidden secondary archive (not in the basement -- a different location). Believes Thomas Wren's notebook is incomplete and that the full story requires paper, not memory.

### fear
That documents will be destroyed, burned, or confiscated. Has seen one generation's "secret" become the next generation's "proof of scandal."

### values
Accuracy over loyalty. Documentation over memory. Truth over protection.

### contradiction
Preserves the truth but believes some truths should never be published. A guardian of records who actively withholds from public access.

### strengths
- Institutional knowledge of every record in Alderwick since 1750
- Can forge a document convincingly (to protect the archive)
- Patient to the point of invisibility
- Understands property law better than any solicitor in town
- Has physical access to parish and town records

### weaknesses
- Physically frail; cannot travel far or fast
- Socially invisible -- people talk freely around him, but he has no allies
- Obsessive about records over human lives
- When pressed, becomes rigid and unyielding
- His age means he has limited time

### secret
Has created three separate copies of Thomas Wren's notebook, stored in three different locations. One in the parish. One buried beneath the floorboards of a derelict cottage. One sent to London to a solicitor who never acknowledged receipt. Does not know if the London copy was received.

### knowledge_profile
**Knows:** Every property transaction in Alderwick since 1750. Full contents of Thomas Wren's notebook (from before the deathbed handover). Identities of M.R. and J.T. (recorded their signatures on property deeds). Layout of Bellweather House (from building permits).
**Suspects:** The London solicitor who received a copy was compromised -- he sent it through a channel that no longer exists.
**Does not know:** What is in the basement. Whether Leon knows about his archive. What became of Eleanor Vane after Aurelius's death.

### relationship_hooks
- Could serve as the living library -- the person Leon must consult when documents run dry
- Could conflict with Daniel (academic/public history vs. Silas's hidden history)

# CORE PILLAR 2 -- THE ENFORCER

### candidate_id: CORE-ORG-02
### proposed_name: Nathaniel Cross
### age_range: 35-42
### gender: Male
### occupation/social_role: Property solicitor / land agent (operates independently, not through Pembroke & Clarke)
### organization_role: **The Enforcer** -- Handles the practical, sometimes ruthless, side of the organization's operations. Evictions, property transfers, pressure on witnesses, management of the physical network of houses and safe locations. Does the work Aurelius would have done with his hands, not his documents.

### public_persona
A sharp, efficient solicitor who runs a small but respectable practice in Alderwick. Polished, direct, and unapologetically pragmatic. He charges fair prices, meets deadlines, and never loses a case in property disputes. People find him slightly intimidating but always leave his office with their problems solved. He is not rude, but he is not warm.

### private_self
Deeply believes that order is more important than justice. Sees the world as fundamentally competitive and believes that those who cannot secure their own position deserve what they get. Served as Aurelius's operational handler for 15 years, managing the practical mechanics of the property network. Did not know the full purpose of the network -- he managed the buildings, not the beliefs. Became fiercely loyal to whoever inherited Aurelius's authority. Capable of cruelty when he believes it serves order.

### primary_goal
To maintain the structural integrity of the property network Aurelius built. Does not care what the network is for -- cares that it exists. If Leon claims inheritance, Nathaniel will test him rigorously. If Leon passes, Nathaniel becomes his most capable -- and most dangerous -- ally.

### fear
Chaos. Disorganization. A world without structure. Has seen what happens when networks collapse, and he does not want it to happen again.

### values
Order, loyalty, competence, discretion. Believes in results over sentiment.

### contradiction
Claims to value justice but consistently chooses order over fairness. Will protect an innocent person's property but evict a guilty one without hesitation. His "protection" is not kindness -- it is investment.

### strengths
- Legal expertise unmatched in the region
- Physical presence and willingness to use intimidation when necessary
- Connections to solicitors and officials in London
- Understands property law, inheritance law, and conveyancing at a master level
- Capable of acting as Leon's public face in legal matters

### weaknesses
- Emotionally closed off; cannot form genuine intimacy
- Views people as assets or liabilities
- When challenged on his methods, becomes defensive and combative
- His loyalty is conditional -- if Leon fails the test, Nathaniel may oppose him
- Physically capable of violence but reluctant to use it

### secret
Still holds the original property deeds to half a dozen Bellweather-related properties -- deeds never recorded in public registers. If these deeds surfaced, they would prove the extent of Aurelius's hidden network and could endanger Leon if the wrong people learned of them. Nathaniel has refused to hand them over to anyone, including Helen.

### knowledge_profile
**Knows:** The full legal structure of Aurelius's property network. Which deeds are legitimate and which were fabricated. The identities of property holders who owe him favors. Which witnesses can be pressured and which cannot.
**Suspects:** That Thomas Wren's notebook contains information Nathaniel never authorized -- personal details about Aurelius irrelevant to the property business.
**Does not know:** The full purpose of Bellweather House. What is in the basement. What Leon's true connection to the Ashcroft line is.

### relationship_hooks
- Primary point of contact for any legal/property investigation Leon undertakes
- Natural antagonist to Daniel (academic transparency vs. Nathaniel's legal opacity)
- Could become Leon's fierce protector -- but only after passing a loyalty test

# CORE PILLAR 3 -- THE WEAVER

### candidate_id: CORE-ORG-03
### proposed_name: Clara Bellweather (nee Whitfield)
### age_range: 30-38
### gender: Female
### occupation/social_role: Widow; operates a discreet salon for women of Alderwick
### organization_role: **The Weaver** -- Manages the social connections between women of influence. Coordinates the feminine intelligence network that Aurelius could never formally participate in.

### public_persona
A refined widow who hosts monthly drawing room gatherings. Elegant, well-connected, effortlessly charming. Speaks softly, listens intently, making every woman feel like the most interesting person in the world. Widely respected, rarely suspected beyond sociability.

### private_self
A master strategist. Social gatherings are intelligence operations in disguise. Has collected information for 20 years, long before Aurelius. Joined because Aurelius treated her network as legitimate intelligence rather than idle gossip. Fiercely protective of her women -- they are her family, army, and shield. Her loyalty is conditional on protecting her women first.

### primary_goal
Maintain and expand the network of women who control Alderwick's social landscape. Ensure the organization survives through social influence, not just documents. Men build networks with paper; women build networks with trust.

### fear
That the women in her network will be exposed, ridiculed, or ruined. Her women trust her with everything; she will not betray that trust.

### values
Trust, loyalty, discretion, social intelligence. Believes friendship-based information is more reliable than documentary evidence.

### contradiction
Uses social grace as a weapon. Pretends to be merely charming while systematically gathering and deploying intelligence. The most honest person in the room who tells the most lies.

### strengths
- Unmatched access to private conversations across all social classes
- Can mobilize women across the entire town for specific purposes
- Understands social dynamics better than anyone
- Discreet, patient, strategically brilliant
- Her salon is neutral ground where enemies can meet without suspicion

### weaknesses
- Cannot act directly in public (social constraints on women)
- Must use proxies and intermediaries for overt action
- When her women are threatened, becomes vindictive and unpredictable
- Her network is invisible to men -- dangerous but fragile
- If one woman is compromised, the entire chain breaks

### secret
Knows exactly who in Alderwick is sleeping with whom, who is pregnant out of wedlock, who is bankrupt, who is leaving town. Has never used this for personal gain but has threatened to reveal it to keep people in line. The list exists only in her mind.

### knowledge_profile
**Knows:** Private lives of every woman of influence. Which families are hiding debt. Which marriages are unhappy. Identities of women approached by the organization (and their responses).
**Suspects:** That Leon is more connected to Aurelius than he admits. Has been watching him since Chapter 1.
**Does not know:** What is in the Bellweather House basement. Full contents of Wren's notebook. The London connection.

### relationship_hooks
- Source of intelligence Leon cannot get through documents
- Could become Leon's most important ally or most dangerous opponent
- Natural ally to Helen (both guard the house and its secrets)

# CORE PILLAR 4 -- THE BETRAYER

### candidate_id: CORE-ORG-04
### proposed_name: Julian Ashworth (no relation to Leon)
### age_range: 28-35
### gender: Male
### occupation/social_role: Junior clergyman at St. Bartholomew's Church
### organization_role: **The Betrayer** -- Was a member of Aurelius's network but turned against it. Believes the secret society corrupted the church and perverted faith into power. Works from within the institution to dismantle the organization. A zealot, not a villain.

### public_persona
Young, earnest clergyman well-liked by his congregation. Devout, articulate, genuinely concerned for the spiritual welfare of his flock. Preaches sermons that are intellectually rigorous and morally uncompromising. Has the kind of sincere earnestness that makes people trust him immediately.

### private_self
Consumed by a single corrosive conviction: Aurelius's secret network corrupted Alderwick's moral fabric, and the church has been complicit through silence. His father attended Aurelius's gatherings; Julian grew up believing his father's "good works" were a mask. Joined the clergy to fight the organization from within. Not a nice person -- a moral person, which is different and more dangerous. Believes ends justify means he would never admit to anyone.

### primary_goal
To systematically dismantle the organization's remaining structure without exposing the church's complicity. Will manipulate Leon, use the church's authority, and turn members against each other -- all while maintaining his public sanctity.

### fear
Being exposed as the manipulator behind Leon's discoveries. If Leon realizes the "clues" were planted by Julian, everything collapses.

### values
Faith, moral clarity, institutional integrity. Believes truth must be publicly revealed and secrecy is always evil.

### contradiction
Demands transparency from others while hiding his own role as the architect of Leon's investigation. The ultimate hypocrite who believes he is the last honest man in Alderwick.

### strengths
- Church authority gives him access to anyone, anywhere
- Genuine charisma makes people voluntarily share their secrets
- Deep knowledge of Aurelius's network (his father told him)
- Moral conviction makes him fearless
- Can weaponize the church's institutional power without appearing to

### weaknesses
- Blind to his own hypocrisy -- truly believes he is righteous
- When challenged, doubles down rather than reflects
- Cannot tolerate ambiguity -- everything is either holy or damned
- Single-mindedness makes him vulnerable to people with mixed motives
- If exposed, he will not stop -- he will escalate

### secret
Has been feeding Leon's investigation directionally for months, steering him toward certain documents and away from others. Already ensured Nathaniel Cross is under suspicion and Silas Hawthorne's archive has been partially compromised. Leon has not discovered Julian's influence yet -- but it is embedded in every step of the investigation so far.

### knowledge_profile
**Knows:** Everything Aurelius's network did that the church tried to cover up. Identities of every clergyman who was a member. Connection between the network and church properties. Exactly which documents Leon has read and which he has not.
**Suspects:** That Leon will discover Julian's manipulation and react with either fury or understanding -- Leon's own methods involve selective information.
**Does not know:** What is in the Bellweather House basement. Full scope of the London connection. Whether Helen knows about his involvement.

### relationship_hooks
- Primary antagonistic force disguised as an ally -- the "friendly vicar" who plants every clue
- Natural conflict with Silas (Julian wants to expose; Silas wants to protect)
- Conflict with Nathaniel (Julian sees Nathaniel as corruption of faith into power)

One of the most observant women alive. Has heard every secret whispered in every bedroom over 25 years. ### values
Family, neutrality, professionalism.

### contradiction
Maintains absolute neutrality while being secretly invested in every conflict. Never takes a side, but always knows who is winning.

- Physical access to every document, letter, and personal item in Alderwick's elite

**Suspects:** Julian Ashworth is steering Leon's investigation.

Meticulous, slightly nervous physician respected for medical skill but not particularly liked for his manner. Speaks in precise, technical sentences. Uncomfortable in social situations. Patients trust his medical skill but find his office visit emotionally exhausting.

A brilliant amateur scholar whose intellect has craved engagement with the wider world while stuck in Alderwick. Aurelius recruited him for his ability to read, translate, and decode ancient texts. ### fear
That the texts will be destroyed or lost. That someone will use his expertise for purposes that degrade the intellectual tradition Aurelius was building.

### values
Knowledge, intellectual rigor, truth. Understanding is a moral act.

- Brilliant pattern-recognition ability
- Genuine scholarly curiosity makes him persistent

- Scholarly resource for decoding Aurelius's documents and library
- Could be baited by offering him "texts to decode" (a manipulation Leon could use)

# SPECIALIST 2 -- THE MASON

### proposed_name: Thomas "Tom" Bellweather (no relation to Clara Bellweather)
### age_range: 35-45
### gender: Male
### occupation: Master mason / builder / structural surveyor

Burly, straightforward man highly respected in his trade. The builder everyone calls when something needs to be done right. Honest, reliable, not interested in politics or gossip. Understands the physical bones of Alderwick better than anyone. Knows which walls are load-bearing, which foundations are cracking, which rooms were added and when, and which parts of Bellweather House were built specifically to hide something. Aurelius told him to build a room, reinforce the walls, and never ask questions. Tom built it. He knows the structural details of the secret room better than anyone alive. He has never told anyone what he built, because he was paid not to.

### primary_goal
To maintain the structural integrity of the buildings he worked on. Takes pride in his work and wants to ensure that what he built still stands. Not invested in the organization's purpose -- invested in the quality of his craftsmanship.

### fear
That someone will discover what he built in the basement, and he will be held responsible for something he did not understand.

### values
Craftsmanship, honesty, reliability. A good build should last 200 years.

### contradiction
Claims to have been paid not to ask questions, but has spent 40 years wondering what is in the room he built. Never told anyone, but has thought about it every single day.

### strengths
- Encyclopedic knowledge of Bellweather House's structure, including the basement alterations
- Can assess the structural integrity of any building in Alderwick
- Practical problem-solving ability
- Honest and reliable -- if he says something, it is true

- Loyal to his craft means values structural integrity over moral considerations

The plans show a room built specifically to hold something heavy and dangerous. Does not know what the room was built to hold -- but knows exactly how heavy the floor needed to be.

### knowledge_profile
**Knows:** Every structural detail of Bellweather House, including the basement alterations. Which walls are solid and which are false. The load-bearing capacity of the basement floor. The date when the basement room was built.
**Suspects:** That the room was built to hold something heavy and dangerous. Not sure what "heavy and dangerous" means literally or metaphorically.
**Does not know:** What is in the basement. The purpose of the organization. Why Aurelius needed a hidden room.

### relationship_hooks
- Could provide Leon with structural access to the basement that no one else can
- Honest and straightforward -- if Leon earns his trust, Tom will tell him everything he knows

Warm, cosmopolitan woman who runs one of Alderwick's most popular establishments. The Rose and Thorn is where people come for excellent tea, light refreshment, and the latest news of the wider world.
