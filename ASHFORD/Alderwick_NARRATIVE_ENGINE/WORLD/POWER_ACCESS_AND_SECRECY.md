## AUTHORITATIVE PRINCIPLE

Alderwick is set in historical England, 1816.

The anomalous / supernatural / ARC power system is **NOT** common public knowledge.

Most ordinary people should **NOT** understand:
- ARC mechanics
- anomalous abilities
- true supernatural entities
- advanced protection systems
- Aurelius‑related power
- hidden organizations

Supernatural knowledge must remain scarce.

---

## PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE TIERS

| Tier | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **PUBLIC_DENIAL** | Ordinary society considers supernatural claims false, foolish, or superstition. |
| **RUMOR** | Strange stories circulate without reliable understanding. |
| **FOLK_KNOWLEDGE** | Some old traditions contain fragments of genuine truth. |
| **RESTRICTED_KNOWLEDGE** | Certain officials, scholars, investigators, families, or institutions know anomalies exist. |
| **HIDDEN_TRUTH** | Only a very small number understand the underlying system. |

Knowledge is itself a form of power.

---

## ACCESS CONTROL METADATA

Every supernatural ability/item **must** have the following fields documented:

- `public_visibility` – what the average person sees
- `known_by` – who currently knows about it
- `suspected_by` – who suspects but cannot confirm
- `verified_by` – who has confirmed its nature
- `access_requirement` – what is needed to obtain/use it
- `rarity` – COMMON / UNCOMMON / RESTRICTED / SECRET / RARE / UNIQUE
- `acquisition_method` – how it is obtained (inheritance, discovery, trade, etc.)
- `training_requirement` – what skill/knowledge is needed to use it safely
- `risk_of_discovery` – how likely it is to become public
- `institutional_interest` – whether organisations (military, church, government) seek it
- `theft_risk` – how likely it is to be stolen
- `misuse_risk` – potential harm if used improperly

---

## CONVENTIONAL WEAPONS REMAIN DANGEROUS

No supernatural character receives automatic immunity.

Track for each protection ability:

- `conventional_vulnerability` – what ordinary weapons can still hurt them
- `resistance` – degree of mitigation (narrative, not percentage unless required)
- `protection` – areas/conditions covered
- `coverage` – body parts or situations where protection applies
- `duration` – how long it lasts
- `failure_conditions` – when it stops working
- `weak_points` – specific vulnerabilities
- `counters` – known ways to bypass or overwhelm it

A supernatural individual can still be killed by conventional weapons if:
- surprised
- unprepared
- exhausted
- struck in a vulnerable area
- overwhelmed by multiple attackers
- exposed to artillery
- hit by a weapon their protection does not counter

---

## NO ARBITRARY PERCENTAGE IMMUNITY

Do not use simplistic "70% bullet resistance" values unless the specific Alderwick ability explicitly requires quantitative mechanics.

Prefer narrative conditions:

- *glancing hit survivable*
- *direct hit lethal*
- *protection covers torso but not head*
- *protection works only once*
- *protection requires preparation*
- *protection fails after repeated impacts*
- *protection only counters certain weapon types*

---

## POWER LEVEL VS KNOWLEDGE

Separate these dimensions:

- **RAW POWER** – inherent strength
- **KNOWLEDGE** – understanding of the system
- **CONTROL** – precision and reliability
- **PREPARATION** – readiness and resources
- **INFORMATION** – situational awareness
- **RESOURCES** – tools and allies
- **TACTICAL SKILL** – in‑the‑moment decisions
- **STRATEGIC SKILL** – long‑term planning

A weaker character can defeat a stronger one through superior information, preparation, positioning, or countermeasures.

---

## ARC SECRECY

If ARC remains part of current Alderwick canon:

ARC should be rare, controlled, and poorly understood by the general public.

Do **NOT** allow:
- mass production
- unrestricted access
- universal knowledge
- random civilian use

unless future canon explicitly establishes this.

For each ARC item track:
- origin
- creator
- owner
- current location
- known users
- known capabilities
- unknown capabilities
- limitations
- history
- transfer conditions
- secrecy level
