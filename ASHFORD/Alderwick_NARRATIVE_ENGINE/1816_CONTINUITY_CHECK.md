# 1816 Continuity Checklist

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Purpose:** Mandatory checklist for every future Alderwick chapter
**Rule:** START ERA = 1816 (Regency England); the governing temporal standard is the **CURRENT STORY YEAR** (see `../LIVING_CANON_TIMELINE_SOP.md`, tracked in `15_METADATA/WORLD_STATE.md`). Any draft containing technology, institutions, social practices, objects, vocabulary, or events incompatible with the current story year must be flagged during continuity QA.

## CHAPTER 1816 CONTINUITY CHECKLIST

### A. ERA CHECK (1816 Regency England)

- [ ] **NO railways.** First passenger railway: 1825. Leon does not arrive by train. No stations. No locomotives.
- [ ] **NO electricity.** No lamps, switches, sockets, bulbs. Lighting: candles, lamp oil.
- [ ] **NO telephones.** No telegraphs. Communication: letters, face-to-face, newspapers.
- [ ] **NO automobiles.** Transportation: walking, horse-drawn carriages, diligence, hackney cab.
- [ ] **NO professional police force.** Metropolitan Police: 1829. Law enforcement: watchmen, constables.
- [ ] **NO gas lighting.** Not yet widespread in 1816.
- [ ] **NO digital technology.** No computers, internet, televisions, cameras, GPS.
- [ ] **NO modern medicine.** No germ theory, no anesthesia. Bloodletting, herbal remedies.
- [ ] **Currency is pounds/shillings/pence.** NOT decimal. NOT £X.XX format.
- [ ] **No pillar boxes.** Letters collected at post office.
- [ ] **No central heating.** Fireplaces in every room. Chamber pots for toilets.

### B. SOCIAL / ETIQUETTE CHECK

- [ ] **Class distinctions respected.** Characters behave according to social position.
- [ ] **Formal address where appropriate.** "Mr.," "Mrs.," "Sir," "Ma'am" as period-appropriate.
- [ ] **Gender roles reflect 1816.** Men and women have different social expectations.
- [ ] **Religion is part of daily life.** Church is community center.
- [ ] **Education is limited.** Grammar schools, private tutoring. No universal schooling.

### C. LOCATION CHECK

- [ ] **No "train station."** Replace with: coach station, market square, or arrival by road.
- [ ] **Church name is St. Bartholomew's.** (Per World Foundation v2. NOT St. Mary's.)
- [ ] **Buildings are Georgian-era (1816).** No Victorian, Edwardian, or 20th century buildings visible.
- [ ] **Fog is era-appropriate.** Natural fog, not industrial pollution fog.
- [ ] **No modern signage.** Hand-painted signs, wooden boards, iron hang-signs.

### D. CHARACTER BEHAVIOR CHECK

- [ ] **No modern speech patterns.** No slang from post-1816 eras.
- [ ] **No modern concepts of privacy, romance, or personal freedom.**
- [ ] **Investigation requires physical effort.** Leon walks, visits, reads, observes.
- [ ] **Leon's notebook is his primary tool.** Not a phone, not a computer.
- [ ] **Helen's identity is hidden.** She always knows more than she reveals.

### E. INFORMATION FLOW CHECK

- [ ] **Information travels at 1816 speed.** No instant knowledge.
- [ ] **Characters have believable information sources.** Letters, documents, conversation, observation.
- [ ] **Physical evidence exists.** Documents, objects, observations.
- [ ] **Letters and documents are used as plot devices.** Not exposition dumps.
- [ ] **Misinformation is plausible.** In a world without instant verification.

### F. OBJECT STATE CHECK

- [ ] **Objects don't disappear.** If Leon picks something up, he has it until used/lost/stored.
- [ ] **Clothing is period-appropriate.** Georgian-era garments.
- [ ] **Food is era-appropriate.** November 1816 autumn/winter menu.
- [ ] **Transportation is period-appropriate.** Walking, carriage, horse.

## ESCALATION RULES

If ANY item above fails:
1. **FLAG the draft** with `[[1816-ERROR]]` tag
2. **Document what the error is** and which rule it violates
3. **DO NOT silently fix.** Present to author if the fix requires creative decisions
4. **If fix is mechanical** (e.g., "train" -> "stagecoach"), apply fix and note it
