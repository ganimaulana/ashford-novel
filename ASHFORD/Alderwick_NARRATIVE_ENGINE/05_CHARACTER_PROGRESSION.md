# System 05 — Character Progression

**Status:** CORE SYSTEM | **Created:** 2026-08-29
**LOTM Transfer:** Rules 2, 10 (gradual capability, structured progression)
**Evidence Confidence:** 8/10 (strong canon support)

---

## A. PURPOSE

Translate gradual capability scaling into CURRENT Alderwick terms. Progression is earned, visible, consequential, and imperfect — NOT through supernatural levels.

## B. CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Progression is not leveling.** Characters grow through experience, knowledge, trust, and consequences — not ability scores.
2. **Every gain has a cost.** Advancement is not free. Every new capability comes with new risk or responsibility.
3. **Setbacks are essential.** Characters should lose ground before gaining it. Progress is not linear.
4. **Progression must be VISIBLE.** The reader should see the difference between early-Lion and late-Leon.

## C. PROGRESSION DIMENSIONS

Track character growth across non-mutually-exclusive dimensions:

| Dimension | What It Measures | Leon's Trajectory |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| Competence | Ability to solve problems | Developing through investigation |
| Knowledge | Understanding of the world | Growing through document discovery |
| Trust | Ability to form alliances | Slowly building with Daniel, Arthur, Margaret |
| Access | Ability to reach information | Increasing through social connections |
| Responsibility | Weight of knowledge | Increasing as mystery deepens |
| Emotional Maturity | Handling uncertainty and loss | Gradual development |
| Investigative Ability | Skill at finding and interpreting clues | Clear upward trajectory in Ch 1-8 |
| Social Standing | Position in town hierarchy | Low to moderate over time |
| Risk Tolerance | Willingness to engage with danger | Increasing slowly |
| Understanding of the Anomalous | Comprehension of supernatural elements | Early stages, developing |

## D. OPERATIONAL RULES

1. **No character gain without consequence.** If Leon discovers something, something must change that costs him emotionally, socially, or physically.
2. **Setbacks must be meaningful, not cosmetic.** A setback that doesn't change the trajectory is just noise.
3. **Progression must be earned through the story world's logic.** No external deus ex machina.
4. **Visible progression markers.** Document clear before/after states for key competencies.
5. **Different characters progress at different rates.** Leon advances investigation skills; others may advance trust or knowledge.

## E. WHEN TO USE

- Every major chapter (not every scene needs progression)
- Every arc milestone
- Every significant revelation
- Every relationship development

## F. WHEN NOT TO USE

- Pacing-release scenes
- Transitional chapters
- Scenes focused on atmosphere or worldbuilding

## G. FAILURE MODES

| Failure | Sign | Fix |
|---------|------|-----|
| Unearned advancement | Character suddenly capable without showing work | Add the process |
| No setbacks | Character only improves | Insert a meaningful failure |
| Static characterization | Character is same at end as at start | Map progression arc |
| Power fantasy | Character becomes too capable too fast | Add consequences and limits |

## H. QA QUESTIONS

- [ ] Is this progression earned through story events?
- [ ] Does the character face setbacks as well as gains?
- [ ] Can I see the difference between early and late states?
- [ ] Does advancement come with a cost?
- [ ] Is the progression visible to the reader?

## I. Alderwick-SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION

- Leon's progression is INTELLECTUAL and EMOTIONAL, not physical or supernatural
- Track his notebook entries as a progression metric — do they get deeper/more complex?
- Track his trust in others — does he rely more on Daniel/Arthur over time?
- Track his risk tolerance — does he venture into riskier territory as he gains confidence?
- His progression from "passive observer" to "active investigator" is the arc spine
- Helen's progression: from "hidden gatekeeper" to "potential ally" or "antagonist"

## J. CROSS-SYSTEM DEPENDENCIES

- **Character Depth** — Progression builds on established depth
- **Mystery Engine** — Investigation competence drives mystery progression
- **Long-Range Arc** — Character progression is the spine of the long arc
- **Scene Construction** — Scenes should contain progression moments
- **Continuity** — Progression state must be tracked and preserved

## K. CONFIDENCE / EVIDENCE STATUS

**CORE status. HIGH evidence.** The character profiles explicitly describe Leon's trajectory. The published chapters show progression from passive to active investigation. The Supernatural_System.md progression model (Misunderstanding -> Mastery) confirms this framework.
