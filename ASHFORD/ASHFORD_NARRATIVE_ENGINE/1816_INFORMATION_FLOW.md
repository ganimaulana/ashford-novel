# 1816 Information Flow Model

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Purpose:** Model information movement in 1816 Ashford for Mystery Engine and Information Control system
**Source:** World Foundation v2 (1816)

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

In 1816, information is SLOW, PHYSICAL, and LOCALIZED. There is no instant retrieval. Every piece of knowledge requires effort: a journey, a letter written, a person found, a document read.

This is NOT a limitation to work around. It is the SOURCE of narrative tension, pacing, and mystery structure.

## DISTORTION FACTORS

| Factor | Effect | Example |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Memory decay | Details fade or change | Witness remembers color wrong |
| Deliberate lie | False information given | Character lies to protect someone |
| Partial knowledge | Information is incomplete | Gossip chain loses key detail |
| Personal bias | Observer interprets through worldview | Class bias affects reading of event |
| Document forgery | Fake documents exist | Letter could be forged |
| Lost documents | Information destroyed | Records burned, water damaged, misplaced |
| Class barriers | Access restricted by social position | Working class cannot access documents |
| Death | Information source permanently gone | Key witness dies before testimony |

## HOW LEON INVESTIGATES IN 1816

1. **Leon walks.** He goes to the library, the church, the vicar's house, the solicitor, the shop.
2. **Leon reads documents.** Letters, parish records, deeds, newspapers, books, artifacts.
3. **Leon talks to people.** The vicar, the librarian, Arthur, Margaret, the constable, locals.
4. **Leon observes.** He notices things others miss: physical details, behavioral patterns, environmental clues.
5. **Leon collects in his notebook.** Physical record of findings.
6. **Leon waits for responses.** Letters take time. People take time to respond.
7. **Leon must physically return to locations.** He cannot email anyone. He must visit.

## INFORMATION AS TENSION

- **Slow information = slow tension.** The delay between asking a question and getting an answer IS the tension.
- **Misinformation is easy.** Without instant verification, characters work from incomplete information.
- **Physical evidence is rare.** A letter, a deed, a diary entry are precious because they are HARD TO GET.
- **Geographic barriers are real.** If the answer is in London, Leon must travel there.
- **Social barriers are real.** Leon's access to information depends on his social position.
