---

## AUTHORITATIVE PRINCIPLE

This document establishes the conventional military reality of 1816 England.  
All supernatural or anomalous developments are layered **on top** of this baseline, never replacing it.

---

## INFANTRY

- Primary weapon: **flintlock smoothbore musket** (Brown Bess pattern)
  - Effective range: ~100 yards (accuracy), ~200 yards (area fire)
  - Rate of fire: ~2–3 rounds per minute
  - Bayonet fixed for close combat
- Elite units: **Rifle regiments** (e.g., 95th Rifles) use the Baker rifle
  - Greater accuracy and range (~200+ yards)
  - Slower to reload
  - Often used as skirmishers
- Soldiers also carry a **sword** or **bayonet** as sidearm
- Standard ammunition: paper cartridges containing ball and powder

---

## CAVALRY

- Heavy cavalry (Life Guards, Dragoon Guards): wear cuirass (breastplate) for protection
  - Weapons: **sabre**, **pistol**, **carbine**
- Light cavalry (Hussars, Light Dragoons): focus on speed and reconnaissance
  - Weapons: **sabre**, **carbine**, **lance** (in some units)
- Cavalry charges are devastating against unprepared infantry

---

## ARTILLERY

- Field guns (6‑pounder, 9‑pounder, 12‑pounder)
- Howitzers for indirect fire
- Canister shot and grape shot for anti‑personnel use
- Artillery is the most destructive weapon on the battlefield
- Requires trained crews and horses to move

---

## SMALL ARMS (Private / Civilian)

- Hunting firearms: flintlock fowling pieces, rifles
- Pistols: pocket pistols, duelling pistols (smoothbore, flintlock)
- Swords: personal sidearms for gentlemen, officers

---

## LIMITATIONS

- Flintlock ignition is unreliable in wet weather
- Black powder produces thick smoke, obscuring vision
- Reloading is slow, leaving soldiers vulnerable
- Effective accuracy of muskets is low beyond 100 yards
- Communication on battlefield is limited to drums, bugles, and couriers

---

## SUPERNATURAL INTERACTION

- Conventional weapons **remain dangerous**
- No automatic immunity to musket, rifle, or artillery fire
- Protection must be conditional and limited
