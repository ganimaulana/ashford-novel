## DETAILED INTRODUCTION TIMELINE

### Arc 001: ESTABLISH THE HOUSE

**New Organization Member:** Margaret Whitmore (CORE-ORG-06)

- Margaret is **already in canon**. She provides the easiest, most natural entry point.
- Her organizational role is NOT revealed in this arc. She remains "the warm old cook."
- Subtle hints that she notices things about Leon that a simple cook should not notice.

### Arc 003: DEEPEN THE MYSTERY

**New Organization Members:** Silas Hawthorne (CORE-ORG-01), Eleanor Vane (CORE-ORG-11, organizational role revealed), Nathaniel Cross (CORE-ORG-02)

- **Silas Hawthorne** introduced when Leon needs to access parish records. He is the invisible clerk who knows everything. His organizational role as The Archivist begins to become visible.
- **Eleanor Vane** has been in canon since Arc 001, but her organizational role as The Heir Apparent is revealed for the first time. She gives Leon Aurelius's locked box (or teases that she will). This is a major plot point.
- **Nathaniel Cross** introduced when Leon encounters legal pressure -- a property dispute or estate complication. He tests Leon rigorously.
- **Purpose:** Provide Leon with his first real knowledge resources (Silas), his first major emotional revelation (Eleanor's box), and his first major obstacle/test (Nathaniel).

**Reader Load:** 3 new organization members. This is the maximum recommended load. The reader needs to be deeply invested in the mystery by this point.

### Arc 004: HIDDEN INTELLIGENCE

**New Organization Members:** Ruth Ashworth (CORE-ORG-05), Thomas "Tom" Bellweather (CORE-ORG-09)

- **Ruth Ashworth** is introduced when Leon encounters evidence that seems to come from nowhere. He gradually realizes someone invisible (the laundress) is providing it.
- **Thomas Bellweather** is introduced when Leon needs help with the physical structure of Bellweather House. He is the honest, practical craftsman who knows about the basement.
- **Purpose:** Provide Leon with physical access (Tom) and hidden intelligence (Ruth). Both operate below the radar.

**Reader Load:** 2 new organization members. By this arc, the reader is accustomed to organization members.

---

## CRITICAL INTRODUCTION RULES

1. **Never introduce more than 3 new organization members in a single arc.** This is a hard maximum. 2 is the recommended target.
2. **Never introduce a NO_POV character without a clear narrative purpose.** If a character will never get POV, the reader must see them through Leon's eyes and have a strong reason to care.
3. **Never introduce Julian Ashworth as a new character.** He has been present from early in the story as a helpful figure. His reveal recontextualizes existing material.
4. **Never reveal an organization member's full role immediately.** Each character should be introduced as something other than their organizational identity. The role becomes visible gradually.
5. **Always introduce a character through Leon's investigation.** Leon should encounter them because his investigation creates a need. They should never appear arbitrarily.
6. **Always respect knowledge boundaries.** No unexplained prior meetings, no unexplained familiarity, no unexplained trust.
7. **The reader should discover an organization member's true role through evidence, not exposition.** If Leon learns that Margaret is The Informant, he should piece it together from evidence. The reader should discover it the same way.

**New Organization Members:** Julian Ashworth (CORE-ORG-04, reveal), Dr. Samuel Finch (CORE-ORG-08)

- **Julian Ashworth** is the **big reveal**. He has been present as a helpful figure since Arc 002. The reveal that he has been manipulating Leon's investigation from the start should happen here. Every clue Leon has discovered is recontextualized. This is the most narratively explosive moment in the entire document.
- **Dr. Samuel Finch** is introduced when Leon needs help decoding a specific Aurelius document.
- **Purpose:** Julian's reveal recontextualizes the entire investigation. Samuel provides the scholarly dimension.

**Reader Load:** 1 major reveal (Julian) + 1 new character (Samuel). Julian is NOT introduced as a new character -- he has been present and trusted. The reveal recontextualizes existing material.

### Arc 006+: LATE-GAME CHARACTERS

**As Needed:** Arthur Penhaligon (CORE-ORG-12), Daniel Carter (CORE-ORG-13), Edward Pembroke (CORE-ORG-07)

- **Arthur Penhaligon** introduced when public narrative vs. private secrecy becomes relevant.
- **Daniel Carter** introduced when Leon needs academic evaluation of Aurelius's documents.
- **Edward Pembroke** introduced when Leon encounters a financial obstacle.
- **Purpose:** These characters provide the final pieces of the puzzle -- public narrative, academic rigor, and financial access.

- **Purpose:** Establish the house as a character. Introduce the possibility that Margaret sees more than she says.

**Reader Load:** 1 new organization member (Margaret, but canon-validated so feels like 0)

### Arc 002: EXPAND THE SOCIAL WORLD

**New Organization Members:** Clara Bellweather (CORE-ORG-03), Isabella Chen (CORE-ORG-10)

- **Clara Bellweather** introduced through Leon's first visit to a drawing room gathering. Elegant, charming, subtly testing Leon. Her organizational role is not yet visible.
- **Isabella Chen** introduced through Leon's visit to her tea shop. Warm, cosmopolitan, subtly observant. Her organizational role is not yet visible.
- Both provide intelligence Leon cannot get through documents.
- **Purpose:** Establish that Ashford has a social world that Leon does not understand. Both introduced as helpful but mysterious.

**Reader Load:** 2 new organization members (both introduced as friendly/mysterious, not as org members yet)
