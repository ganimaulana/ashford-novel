## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### 1. Differentiation Over Similarity
Each of the 13 candidates must be meaningfully different from every other candidate in at least 5 of: personality, motivation, worldview, competence, weakness, social position, organization role, mystery relevance, and POV potential.

### 2. Hierarchy of Importance
Not all 13 are equal. The pool contains:
- **4 Core Pillars** — Central to organization function and plot
- **3 Major Supporting** — Regularly active, important to arcs
- **3 Specialist** — Niche competence, situationally crucial
- **3 Quiet/Delayed** - Low visibility now, high impact when revealed

### 3. Reader Load Management
**Recommendation:** No more than **2-3 new organization members** should become reader-visible within a single arc. More than this risks:
- Name fatigue (readers cannot distinguish names)
- Relationship dilution (connections feel unearned)
- Mystery dilution (too many unknowns)
- POV fragmentation (too many perspectives)

### 4. Knowledge Boundaries Are Sacred
Every proposed relationship must respect:
- No unexplained prior meetings
- No unexplained familiarity
- No unexplained trust
- No unexplained secret knowledge

### 5. PROPOSED Until Approved
Every candidate in this directory carries `status = PROPOSED`. They may be:
- Approved as-is
- Approved with modifications
- Combined with another candidate
- Rejected entirely
- Saved as deep reserve for future arcs

## RELATIONSHIP DESIGN RULES

When recording relationships between candidates:
- Direction matters (A knows B does not mean B knows A)
- Trust is a spectrum, not binary
- Conflict is a feature, not a bug
- Hidden relationships (known to author but not reader) are tracked
- Knowledge asymmetry is intentional and narratively useful

---

## POV DESIGN RULES

POV classifications:
- **NO_POV** — Only seen through Leon's eyes; no internal access
- **OCCASIONAL_POV** — Brief POV sections in future arcs (1-2 per arc max)
- **RECURRING_POV** — Regular POV sections (requires narrative infrastructure)
- **MAJOR_POV** — Primary or co-POV (very expensive narratively)
- **POSSIBLE_FUTURE_POV** — Potential but not currently planned
