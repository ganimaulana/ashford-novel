# 1816 Event Calendar

**Status:** PROPOSED — NOT CANON
**Purpose:** Chronological reference for historically significant events

---

## JANUARY 1816

### 9 January — Davy Lamp Tested
Sir Humphry Davy's safety lamp first tested at Hebburn Colliery, Durham. Response to coal mine firedamp explosions.
- **Mood:** Technology as hope and false dawn.

### Late January — Post-War Unemployment Peaks
Winter unemployment at its worst. Demobilized soldiers and sailors struggle. Frost fairs on the Thames possible (following 1814 example).
- **Mood:** Desperation and survival.

---

## FEBRUARY 1816

### 20 February — Preston Gas Lighting
Preston becomes first English town outside London with public gas lighting (Jesuit priest Joseph Dunn).
- **Mood:** New cutting-edge technology making the ordinary world seem archaic.

---

## MARCH 1816

### 18 March — Income Tax Abolished
Wartime income tax abolished. Benefits the wealthy; does nothing for the unemployed poor.
- **Mood:** The rich get richer while the poor starve.

---

## MAY 1816

### 2 May — Prince Leopold Marries Princess Charlotte
Marries Princess Charlotte Augusta (d. 1817).
- **Mood:** Royal pageantry vs. popular suffering.

### 16 May — Beau Brummell Flees to France
Flees England over gambling debts. The arbiter of fashion abandons England.
- **Mood:** Fragility of reputation and status.

### 22 May — Littleport and Ely Riots
Mass riot in East Anglia over economic distress. Militia fires on crowd.
- **Mood:** Fear of the "mob." Social instability.

---

## JUNE 1816

### 14 June — Peace Society Founded
Society for the Promotion of Permanent and Universal Peace established in London.
- **Mood:** Post-war pacifism vs. imperial ambition.

### 18 June — Wimbledon Common Riot
False newspaper reports about Waterloo anniversary military review trigger riot.
- **Mood:** Chaos from misinformation.

### 28 June — Luddites Destroy Bobbinet Machines
Luddites destroy lace-making machinery at Loughborough.
- **Mood:** Resistance to technological change.

---

## JULY 1816

### 9 July — Argentine Independence Declared
Argentina declares independence from Spain (Congress of Tucuman).
- **Mood:** Cracks in the empire.

---

## AUGUST 1816

### 13 August — Aberdeen Earthquake
Strongest earthquake ever in Scotland. Felt across northern Britain.
- **Mood:** Ominous. The earth shaking itself.

---

## SEPTEMBER 1816

### Frost in September
Frost across Britain, killing crops already weakened by summer cold.
- **Mood:** Despair. The earth hostile.

---

## NOVEMBER 1816 — CANON PERIOD

### 12-26 November 1816 (Chapters 001-014)

By this point, 1816 has fully revealed itself as the Year Without a Summer:
- Harvest failed. Food scarce and expensive.
- Cold setting in — unusually severe autumn/winter.
- Demobilized soldiers still searching for work.
- Political anxiety at a peak.
- Sky perpetually grey.
- Social unrest simmers beneath the surface.

**This is the atmospheric foundation of the entire Ashford narrative.**
