## PRIMARY SOURCES (CONTEMPORARY TO 1816)

### Newspapers and Periodicals
- *The Times* (London) — daily newspaper, 1816 archives
- *Lloyd's List* — shipping and trade news, published 3x/week
- *The British Almanac* (1817) — annual reference
- *Ackermann's Repository of Arts* — fashion and culture journal
- *The Black Dwarf* (founded 1817) — radical newspaper

### Government Documents
- Parliamentary debates and proceedings (Hansard)
- Proclamations and gazette notices
- Poor law records
- Magistrates' session records

### Personal Accounts
- Diaries and letters of the period (various collections)
- Travel accounts

## CONFIDENCE NOTES

All historical facts in this reservoir are drawn from the sources above. Where a fact is:

- **VERY HIGH confidence:** Supported by multiple authoritative secondary sources and/or primary documentation
- **HIGH confidence:** Supported by at least one strong secondary source
- **MEDIUM confidence:** Supported by one source or involves reasonable inference from documented facts

Where a claim is an inference or fictional adaptation, it is explicitly labeled as such in the reservoir entries.
