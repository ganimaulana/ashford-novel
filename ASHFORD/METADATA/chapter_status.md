## Status Definitions

- **OFFICIAL**: Released/locked chapter in official canon. Edits require explicit approval.
- **DRAFT**: Working version of a chapter under active development.
- **EXPLORATION**: Exploratory material, alternate scenes, or early drafts.
- **ARCHIVE**: Preserved older versions for reference.

## Workflow Rules

1. **CHAPTERS/** is protected. No automatic modifications during exploration work.
2. **ARCS/** is the workspace for all exploration, generation, and drafting.
3. Material only moves from ARCS to CHAPTERS with explicit user approval.
4. Never delete exploration material after promotion; preserve as development history.
