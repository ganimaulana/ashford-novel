## Underground Network Role

**FRAMEWORK INFORMATION - NOT YET CANON**

### Leon's Position dalam Underground

**Status:** Part of informal underground network di Ashford

**Role:**
- Pattern recognizer - menghubungkan informasi disparate
- Investigator - aktif mencari informasi
- Observer - dokumentasi cases
- Network connector - social connections yang membantu investigation

**Leon's Strengths dalam Underground:**
- Observasi detail
- Logical deduction (berkembang over time)
- Local knowledge
- Social network
- Ability to ask right questions

**Leon BUKAN:**
- Paranormal expert dari awal
- Leader atau authority figure
- Always right
- Omniscient
- Supernatural hunter atau hero

### Leon's Knowledge Development

**Early story:**
- Mungkin tidak aware ada underground network
- Mulai notice patterns sendiri
- Bertemu dengan others yang juga notice

**Mid story:**
- Realizes ada others tracking anomalies
- Learns Universal Rules through experience
- Begins understanding scope

**Later story:**
- Active participant dalam network
- Contributes observations dan deductions
- Helps document cases

### Leon's Relationship dengan ARC System

**Leon TIDAK:**
- Memiliki access ke complete ARC catalogue
- Tahu sistem ARC exists di awal
- Memiliki authority dalam classification
- Dapat classify cases sendiri tanpa experience

**Leon TAHU:**
- Cases yang dia personally investigate
- Information shared dengan him oleh network
- Cases yang dia discover independently
- Patterns yang dia recognize sendiri

### Public Life vs Underground Activity

**Public persona:**
- [BELUM DITETAPKAN - tergantung pekerjaan/profesi Leon]
- Normal daily life
- Regular routines
- Public interactions

**Underground activity:**
- Investigation dilakukan quietly
- Information gathering through conversations
- Pattern recognition dan documentation
- Network meetings informal, tidak scheduled
- Keeps supernatural knowledge dari public

**Balance:**
- Underground NOT full-time job
- Investigation fits into normal life
- Must maintain normalcy (Rule 10)
- Cannot reveal knowledge to public (Rule 04)

### Leon's Approach to Anomalies

**Initial response:**
- Mencari penjelasan logis terlebih dahulu
- Skeptical tentang supernatural
- Collects evidence sebelum concluding

**Development:**
- Gradually accepts some things tidak dapat dijelaskan normally
- Learns to recognize patterns
- Understands importance of rules
- Balances skepticism dengan openness

**Consistent traits:**
- Cautious, tidak reckless
- Methodical dalam investigation
- Respects safety protocols (eventually learns Universal Rules)
- Preserves documentation

### Leon dengan Universal Rules

Leon should gradually learn and apply:
- **RULE 01** — Never Assume Intent
- **RULE 02** — Never Test Without Reason
- **RULE 04** — Never Publicize
- **RULE 05** — Record Before Interpretation
- **RULE 08** — Two-Person Rule
- **RULE 09** — No Heroics
- **RULE 10** — Preserve Normality

**Learning process:**
- Not all rules known immediately
- Some learned through mistakes
- Understanding WHY rules exist
- Occasionally breaking rules (dengan consequences)
