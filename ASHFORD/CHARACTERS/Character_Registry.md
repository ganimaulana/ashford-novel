## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Characters are organized into three layers:

**LAYER 1 — ORDINARY CHARACTERS**
People who populate Ashford's everyday life. Not all supernatural. They are the backdrop that makes the world feel real.

**LAYER 2 — INTERESTING CHARACTERS**
People with something beneath the surface. They may or may not enter the Hidden World. Their depth makes Ashford feel lived-in.

**LAYER 3 — HIDDEN WORLD CHARACTERS**
Directly connected to the occult, secret organizations, bloodlines, or supernatural entities. A small, growing list.

### Margaret Eleanor Whitmore
**Role:** Tearoom/Coffee shop owner, Margaret's
**Age:** ~45–50
**Class:** Merchant class
**Location:** Market Square / High Street (Margaret's Tearoom & Coffee Shop)
**Function in Story:** Social hub, gossip network, local information source
**Personality:** Warm, practical, observant, no-nonsense, caring but not sentimental
**Knowledge:** Knows everyone in Ashford; knows everyone's business; gathering place where locals meet

## CLASS RELATIONSHIPS

`
Gentry ↔ Professional: Mutual respect, occasional social mixing
Professional ↔ Merchant: Respectful distance; business relationships
Merchant ↔ Artisan: Trade relationships; some familiarity
Artisan ↔ Laborer: Daily contact; practical relationships
All classes ↔ Poor: Distant; charity-based interactions
`

**Leon's Position:** 
- Gentry by NAME (Ashcroft)
- Appears lower class by CIRCUMSTANCE (no visible wealth)
- This mismatch creates social ambiguity — people react differently depending on what they know
