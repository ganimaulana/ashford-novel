# Universal Rules - Underground Network

## Status Dokumen

**Tujuan:** Aturan universal untuk interaksi dengan fenomena abnormal  
**Status:** Core principles - world-building framework  
**Terakhir diupdate:** 2026-08-26

---

## PRINSIP UTAMA

Universal Rules adalah prinsip-prinsip yang diikuti oleh underground network ketika berhadapan dengan fenomena abnormal.

**PENTING:**
- Rules ini **BUKAN** hukum resmi
- Rules ini **BUKAN** diketahui publik
- Rules ini dikembangkan melalui experience (often painful)
- Rules ini **TIDAK selalu diikuti** - orang membuat kesalahan
- Rules ini **TIDAK menjamin keselamatan** - hanya mengurangi risiko

---

## The Ten Rules

### RULE 01 — Never Assume Intent

**Prinsip:** Jangan mengasumsikan bahwa fenomena abnormal memiliki niat, tujuan, atau motivasi yang dapat dipahami.

**Explanation:**
- Anomali tidak selalu "ingin" sesuatu
- Pattern bukan bukti consciousness
- Anthropomorphizing berbahaya
- Behavior dapat mechanical, bukan purposeful

**Contoh kesalahan:** "Benda ini pasti ingin dibawa ke tempat X."

**Seharusnya:** "Benda ini consistently bergerak ke arah X dalam kondisi Y."

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Misinterpretation of behavior
- Dangerous assumptions tentang "what it wants"
- Treating phenomenon sebagai negotiable entity

---

### RULE 02 — Never Test Without Reason

**Prinsip:** Jangan melakukan eksperimen hanya untuk melihat "what happens".

**Explanation:**
- Every interaction memiliki potential consequences
- Testing dapat activate dormant phenomena
- Curiosity bukan justification
- "Just to see" adalah alasan terburuk

**Valid reasons untuk testing:**
- Understanding rules untuk safety
- Confirming pattern untuk prediction
- Necessary untuk resolving case
- Protecting others from greater harm

**Invalid reasons:**
- Curiosity
- "Proving" something untuk ego
- Entertainment
- Showing off

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Activating something yang sebelumnya harmless
- Escalating situation
- Creating new problems
- Putting others at risk

---

### RULE 03 — Never Replicate

**Prinsip:** Jangan mencoba menggandakan, reproduce, atau recreate fenomena abnormal.

**Explanation:**
- Tidak tahu apa yang membuat sesuatu work
- Replication dapat create uncontrolled version
- Tidak dapat guarantee sama outcome
- Artificial anomalies mungkin worse

**What this means:**
- Jangan membuat copy object
- Jangan recreate conditions yang caused event
- Jangan try to reproduce phenomenon "in controlled setting"
- Jangan teach others "how to make it happen"

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Multiple versions of same anomaly
- Loss of control
- Spreading phenomenon beyond original scope
- Creating worse variant

---

### RULE 04 — Never Publicize

**Prinsip:** Jangan menyebarkan informasi tentang fenomena abnormal kepada public.

**Explanation:**
- Public panic serves no one
- Information can inspire dangerous behavior
- Cannot control how information spreads
- Normal life must be preserved

**What can be shared publicly:**
- Generic warnings ("area unsafe")
- Cover stories (structural damage, gas leak)
- Historical information (without paranormal context)

**What must NEVER be shared:**
- Specific details of anomaly
- Rules of interaction
- Location of objects
- Names of involved individuals
- ARC system existence

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Mass hysteria
- Copycats attempting to find anomaly
- Media circus
- Loss of ability to investigate quietly

---

### RULE 05 — Record Before Interpretation

**Prinsip:** Pisahkan observasi dari teori. Document facts before developing explanations.

**Explanation:**
- Interpretation can bias further observation
- Theories can become self-fulfilling
- Facts remain facts even when interpretation changes
- Raw data is invaluable untuk future analysis

**What to record:**
- **What happened** - exact sequence
- **When** - date, time, duration
- **Where** - precise location
- **Who** - witnesses, participants
- **Conditions** - weather, lighting, circumstances
- **Exact words** - if speech involved
- **Physical changes** - any alterations

**What to separate:**
- **Theories** - clearly labeled as speculation
- **Feelings** - subjective responses
- **Assumptions** - what you think it means
- **Connections** - to other cases (may be coincidental)

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Confirmation bias
- Missing important details
- Inability to revise understanding
- Spreading false information

### RULE 06 — Never Remove an Anchor

**Prinsip:** Jika anomali terikat pada lokasi atau objek tertentu, jangan memindahkan anchor tanpa alasan kuat.

**Explanation:**
- Anchor mungkin containing atau limiting phenomenon
- Removal dapat release atau spread anomaly
- Anchor relationship mungkin tidak obvious
- May be there for good reason

**Valid reasons untuk removal:**
- Anchor itself causing greater danger
- Removal absolutely necessary untuk preservation of life
- Studied long enough to understand relationship

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Spreading phenomenon to wider area
- Losing ability to predict behavior
- Making anomaly mobile when previously stationary

---

### RULE 07 — Do Not Follow the Voice

**Prinsip:** Jika fenomena memanggil dengan nama familiar atau menggunakan suara familiar, jangan mengikuti sumber suara tanpa memahami nature-nya.

**Specific warnings:**
- Voice dari loved one yang not present
- Voice calling from impossible location
- Voice dengan wrong timing
- Voice yang sounds right tetapi says wrong things

**What to do instead:**
- Verify person is actually present
- Question inconsistencies
- Bring others dengan you
- Document what voice said
- DO NOT go alone to remote area

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Isolation dari safety
- Disappearance
- Exposure to harmful phenomenon

---

### RULE 08 — Two-Person Rule

**Prinsip:** Untuk kasus-kasus tertentu, investigation atau interaction harus dilakukan oleh minimal dua orang.

**When Two-Person Rule applies:**
- First investigation of new anomaly
- Entering location dengan history of disappearances
- Handling object dengan unknown properties
- Following up on R3+ risk classifications
- Investigation after dark
- Any case involving disorientation atau memory effects

**Responsibilities:**
- One person observes, one person records
- One person interacts, one person monitors
- Both verify each other observations
- Both can call off investigation if concerned

**Consequences of breaking:**
- No verification of what happened
- No one to help if danger occurs
- Disappearance dengan no witnesses

---

### RULE 09 — No Heroics

**Prinsip:** Jika tidak memahami fenomena, mundur. Survival lebih penting than investigation.

**What this means:**
- Retreat jika situation escalates beyond understanding
- Jangan "save" someone jika doing so risks multiple lives
- Jangan push investigation beyond safe limits
- Jangan feel obligated to "solve" everything

**When to retreat:**
- Physical danger imminent
- Phenomenon behaving unpredictably
- Rules being violated dengan consequences
- Feeling disoriented, confused, atau unwell
- Instinct says leave

**This is NOT cowardice:**
- Retreat untuk regroup adalah smart
- Protecting yourself protects future investigations
- Living to share information saves others

**Consequences of breaking:**
- Injury atau death
- Creating new victims
- Losing experienced investigators

---

### RULE 10 — Preserve Normality

**Prinsip:** Tujuan utama bukan "mengalahkan" anomali. Tujuannya adalah mencegah kehidupan normal masyarakat terganggu.

**What this means:**
- Public tidak perlu tahu what is being prevented
- Success adalah when nothing seems wrong
- Best outcome: anomaly remains obscure
- Intervention only when necessary

**Priorities:**
1. Prevent public exposure
2. Prevent casualties
3. Prevent property damage
4. Understand phenomenon
5. Document untuk future
6. Resolve jika possible (tetapi not required)

**This is underground actual role:**
- Not heroes
- Not paranormal police
- Not supernatural hunters
- Observers, recorders, quiet managers

## Application Notes

### Rules Are Not Absolute

These rules are **guidelines developed through experience.**

People dapat:
- Disagree with specific rules
- Have different interpretations
- Break rules deliberately (dengan consequences)
- Learn new rules through mistakes

Underground is not monolithic organization dengan strict enforcement.

### Rules Can Conflict

Sometimes rules conflict dalam specific situation. Make judgment call based on immediate danger vs long-term risk.

### Leon Relationship dengan Rules

Leon should:
- Know these rules (learned through experience)
- Generally follow them
- Understand why they exist
- Occasionally break them (dengan consequences)
- Teach them to others

Leon should NOT:
- Blindly follow without understanding
- Never make mistakes
- Always know best rule
- Be perfect rule-follower
