## Description

A coin (1970s British penny) yang consistently returns ke lokasi spesifik dalam Market Square area setelah dipindahkan.

**Physical characteristics:**
- British penny
- Date: 1971
- Worn condition
- Distinctive scratch on reverse
- Normal weight and composition (when tested)

**Anomalous behavior:**
- Reappears at original location
- Timeframe varies (hours to days)
- No visible movement observed
- Appears spontaneously

---

## Observed Behavior

**Pattern observed:**
1. Coin ditemukan di lokasi A (specific spot di Market Square)
2. Coin dipindahkan ke lokasi B
3. Setelah waktu tertentu (4-72 hours), coin menghilang dari lokasi B
4. Coin muncul kembali di lokasi A

**Conditions:**
- Occurs regardless of distance removed
- Occurs even if coin placed in container
- Occurs even if coin marked atau tested
- No pattern observed untuk timing

**Exceptions noted:**
- Unknown apakah behavior continues if coin destroyed
- Unknown apakah behavior continues jika lokasi A significantly altered
