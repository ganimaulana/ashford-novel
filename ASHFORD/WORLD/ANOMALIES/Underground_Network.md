# Underground Network Overview

## Status Dokumen

**Tujuan:** Framework untuk underground network di Ashford  
**Status:** World-building framework - NOT detailed canon yet  
**Terakhir diupdate:** 2026-08-26

---

## PRINSIP UTAMA

### Underground adalah INFORMAL Network, Bukan Organisasi

**Underground network BUKAN:**
- Organisasi resmi dengan struktur hierarchy
- Memiliki membership formal atau headquarters
- Memiliki funding atau resources
- Diketahui publik

**Underground network ADALAH:**
- Informal connections antar individuals
- People yang notice patterns
- Shared information secara selective
- Loose cooperation, no central authority

---

## Composition

**Types of people dalam underground:**
- Local residents yang notice patterns
- Researchers dan historians
- People yang experienced anomalies directly
- Curious individuals who ask questions
- Some professionals (librarians, archivists, etc.)

**Common traits:** Observant, curious, discreet, respect untuk rules

**NOT required:** Special powers, supernatural abilities, expert knowledge

---

## How Network Operates

### Information Sharing

**Methods:** Casual conversations, informal meetings, passing along stories, word of mouth

**NOT:** Official meetings, structured briefings, digital databases, mass communications

### Network Connections

**Structure:**
- Person A knows Person B, Person B knows Person C
- NOT everyone knows everyone
- Some central connectors, many peripheral members
- Varying degrees of involvement

### Levels of Involvement

**Core members:** Actively investigate, share regularly, know Universal Rules well

**Active members:** Occasionally investigate, share when notice something

**Peripheral members:** Rarely investigate, minimal involvement

**Aware individuals:** Know anomalies exist, not actively involved, don\'t spread publicly

---

## Goals and Purpose

### Primary Goals

**Goal #1: Preserve Normality** - Keep public unaware, maintain normal life

**Goal #2: Understand and Document** - Learn patterns, document behavior

**Goal #3: Prevent Harm** - Warn others, identify dangers, minimize casualties

### What Underground is NOT Trying to Do

**NOT:** Eliminate all anomalies, "save the world", become heroes, fight entities, gain power, profit from knowledge

---

## Underground Culture

### Values

- **Discretion** - Keep things quiet
- **Caution** - Don\'t take unnecessary risks
- **Respect** - For rules, for others, for unknown
- **Humility** - Admit what we don\'t know
- **Pragmatism** - Do what works
- **Community** - Protect Ashford residents

### Common Attitudes

- "Better not to test that"
- "Keep it to yourself"
- "Normal people don\'t need to know"
- "Rules exist for a reason"
- "We\'re observers, not fighters"
- "Document first, theorize later"

## Public vs Underground

### What Public Knows

Urban legends, ghost stories, weird coincidences, local oddities, historical mysteries

### What Public DOESN\'T Know

Patterns connecting events, rules governing anomalies, underground network exists, systematic documentation

### Maintaining Separation

**Methods:** Cover stories, generic explanations, letting people believe coincidence, encouraging mundane interpretations

---

## Joining the Network

### How People Get Involved

**Common paths:**
- Notice something unusual repeatedly
- Ask questions to right person
- Experience anomaly directly
- Know someone in network who shares information
- Research local history and find patterns

**NOT:** Formal recruitment, applications, interviews, tests, proving worthiness

### Gradual Process

1. Person notices patterns
2. Person talks to network member
3. Network member shares some information
4. Person decides level of involvement
5. Information shared based on involvement
6. Person integrated into network connections

---

## Network Challenges

### Problems Underground Faces

**Information control:** How much to share with whom, when to warn public vs keep quiet

**Resource limitations:** No funding, no equipment, limited time, limited expertise

**Coordination:** No central authority, different opinions, conflicting information

**Safety:** Investigations dangerous, no formal training, no backup system

### Internal Conflicts

Possible tensions about: How active vs passive, how much to intervene, when rules should be broken, who should know what

Underground tidak perfect atau unified. Real people dengan real disagreements.

## Underground dalam Story

### For Writing

**DO:**
- Show informal nature
- Include variety of involvement levels
- Show disagreements dan different opinions
- Make it feel organic, not organized
- Show limitations dan challenges

**DON\'T:**
- Make it feel like organization
- Give it structure atau hierarchy
- Make everyone know everything
- Make it too efficient
- Remove human element
