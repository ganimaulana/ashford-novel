## Arsitektur

**Karakteristik:**

### Periode Dominan
- **Victorian industrial** - mid-to-late Victorian (1850s-1890s kemungkinan)
- **Purely functional** - tidak dekoratif
- **Some Edwardian** - expansions awal 1900s
- **20th century modifications** - repairs, conversions

### Tipe Bangunan
- **Factories** - large brick buildings
- **Warehouses** - multi-storey storage
- **Workshops** - smaller scale production
- **Mills** (jika ada water/river)
- **Workers' terraces** - housing dekat pabrik

### Karakteristik Visual Industrial
- **Bata merah utilitarian** - plain, functional
- **Large windows** - untuk natural light di workshop
- **Tall chimneys** - untuk steam engines (beberapa mungkin sudah dibongkar)
- **Loading bays** - untuk goods
- **Minimal decoration** - purely functional design
- **Large open spaces interior** - untuk machinery

### Kondisi (1816)

**Mayoritas terbengkalai atau underused:**
- **Brickwork weathered** - menghitam dari polusi industri
- **Windows broken/boarded** - banyak kosong
- **Roofs damaged** - slate tiles hilang, bocor
- **Graffiti** - pada beberapa bangunan kosong
- **Overgrown** - weeds di yards
- **Chimneys** - beberapa sudah dibongkar untuk safety

**Beberapa converted:**
- **Loft apartments** - warehouse conversions (tren 1990s-2000s)
- **Studios** - artists' spaces
- **Small businesses** - storage, workshops
- **Original features** - exposed brick, beams, large windows

**Beberapa masih industrial:**
- **Small workshops** - masih operasional
- **Storage facilities**
- **Light manufacturing**

**Prinsip:** Old Industrial District adalah Victorian industrial architecture - purely functional, sekarang mayoritas abandoned atau undergoing conversion. Kontras antara decay dan adaptive reuse.

### Aktivitas Harian

**Pagi-Siang (6:00-17:00):**
- Small businesses - workshops, storage units operational
- Delivery lorries - accessing still-active businesses
- Converted loft residents - going to/from work
- Dog walkers - using abandoned spaces
- Urban explorers - occasional [trespassing]

**Sore-Malam (17:00-23:00):**
- Even quieter - businesses close
- Loft apartments - lights on
- Very isolated feel

**Sangat Larut (23:00-6:00):**

## Potensi Lokasi Paranormal

**[POTENSI LOKASI PARANORMAL - Old Industrial District]:**

**OLD INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT ADALAH LOKASI PARANORMAL IDEAL.**

**Karakteristik yang cocok:**
- **Abandoned factories** - empty, echoing, dark
- **Old warehouses** - large spaces, multiple floors
- **Industrial deaths** - accidents common in industrial history
- **Workers who died** - machinery accidents, collapses
- **Night watchmen** - lonely vigils, possible deaths
- **Basements** - flooded, dark, forgotten
- **Chimneys** - atmospheric, some demolished
- **Loading bays** - dark recesses
- **Broken windows** - wind sounds, movement
- **Graffiti-covered walls** - layers of history
- **Overgrown yards** - nature reclaiming

**Industrial-specific paranormal:**
- **Phantom machinery sounds** - factories running when empty
- **Workers continuing shifts** - never stopped working
- **Accidents replaying** - machinery deaths
- **Night watchmen** - still patrolling
- **Figures in windows** - upper floors, no access
- **Footsteps on metal stairs** - empty buildings
- **Whistle sounds** - factory whistles from past

**Atmospheric advantages:**
- **Isolation** - Leon alone, nobody around
- **Dark** - poor lighting, shadows
- **Echoing spaces** - sounds amplified
- **Decay** - visual atmosphere strong
- **Dangerous** - unstable buildings add physical threat
- **Weather effects** - wind through broken windows, rain through damaged roofs

**Timing:**
- **Night** - classic, deserted, dark
- **Dusk** - transition time, shadows lengthening
- **Fog** - industrial district in fog = extremely atmospheric
- **Rain** - adds to bleakness

**PENTING:** Old Industrial District perfect untuk sustained paranormal sequences. Isolation, atmosphere, history semua mendukung.

**Tetap realistis:** Jangan terlalu "creepy" - ini area industri normal yang sebagian menurun, dengan beberapa bisnis masih operational.
