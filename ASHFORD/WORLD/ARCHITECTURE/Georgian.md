## Karakter Georgian

Georgian harus terasa:
- **Formal** - order dan regularity
- **Simetris** - balanced facades
- **Proporsional** - classical proportions
- **Teratur** - systematic layout
- **Lebih tua** daripada mayoritas Victorian Ashford

**Kesan:** Refinement dan elegance, bukan ornate decoration

---

## Fasad

**Karakteristik façade Georgian:**

### Layout
- **Simetris** - perfectly balanced
- **Brickwork rapi** - regular, even courses
- **Bata merah/cokelat** - high quality bricks
- **Stone detailing** - stone surrounds, quoins, string courses
- **Fasad relatif datar** - not projecting bays
- **Proporsi klasik** - golden ratio dalam window placement
- **Horizontal emphasis** - string courses membagi lantai

### Struktur
- **Rectangular plan** - simple geometry
- **Symmetrical window arrangement** - 3 atau 5 windows across
- **Central door** - focal point
- **Even roofline** - no gables projecting

---

## Jendela

**Karakteristik jendela Georgian:**

### Sash Windows
- **Jendela geser vertikal** - double-hung sashes
- **Susunan teratur** - evenly spaced, symmetrical
- **Proporsi tinggi** - taller than wide
- **Beberapa menggunakan panel kaca kecil:**
  - Georgian awal: 12-over-12 atau 9-over-9 panes
  - Georgian akhir: 6-over-6 atau 4-over-4 panes
  - Regency: larger panes, sometimes 2-over-2
- **White painted wooden frames** - traditional
- **Set back** dari façade (reveals)

### Layout
- **Decreasing size** dengan lantai lebih tinggi (largest windows pada piano nobile)
- **Regular spacing** - matematically proportioned
- **No bay windows** - Georgian lebih formal, flat façade

---

## Pintu

**Karakteristik pintu Georgian:**

### Residential Doors
- **Pintu utama formal** - central, imposing
- **Panel kayu** - 6 panels paling umum
- **Fanlight** - semi-circular glazed panel di atas pintu (sangat karakteristik Georgian)
- **Pilaster/doorcase** pada bangunan tertentu - classical columns flanking door
- **Pediment** pada bangunan lebih grand - triangular or segmental
- **Steps** menuju raised ground floor
- **Warna:** Biasanya dark colors (hitam, dark green, dark blue)

### Door Furniture
- **Brass fittings** - knockers, handles, letter plates
- **Simple, elegant design**

---

## Atap

**Karakteristik atap Georgian:**

### Roofline
- **Slate tiles** - grey/blue slate
- **Hipped roofs** atau **gabled roofs**
- **Parapet pada beberapa bangunan** - low wall menyembunyikan roof (terutama townhouses)
- **Roofline tidak selalu terlihat dari jalan** (karena parapet)
- **Chimneys** - symmetrical placement
- **Dormers** jarang pada Georgian (lebih umum pada Regency)

### Guttering
- **Hidden gutters** di balik parapet, atau
- **Cast iron gutters** simple design
