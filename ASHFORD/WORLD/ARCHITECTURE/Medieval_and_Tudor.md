## Periode

**Medieval:** Sebelum 1485  
**Tudor:** 1485-1603

---

## Karakteristik

**Medieval:**
- Stone construction
- Timber framing
- Small windows
- Thick walls

**Tudor:**
- Timber framing (half-timbered)
- Wattle and daub
- Jettied upper floors
- Decorative timber patterns
- Brick mulai digunakan
