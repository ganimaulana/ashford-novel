## FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE

**Ashford is a river valley town, NOT a mountain town.**

Ashford berada di lembah sungai yang relatif rendah, dikelilingi oleh **layered landscape** yang naik bertahap:
- River valley (center) → Low-lying areas → Rising ground → Heights → North Downs

Ini memberikan **depth dan variety** tanpa membuat Ashford terasa seperti Alpine village.

---

## Geographic Character

### What Ashford IS

**A historic market town in a river valley**

- Founded di pertemuan sungai (Great Stour confluence)
- Surrounded by gradually rising landscape
- North Downs ridge visible di kejauhan (utara)
- Mix of flat river valley dan gentle slopes
- Close enough to coast untuk connections (30-40km)
- Woodland, heath, dan agricultural land di sekitarnya
- Old industrial areas near water
- Victorian expansion up gentle slopes

### What Ashford is NOT

- ❌ Mountain town
- ❌ Directly coastal (bukan seaside town)
- ❌ Isolated highland settlement
- ❌ Dramatic Alpine setting
- ❌ Purely flat landscape

### Geographic Identity

**"Old market town di lembah Great Stour, between the Downs and the Weald, dengan kabut sering turun dari dataran tinggi ke jalan-jalan tua."**

## The Four Geographic Zones

### 1. VALLEY FLOOR - The River Ashford

**Elevation:** Lowest (15-30m approximate)

**Character:**
- Great Stour dan tributaries
- Historic center di relatively flat ground
- Old bridges, mills, warehouses
- Prone to kabut (cold air drainage)
- Dampest areas
- Some flooding risk historically

**Includes:**
- Riverside walks
- Old industrial district
- Mill areas
- Canal remnants
- Station area (valley crossing point)
- Market Square (historic low-lying center)

**Atmospheric qualities:**
- Kabut tebal pada malam dingin
- Udara lembap
- Sound carries strangely
- Sheltered dari angin
- Colder di malam hari (cold air sinks)

---

### 2. RISING GROUND - Victorian Ashford

**Elevation:** Gentle slopes (40-70m approximate)

**Character:**
- Victorian residential expansion
- Gradual slopes naik dari valley
- Tree-lined streets
- Churches on slight rises
- Schools, civic buildings
- Mix of terraced dan detached houses

**Includes:**
- Most Victorian neighborhoods
- Ashford Grammar School
- Parish churches
- Parks pada slopes
- Connecting roads
- Mix of old dan newer development

**Atmospheric qualities:**
- Kabut thinner than valley
- Better drainage
- Some wind exposure
- Views down ke valley
- Frost less persistent

---

### 3. HIGH GROUND - Ashford Heights

**Elevation:** Higher ground (80-120m approximate)

**Character:**
- Older estates dan manor lands
- Woodland patches
- Quiet lanes
- Some Victorian villas
- Fewer developments
- More rural feeling
- Panoramic views

**Includes:**
- Ashford Heights neighborhood
- Old estates
- Woodland edges
- Hilltop landmarks
- Narrow lanes
- Scattered older properties

**Atmospheric qualities:**
- Clearer air (above kabut layer often)
- Wind exposed

### 4. NORTH DOWNS - The Ridge

**Elevation:** Highest visible ridge (150-180m+ approximate)

**Character:**

**Includes:**

- Woodland

## Water Systems

### Primary: Great Stour

**Character:**
- Main river through Ashford
- Moderate flow, not dramatic
- Brown/grey water (typical English lowland river)
- Width varies: 10-20m in places
- Multiple bridges dalam town
- Historic navigation route
- Prone to rising levels in winter

**Course through Ashford:**
- Enters dari northwest
- Meanders through valley
- Pertemuan dengan East Stour
- Multiple channels dalam town
- Old mill races
- Flows southeast menuju coast

## Climate & Weather Patterns

### General Character

**Temperate Maritime Climate - English Lowland Type**

- Mild winters (cold but not extreme)
- Cool summers
- Rainfall spread throughout year
- Changeable weather
- Frequent cloud cover
- Wind dari southwest typically
- Occasional frost
- Snow rare but possible

### Seasonal Patterns

**Autumn/Winter (Novel setting period):**
- Cold & damp
- Frequent kabut
- Grey skies common
- Rain/drizzle frequent
- Short daylight hours
- Temperature: 0-10°C typical
- Frost possible
- Wind variable

**Fog Patterns:**

Kabut TIDAK constant.

Kabut terjadi dalam kondisi:
- Clear calm nights
- After rain (high humidity)
- Autumn/winter mornings
- Cold air drainage
- Still air conditions

**Where fog is thickest:**
1. River valley (cold air sinks)
2. Low-lying areas near water
3. Sheltered valleys
4. Areas with high humidity

**Where fog is thinner/absent:**
1. Heights (above inversion layer)
2. Windward slopes
3. Exposed hilltops
4. Areas dengan air circulation

**This creates atmospheric variety naturally.**

## How This Supports Story

### Atmospheric Variety by Location

Different parts of Ashford have different feels:

**Valley floor:** Dense, damp, foggy, enclosed  
**Rising ground:** Mix of town & suburban  
**Heights:** Exposed, panoramic, isolated  
**Woods:** Dark, tangled, disorienting  
**River:** Liminal, reflective, cold  
**Downs:** Open, windswept, ancient

### Paranormal Implications

Geography naturally supports different phenomena:

- **Valley fog** - visibility, disorientation, isolation
- **Height differences** - perspective shifts, seeing things dari atas
- **Water** - reflections, sounds, boundaries
- **Woods** - getting lost, old places, isolation
- **Old town** - layered history, basements, tunnels
- **Industrial areas** - abandonment, machinery, echoes

### Leon\'s Movement

Leon can naturally move through different zones:
- Lives somewhere dalam town
- Works/studies somewhere
- Visits Arthur\'s shop
- Investigates various locations
- Gradually explores wider area
- Eventually reaches outer zones

Geography creates natural story progression.
