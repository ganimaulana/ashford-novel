## PRIMARY WATER FEATURES

### Great Stour River

**Type:** Lowland river  
**Character:** Main watercourse through Ashford

**Physical Characteristics:**
- Width: 10-20m typical
- Depth: Variable, 1-3m in town
- Flow: Moderate, increases after rain
- Bed: Mud, gravel, some weed
- Banks: Mix of natural dan reinforced
- Color: Brown-grey (typical English river)

**Course:**
- Enters Ashford dari northwest
- Meanders through valley floor
- Multiple channels dalam town area
- Meets East Stour dalam town
- Exits southeast towards coast
- Eventually reaches English Channel

**Features:**
- Old stone bridges
- Weirs (some working, some abandoned)
- Mill races
- Riverside walks
- Embankments
- Some canalised sections
- Natural bends outside town

**Wildlife:**
- Waterfowl (ducks, swans, moorhens)
- Fish (coarse fish - pike, perch, roach)
- Riverbank vegetation
- Some kingfishers
- Occasional heron

**Atmospheric Qualities:**
- Sound of flowing water
- Fog generation at night
- Reflection of lights
- Cold air near water
- Damp banks
- Smell of river water

**Paranormal Potential:**
- Reflections (distorted, wrong)
- Voices carrying over water
- Things moving upstream
- Objects appearing in water
- Fog patterns
- Missing time near water

### East Stour River

**Type:** Tributary  
**Character:** Smaller watercourse

**Physical Characteristics:**
- Width: 5-10m
- Shallower than Great Stour
- Faster flow
- More natural character

**Course:**
- Flows from north/northeast
- Joins Great Stour di Ashford
- Historically important untuk mills

**Features:**
- Less developed banks
- Some footpaths
- Overgrown sections
- Old mill sites

**Character:**

## STANDING WATER

### Ashford Lake/Reservoir

**Type:** Artificial reservoir  
**Location:** Southwest of town (3-5km)  
**Character:** Water supply reservoir dengan recreational use

**Physical Characteristics:**
- Size: Moderate (50-100 hectares estimate)
- Depth: Variable, deep in places
- Water: Usually clear to murky
- Shape: Irregular following valley
- Dam/embankment at one end

**Surroundings:**
- Woodland edges
- Walking trails (official)
- Some overgrown areas
- Old waterworks buildings
- Fishing platforms
- Small car park
- Restricted access areas

**Facilities:**
- Public footpaths
- Some picnic areas
- Bird watching points
- (No swimming - prohibited)

**Atmosphere:**
- Quiet except weekends
- Wind over water
- Bird calls
- Lapping water
- Echo from far shore
- Isolated feeling in places
- Mist over water dawn/dusk

**Paranormal Potential:**
- Submerged history (old buildings before reservoir)
- Reflections
- Sounds from across water
- Things beneath surface
- Old waterworks (abandoned sections)
- Drowning sites

## WOODLAND

### Hollow Wood (working name)

**Type:** Ancient woodland  
**Location:** West/Southwest of Ashford (3-6km)  
**Character:** Mixed deciduous woodland dengan ancient character

**Size:** Large (several hundred hectares)

**Tree Species:**
- Oak (dominant)
- Beech
- Ash
- Hazel coppice
- Some yew
- Holly understorey
- Birch in clearings

**Features:**
- Public footpaths
- Bridleways
- Old boundaries (banks, ditches)
- Ancient trees
- Clearings
- Streams
- Old quarry (disused)
- Ruins (old cottage/farm)
- Woodland pond
- Charcoal hearth sites (historical)

**Character Variation:**
- Dense areas (dark, tangled)
- Open areas (glades, clearings)
- Wet areas (muddy, streams)
- Rocky areas (old quarry)
- Edge zones (transitional)

**Access:**
- Main paths well-used
- Side paths less maintained
- Some areas difficult to reach
- Can get lost in dense sections
- Distance from town = isolation

**Atmosphere:**
- Dark under canopy
- Sound of wind in trees
- Bird calls
- Rustling leaves
- Damp earth smell
- Leaf litter
- Fallen branches
- Shadows even in day
- Very dark at night
- Mist accumulates

**Wildlife:**
- Deer (roe, fallow)
- Foxes
- Badgers
- Birds (owls, woodpeckers, corvids)
- Small mammals
- Insects

**Paranormal Potential:**
- Getting lost (spatial disorientation)
- Sounds (footsteps, voices)
- Figures between trees
- Time distortion
- Ancient sites
- Ruins
- Quarry (deep, water-filled)
- Boundaries (old, significance unknown)

## HILLS & HIGH GROUND

### North Downs

**Type:** Chalk escarpment/ridge  
**Location:** North of Ashford (5-10km to prominent areas)  
**Character:** Long ridge dengan steep northern scarp

**Elevation:** 150-180m+ (ridge top)

**Geology:**
- Chalk (underlying)
- Clay-with-flints (surface in places)
- Thin soils
- Occasional chalk exposure

**Features:**
- Ridge line visible dari town
- North Downs Way (ancient track)
- Viewpoints
- Some woodland
- Chalk grassland
- Old quarries
- Farmland
- Small settlements

**Views:**
- Dari ridge: panoramic views south
- Can see Ashford as small town
- Weald stretching south
- Coast visible on clear days
- Weather watching

**Atmosphere:**
- Exposed, windy
- Big sky
- Long views
- Sense of height
- Often clear when valley foggy
- Colder than lowland
- Dramatic light

**Access:**
- Roads up slopes
- Footpaths
- Ancient tracks
- 20-30 min drive dari Ashford center

## SEASONAL VARIATIONS

### Autumn (September-November)

**Rivers:** Flow increasing with rain  
**Woodland:** Leaves turning, falling  
**Weather:** Increasingly wet, foggy mornings  
**Daylight:** Decreasing rapidly  
**Character:** Decay, damp, atmospheric

### Winter (December-February)

**Rivers:** High flow, occasionally flooding  
**Woodland:** Bare trees, leaf litter  
**Weather:** Cold, damp, frequent fog  
**Daylight:** Short days, long nights  
**Character:** Dark, cold, stark, fog

### Spring (March-May)

**Rivers:** Flow decreasing  
**Woodland:** Bluebells, new growth  
**Weather:** Variable, clearing  
**Daylight:** Increasing  
**Character:** Renewal, clearer

### Summer (June-August)

**Rivers:** Lower flow  
**Woodland:** Full canopy  
**Weather:** Warmer, less fog  
**Daylight:** Long days  
**Character:** Green, active, clearer

**Novel setting (1816 autumn/winter) emphasizes darker, foggier characteristics.**
