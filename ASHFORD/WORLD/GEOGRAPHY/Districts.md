# Ashford Districts & Neighborhoods

## Status Dokumen

**Canon Status:** FRAMEWORK - District structure for Ashford  
**Tujuan:** Define neighborhoods, districts, dan character of different town areas  
**Terakhir diupdate:** 2026-08-26

---

## District Overview

Ashford terbagi secara informal menjadi beberapa districts berdasarkan:
- Historical development
- Topography
- Function (residential, commercial, industrial)
- Social character

Districts TIDAK memiliki boundaries resmi - ini adalah understanding lokal.

---

## Central Districts

### OLD TOWN

**Location:** Valley floor, historic center  
**Elevation:** Low (25-35m)  
**Character:** Historic commercial center

**Key Features:**
- High Street (main commercial street)
- Market Square
- Medieval street pattern
- Mix of Georgian, Victorian, Edwardian buildings
- Some older timber-framed structures
- Narrow lanes and alleys
- Banks, shops, cafes
- Town Hall area
- Parish church(es)

**Atmosphere:**
- Busy during day
- Mix of old and modern shops
- Tourist-friendly areas
- Historic pubs
- Some areas quiet at night
- Kabut dapat menggenang di narrow streets

**Age:** Medieval origins, Victorian expansion

**Social character:** Mixed - commercial, some residential above shops, tourists, locals

---

### STATION QUARTER

**Location:** Valley floor  
**Elevation:** Low (20-30m)  
**Character:** Transport hub dengan Victorian development

**Key Features:**
- Station approach roads
- Hotels near station
- Some Victorian terraces
- Commercial buildings

**Atmosphere:**
- Transit area - people passing through
- Busy during commute times
- Mix of travelers dan locals
- Some areas feel transient
- Station concourse atmosphere

**Age:** Mid-Victorian (1840s onwards)

**Social character:** Transit-focused, mix of uses

---

### RIVERSIDE

**Location:** Along Great Stour  
**Elevation:** Lowest areas (15-25m)  
**Character:** Historic waterfront, mixed use

**Key Features:**
- River walks
- Old bridges
- Former mills (some converted)
- Warehouse conversions
- Pubs along river
- Parks near water
- Some newer flats
- Weirs and locks (historical)

**Atmosphere:**
- Damp, cool
- Sound of water
- Kabut tebal di malam hari
- Popular walking area during day
- Quiet at night
- Some areas isolated
- Atmospheric lighting

**Age:** Medieval-Victorian, some modern conversions

**Social character:** Mix - residential conversions, recreation, some isolated areas

## Victorian Expansion Districts

### VICTORIA PARK AREA

**Location:** Rising ground north of center  
**Elevation:** Medium (45-65m)  
**Character:** Victorian residential, tree-lined

**Key Features:**
- Victorian park
- Terraced houses
- Tree-lined streets
- Some detached villas
- Local shops
- Primary school nearby
- Churches
- Good condition overall

**Atmosphere:**
- Residential, quiet
- Family area
- Well-maintained
- Streetlights
- Less kabut than valley

**Social character:** Middle-class residential, families, professionals

## Older/Higher Districts

### ASHFORD HEIGHTS

**Location:** High ground west/northwest  
**Elevation:** High (80-120m)  
**Character:** Old estates, hilltop development

**Key Features:**
- Large older houses
- Victorian villas
- Some Georgian/older properties
- Woodland patches
- Winding lanes
- Gardens and grounds
- Hilltop views
- More rural feeling

**Atmosphere:**
- Quiet, isolated
- Wind-exposed
- Above kabut often
- Dramatic views
- Older trees
- Some properties set back from road
- Can feel remote at night

**Age:** Mix - some pre-Victorian, Victorian villas, scattered

**Social character:** Wealthier historically, mix now, some elderly residents, professionals

---

### BELLWEATHER LANE AREA

**Location:** Edge of Old Town, transitional  
**Elevation:** Low-medium (30-45m)  
**Character:** Old lane dengan mixed buildings

**Key Features:**
- Narrow lane
- Mix of building ages
- Antique shops, curio shops
- Some residential
- Connects different areas
- Historic character
- Arthur\'s shop located here

**Atmosphere:**
- Quieter than High Street
- Historic feel
- Mix of customers
- Some buildings older than Victorian
- Shadow from taller buildings
- Kabut drifts in from valley

**Age:** Older core, Victorian additions

**Social character:** Commercial/residential mix, specialists, older businesses

## District Relationships

**Social Geography:**

- **Old Town** - Everyone passes through
- **Heights** - Viewed as "posh" historically
- **Newtown** - Working-class identity
- **Riverside** - Mixed reputation (pretty walks + isolated areas)
- **Industrial** - Avoided by many, interesting untuk urban explorers
- **Station** - Transit, not destination

**Physical Connections:**

- Roads follow valley then climb slopes
- River creates north-south division
- Height differences create distinct zones
- Footpaths connect across districts

**Fog Distribution:**

- **Thickest:** River valley, Industrial Quarter, low-lying streets
- **Moderate:** Old Town center, Station area
- **Thinner:** Victoria Park, South Ashford
- **Often clear:** Heights, Downs

## Notes for Writing

### District Names

Some names are working names ("Newtown", "Woodland Edge") - dapat diganti with more specific/local names ketika needed.

### Character Variation

Each district should feel different:
- Architecture
- Atmosphere
- Social character
- How kabut behaves
- Sound environment
- Lighting

### Leon\'s Access

Leon can realistically access:
- All central districts (walking)
- Most Victorian areas (walking/bus)
- Outer areas (bus/bike/car)
- Some areas might require specific reason

### Paranormal Potential

Each district type supports different phenomena:
- **Old Town** - Historical, objects, buildings
- **Riverside** - Water-related, reflections, voices
- **Industrial** - Abandonment, machinery, echoes
- **Heights** - Isolation, visibility, weather
- **Churchyard** - Traditional, records, spirits
