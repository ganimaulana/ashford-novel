## FUNDAMENTAL CHARACTER

**Greyhaven is a small coastal town, NOT part of Ashford.**

Greyhaven berada ~30-40km east/southeast dari Ashford, di English Channel coast.

**Relationship:** Connected to Ashford by road, but distinct identity.

---

## Geographic Location

**Position:** Kent coast, English Channel  
**Distance dari Ashford:** 30-40 km  
**Direction dari Ashford:** East/Southeast  
**Setting:** Coastal, harbour town

**Access dari Ashford:**
- Road: Coach road / turnpike

## Climate & Weather

### Coastal Character

**Wind:** Constant, stronger than Ashford  
**Salt:** In air, corrodes everything  
**Fog:** Sea fog (different character dari inland fog)  
**Rain:** Driven by wind  
**Temperature:** Moderated by sea (milder winter, cooler summer)  
**Weather:** Changeable, can see approaching dari sea

### Fog Patterns

**Sea fog (haar):**
- Rolls in dari sea
- Dense, cold, wet
- Can arrive suddenly
- Different dari inland fog
- Smells of salt
- Sound muffled
- Disorienting
- Can persist
