- **Trigger:** Ashcroft blood proximity to Bellweather House

- **Actual mechanism:** Bell tongue was removed 1805, yet it rings through unknown means
- **Circle knowledge:** Only Inner Circle knows this is ARC-001. Most members think it's legend.

**ARC-002: The Lamp (Location+Object composite, R1-Passive)**
- **Status:** Active
- **Location:** Bellweather House upper window (northwest corner)
- **Behavior:** Burns when observed by Ashcroft bloodline, extinguishes when not observed
- **Leon's interpretation:** Someone is in the house
- **Actual mechanism:** Lamp is linked to Ashcroft bloodline presence, burns with no fuel
- **Circle knowledge:** Partially known. Some believe it's maintained by caretaker.

**ARC-003: The Dream Fragment (Phenomenon, R5-Unknown)**
- **Status:** Active, recurring
- **Manifestation:** "Don't be late again" + sense of urgency + date awareness
- **Leon's interpretation:** Stress dream from travel

- **Circle knowledge:** Unknown even to Inner Circle. This is beyond their understanding.

## III. CHAPTER 2 EXPANSION — "A Town I Did Not Know"

### Canonical Narrative (LOCKED)
- Leon explores Ashford, visits Margaret's tearoom
- Meets Daniel Carter (Academy student)
- Visits Bell's Books, meets Arthur
- Finds symbol (circle+3lines+dot) in old book

### EXPANSION NOTES

#### The Symbol — Deep Context

**ARC-004: The Mark of Severance (Conceptual, R1-Passive)**
- **Official designation:** The Mark of Severance
- **Form:** Circle with three lines extending from center, one dot offset
- **Origin:** Pre-dates Aurelius (c. 1450-1500, exact date unknown)
- **Function:** Identifier, not weapon. Marks Circle property, members, safe houses
- **Propagation:** Does not spread on its own. Must be physically carved/drawn.
- **Leon's discovery:** He sees it in a 1791 history of Ashford by Arthur's careful arrangement
- **Actual locations (Ch 2):**
  - Arthur's book (page 47, corner decoration)
  - Church porch (worn stone, barely visible)
  - Old millstone near Market Square
  - Bellweather House door (hidden under ivy)
  - Helen's locket (she wears it under her collar)

**Symbol History:**
- Used by medieval guild (1450-1600)
- Adopted by early Circle (c. 1750)
- Aurelius used it as personal mark (1791-1804)
- Now serves as Circle identifier
- **Different factions interpret it differently:**
  - Preservers: Sacred legacy symbol
  - Reformers: Outdated tradition
  - Destroyers: Mark of corruption

#### Margaret's Tearoom — Intelligence Hub

**What Leon Sees:**
- Friendly tearoom owner
- Good food, reasonable prices
- Local gossip

**What's Actually Happening:**
- Margaret tracks every customer by type: gentry, merchant, worker, stranger
- She notes who sits with whom, who avoids whom
- She has informants: baker's boy, washerwoman, night watchman's wife
- **Her information flow:**
  - Morning: receives gossip from market vendors
  - Midday: serves gentry, notes their conversations
  - Evening: serves workers, notes their complaints
  - Night: compiles mental notes, shares with trusted contacts

**Margaret's Political Position:**
- Sympathizes with Reform Coalition
- Does NOT know about Severance
- Thinks Arthur is just a careful old man
- Will later be used by Leon without knowing it

#### Arthur's Shop — The Arranged Encounter

**What Leon Sees:**
- Antiquarian sells him history books
- Arthur seems knowledgeable but reserved

**What's Actually Happening:**
- Arthur received message from Patriarch (Alpha) yesterday: "New Ashcroft arrives. Prepare Test 1."
- Test 1: Let him "find" the symbol in a book
- Arthur selected 3 books with symbol:
  - 1791 Ashford history (obvious, page 47)
  - 1798 property records (subtle, margin note)
  - 1780 journal (hidden, will sell later)
- **Arthur's instructions:** "Observe. Do not guide. Report if he asks the right questions."

**Arthur's Knowledge Boundary (Layer 1+2 Bridge):**
- Knows: Symbol means Circle, Bellweather House is significant, Ashcroft name is important
- Does NOT know: Circle structure, member identities (beyond his contact), Aurelius's fate
- Reports to: One anonymous correspondent (letter drops at inn)
- **Guilt:** He likes Leon. He hates being a tool.

#### Daniel Carter — Unwitting Asset

**What Leon Sees:**
- Enthusiastic history student
- Helpful with research
- Genuinely interested in Ashford's past

**What's Actually Happening:**
- Daniel's thesis topic was "suggested" by his mentor (Circle sympathizer)
- Topic: "Property ownership patterns in Ashford 1750-1816"
- This will naturally lead him to Ashcroft family records
- **Circle plan:** Use Daniel to feed Leon information without Daniel knowing he's being used
- Daniel is CLEAN — no Circle connection, no knowledge of supernatural
- **Future tragedy:** When he discovers the truth (Ch 501), it will shatter him

#### Historical Context — Food Crisis Worsening

**Morning of Ch 2 (13 Nov 1816):**
- Baker posts notice: "Bread ration 1 loaf per family"
- Poor relief office: line of 40+ people by 8am
- Magistrate Harcourt: emergency meeting with gentry about "preservation of order"
- **Leon doesn't notice:** He's well-fed, well-dressed, insulated by his class

**The Class Divide Visible in Margaret's:**
- Gentry at front tables: white bread, butter, tea
- Workers at back tables: brown bread, water
- Leon sits at front table naturally
- **He doesn't realize:** His class position is being noted by everyone

## IV. CHAPTER 3 EXPANSION — "The Man Who Talks Too Much"

### Canonical Narrative (LOCKED)
- Daniel visits Leon's house
- They have breakfast at Margaret's
- Daniel talks about Academy, thesis, local history
- Leon finds locked door beneath stairs

### EXPANSION NOTES

#### The Locked Door — Physical Mystery

**ARC-005: The Threshold Door (Location+Object, R2-Reactive)**
- **Location:** No. 13 Bellweather Lane, beneath main stairs
- **Physical description:** Oak door, iron handle, no visible lock, no keyhole
- **Behavior:** Cannot be opened by force. Requires specific key (1798 key, Ch 11)
- **Purpose:** Entrance to Aurelius's basement archive
- **Construction date:** c. 1792 (same time as house renovations)
- **Circle knowledge:** Inner Circle knows it exists. Most don't know what's behind it.

**Helen's Reaction (Subtle):**
- Leon mentions the locked door at breakfast
- Helen: "That door has been locked since before I came to this house."
- **What she doesn't say:** She has the key. She's waiting to see if Leon finds his own.
- **Her role:** Gatekeeper. She tests whether Leon is worthy of what's below.

#### Daniel's Thesis — The Planted Research

**Daniel's Research Topics (Suggested by Circle-influenced Mentor):**
- Property ownership patterns 1750-1816
- Focus on "disappeared" properties
- Anomalies in parish records
- **Why this topic?:** Circle wants Leon to learn through Daniel's research
- **Daniel's thesis will "accidentally" discover:**
  - Ashcroft properties systematically erased from records
  - Three names appearing repeatedly: M.R., J.T., M.B.
  - 1798 "key found" notice

**Daniel's Mentor (Future Character):**
- Dr. Edward Thornley, Academy senior tutor
- Age: 60s, historian, antiquarian
- **Secret:** Outer Circle Correspondent, like Arthur
- **Role:** Guides Daniel's research to benefit Circle's Leon-test
- Does NOT tell Daniel about Circle
- Will appear in Ch 15-20 as secondary character

#### The Symbol — Second Sighting

**Leon finds symbol in second book:**
- 1780s journal, page 113
- Marginal note: "Mark of the old guild, still seen on Bellweather properties"
- Leon starts to notice a pattern

**What Leon Doesn't Know:**
- The journal was placed in his house by Helen (she rearranged his bookshelf)
- She is accelerating his discovery
- **Helen's test:** Will he investigate or dismiss it?

#### Margaret's Tearoom — Scene 2

**Political Tension Rising:**
- Morning of Ch 3: two men arguing about bread prices
- Margaret diffuses it quickly
- Leon notices: "Margaret has authority in this room"
- **What's happening:** One man is Crown Conservative, one is Radical sympathizer
- Margaret keeps the peace because her tearoom is NEUTRAL ground
- **Future significance:** This neutrality makes her tearoom valuable to all factions

**Constable Voss — First Appearance (Background):**
- Walks past tearoom, looks in window
- Sees Leon and Daniel
- Mental note: "Newcomer. Gentleman. Talking with Academy student. Watch."
- Does NOT enter
- **Voss's position:** Parish Constable, reports to Magistrate Harcourt
- Does NOT know about Severance yet
- Suspects something is happening in Ashford (increased political tension)

**Helen's Test:**
- Leon confronts her about the house's history

- "Your relative owned this property 1791-1804"

- Reports: "He accepts the inheritance narrative. He does not suspect deeper truth."

### Ch 8: The Library Discovery
**The "Removed from Active List" Document:**
- Leon finds Bellweather House papers in Ashford Library
- Page stamped: "REMOVED FROM ACTIVE LIST 1810"
- No explanation given
- **Actual story:** Circle member in government systematically erased property from official records
- **Purpose:** Hide Bellweather House from routine investigation

**Second Empty Envelope:**
- Appears at Leon's house while he's at library
- Helen "finds" it, gives to Leon that evening
- **Circle's message:** "We know you're investigating. We're watching."

**Leon's Decision:**
- Commits to full investigation (end of Ch 8)
- **Circle's assessment:** "Test phase complete. He is suitable. Begin initiation preparation."
