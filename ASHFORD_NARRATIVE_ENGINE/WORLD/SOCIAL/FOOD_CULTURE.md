# Food Culture — Ashford, 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

Food in 1816 Ashford is determined by class, season, and availability. The diet ranges from plain and basic for the working class to elaborate and varied for the elite. Food is a marker of social status and a central part of hospitality and celebration.

---

## Everyday Food

### Working-Class
- **Staples:** Bread, potatoes, porridge (oatmeal), cheese, ale.
- **Protein:** Salted meat (bacon, ham), occasionally fresh meat (beef, mutton) on special days; fish from the river.
- **Vegetables:** Root vegetables (turnips, carrots, onions) in season; cabbage; peas.
- **Preservation:** Pickling, salting, smoking.
- **Meals:** Breakfast: porridge or bread; Dinner (midday): bread, cheese, ale; Supper: similar to dinner.

### Middle-Class
- **Staples:** Bread, potatoes, meat (more frequent), vegetables; tea or coffee (imported).
- **Protein:** Roast beef, mutton, pork, chicken; game (if available).
- **Vegetables:** A greater variety; perhaps some imported items.
- **Dessert:** Puddings, pies, fruit.
- **Meals:** Breakfast: bread, butter, perhaps eggs; Dinner (midday): a cooked meal; Supper: lighter meal.

### Upper-Class
- **Staples:** Fine bread, roast meats, vegetables, sauces, imported foods, tea, coffee, wine.
- **Protein:** Variety of meats (venison, game, beef), fish, poultry.
- **Vegetables:** Many varieties; often served with butter or sauces.
- **Desserts:** Elaborate puddings, cakes, confections, fruit, cheese.
- **Meals:** Breakfast: bread, eggs, meat; Dinner (mid-afternoon): a large meal of many courses; Supper: lighter, often with wine and sweets.

---

## Tavern Food

- **Common:** Meat pies, bread and cheese, soup, stews.
- **Special:** Roast beef, mutton chops, fish.
- **Cost:** Affordable for working men.

---

## Festival Food

- **Christmas:** Roast goose, beef, mince pies, plum pudding, wassail.
- **Harvest Home:** Feast of roast meats, pies, ale, bread, fruit.
- **Easter:** Roast lamb, eggs (decorated), simnel cake.
- **May Day:** Bread, cheese, ale, cakes.

---

## Seasonal Food

- **Spring:** Fresh greens, lamb, early peas, new potatoes.
- **Summer:** Berries, salad greens, beans, fruit, fresh fish.
- **Autumn:** Root vegetables, apples, pears, game, preserved food.
- **Winter:** Root vegetables, dried meat, preserved foods, ale.

---

## Preservation

- **Salting:** For meat and fish.
- **Smoking:** For meat and fish.
- **Pickling:** For vegetables.
- **Drying:** For fruit and herbs.
- **Dairying:** Butter, cheese.

---

## Social Meaning

- **Status:** The type of food and the way it is served indicate social rank. The upper class has elaborate meals with many courses; the working class has simple, hearty meals.
- **Hospitality:** Offering food is a way to show welcome and respect.
- **Charity:** The wealthy often give food to the poor, especially at festivals.
