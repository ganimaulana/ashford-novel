# Seasonal Social Rhythm — Ashford, 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

The rhythm of life in Ashford is deeply tied to the seasons. Weather, daylight, work, social gatherings, festivals, and even illness all follow an annual cycle.

---

## Spring (March–May)

- **Weather:** Increasingly mild; rains; flowers bloom.
- **Work:** Plowing, sowing, lambing; preparing for the growing season.
- **Markets:** Spring fairs; hiring fairs; increased trade.
- **Social Gatherings:** Rogation processions, Easter observances, May Day celebrations.
- **Daylight:** Lengthening; days become longer.
- **Nightlife:** More evening socializing.
- **Illness:** Spring colds; seasonal allergies.

---

## Summer (June–August)

- **Weather:** Warm; long days; occasional thunderstorms.
- **Work:** Haymaking, shearing, harvest (beginning in August).
- **Markets:** Summer fairs; increased trade in livestock and produce.
- **Social Gatherings:** Midsummer Eve, Summer Fair, St. Bartholomew's Day; outdoor entertainments.
- **Daylight:** Longest days.
- **Nightlife:** Active; evening gatherings, outdoor dances.
- **Illness:** Summer sicknesses; dysentery, heat exhaustion.

---

## Autumn (September–November)

- **Weather:** Cooling; leaves fall; rainy.
- **Work:** Harvesting; threshing; fruit picking; preserving.
- **Markets:** Harvest Home, Michaelmas Fair, Autumn Market; hiring fairs.
- **Social Gatherings:** Harvest festivals; Michaelmas festivities; Bonfire Night (5 November).
- **Daylight:** Shortening; days become shorter.
- **Nightlife:** Less outdoor activity; more indoor tavern gatherings.
- **Illness:** Colds, seasonal influenza.

---

## Winter (December–February)

- **Weather:** Cold; short days; rain and occasional snow.
- **Work:** Slaughtering; butchery; making preserves; indoor crafts.
- **Markets:** Winter Market; pre-Christmas trade.
- **Social Gatherings:** Christmas, New Year's, Twelfth Night; indoor gatherings; Yule logs; feasting.
- **Daylight:** Shortest days.
- **Nightlife:** Evening activities primarily indoors; taverns are busy.
- **Illness:** Colds, influenza, fevers, ague; winter mortality higher.

---

## The 1816 Exception

The year 1816 is known as the "Year Without a Summer" due to volcanic eruptions. This may affect Ashford with:
- **Colder weather** in summer.
- **Crop failures** and food shortages.
- **Social unrest** due to scarcity.
- **Increased poverty** and charity needs.
- **A darker, more somber atmosphere.**

This can be a plot element, but not every unusual weather event must be supernatural.
