# Taverns, Inns, and Public Houses — Ashford, 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

Taverns, inns, and public houses are the social hubs of Ashford, serving as places for drinking, eating, accommodation, entertainment, and information exchange. They are class-stratified, with each establishment attracting a different clientele.

---

## Major Establishments

### 1. The King's Head
- **Location:** High Street, near Market Square.
- **Type:** Inn (accommodation, meals, drinks).
- **Clientele:** Gentry, clergy, wealthier travellers, merchants.
- **Price Tier:** Expensive.
- **Opening Pattern:** Open all day; busy in the evening.
- **Social Reputation:** Respectable, high status.
- **Information Flow:** A place for deals, political discussions, and private meetings.
- **Risks:** Gossip, debts, and occasionally a scandal.
- **Possible Plot:** A nobleman has a secret meeting; a letter is left for someone; a traveller dies mysteriously.

### 2. The Bell
- **Location:** Near the church.
- **Type:** Public house (drinks, some food).
- **Clientele:** Middle class, shopkeepers, artisans, farmers.
- **Price Tier:** Moderate.
- **Opening Pattern:** Busy in the evenings; also mid-afternoon for drinkers.
- **Social Reputation:** Respectable, but not elite.
- **Information Flow:** A hub for gossip, business deals, and political discussion among the middle class.
- **Risks:** Arguments, debts.
- **Possible Plot:** A conspiracy discovered in a back room; a vendor overhears a plot.

### 3. The Queen's Arms
- **Location:** Near the edge of town, on the road to London.
- **Type:** Inn (accommodation, drinks, food).
- **Clientele:** Travellers, coachmen, carriers, some locals.
- **Price Tier:** Moderate.
- **Opening Pattern:** Busy during stagecoach arrivals and departures.
- **Social Reputation:** Mixed; acceptable but not fashionable.
- **Information Flow:** Travelers bring news; letters and packages are exchanged.
- **Risks:** Theft, highwaymen, unreliable innkeepers.
- **Possible Plot:** A traveller arrives with a dangerous secret; a package is misdelivered.

### 4. The Rusty Shovel
- **Location:** Near the Market Square, down a side street.
- **Type:** Tavern (drinks, basic food, games).
- **Clientele:** Working class, laborers, servants, soldiers, suspected criminals.
- **Price Tier:** Low.
- **Opening Pattern:** Opens early; busy from late afternoon to late night.
- **Social Reputation:** Low; a place of drinking, gambling, and occasional violence.
- **Information Flow:** A source of underworld rumors, criminal activity, and secrets.
- **Risks:** Fighting, theft, arrest.
- **Possible Plot:** An informant is found here; a criminal gang meets; a murder occurs.

### 5. The Mermaid's Rest
- **Location:** Near the river, by the old wharf.
- **Type:** Pub and occasional lodging.
- **Clientele:** Riverboat men, fishermen, laborers, smugglers.
- **Price Tier:** Low.
- **Opening Pattern:** Variable; often busy at night.
- **Social Reputation:** Disreputable; known for illicit activity.
- **Information Flow:** Smuggling, illicit trade, secrets of the river.
- **Risks:** Violence, arrest, secrets revealed.
- **Possible Plot:** A smuggling operation; a witness hiding here; a body found in the river.

---

## Functions of Taverns

- **Drinking:** Ale, beer, porter, gin, wine, spirits.
- **Food:** Simple meals, cheese, bread, meat pies.
- **Accommodation:** Bed for the night, often shared rooms.
- **Entertainment:** Music, singing, storytelling, card games, dice, darts, billiards.
- **Employment Information:** Many jobs are found through word of mouth in taverns.
- **Gossip:** A primary source of news and rumor.
- **Political Discussion:** Spaces for political debate, radical pamphlets, and conspiracies.
- **Merchant Networking:** Deals are struck over a drink.
- **Criminal Contact:** A place to hire thieves, find informants, or engage in illicit trade.
- **Romantic Meetings:** Secret assignations often occur in taverns.
- **Private Negotiations:** Back rooms are used for private business.
