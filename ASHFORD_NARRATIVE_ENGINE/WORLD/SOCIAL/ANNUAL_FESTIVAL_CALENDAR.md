# Annual Festival Calendar — Ashford, 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

Ashford's annual calendar blends agricultural, religious, civic, and commercial traditions. Festivals provide opportunities for trade, socialising, and plot development.

---

## Winter

| Festival | Date | Purpose | Plot Functions |
|----------|------|---------|----------------|
| Christmas (Yule-tide) | 25 Dec | Family, charity, relief | Family secrets, charity fraud, mysterious visitor |
| New Year's Day | 1 Jan | Renewal, resolution | New beginnings, contract renewals |
| Twelfth Night | 6 Jan | End of Christmas, revelry | Disguise, mistaken identity, hidden clues |

## Small Recurring Rituals

- **Weekly Market** (every Thursday) – gossip, news, regular commerce.
- **Sunday Church** – social and information hub for all classes.
- **Monthly Tavern Socials** – gatherings for specific groups (merchants, farmers, radicals).
- **Reading Evenings** – private/public readings and discussion.
