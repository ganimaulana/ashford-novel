# Entertainment in Ashford — 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

Entertainment spans theatre, music, dancing, gambling, sports, travelling shows, and intellectual pursuits. Access is determined by class, gender, and wealth.

---

## Theatre & Performance

- **Theatre:** Middle/upper classes; comedies, tragedies, melodramas. Plot: performance mirrors real events, actor's secret.
- **Opera:** Upper class; social occasion. Plot: private conversation in a box.
- **Puppet Theatre:** All classes, especially children; at fairs. Plot: satire of local figure.

---

## Music & Dancing

- **Balls:** Upper/middle; country dances, quadrilles. Plot: courtship, scandal, secret meeting.
- **Public Concerts:** Middle/upper; orchestral, vocal. Plot: musician's secret, coded message in music.
- **Tavern Music:** Working/middle; folk songs, ballads. Plot: ballad reveals local story, drunken confession.

---

## Gambling & Games

- **Card Games (whist, cribbage, faro):** All classes, high stakes for wealthy. Plot: debt of honor, forged card, den raid.
- **Dice Games:** Working/middle; at taverns, fairs. Plot: rigged game, quarrel.
- **Betting:** Horse racing, boxing, cockfighting. Plot: fixed race, bribe.

---

## Sports

- **Horse Racing:** All classes; gambling, social display. Plot: jockey bribed, horse stolen.
- **Boxing:** Working/middle; prize‑fighting. Plot: fixed fight, fighter knows a secret.
- **Cockfighting:** Working/middle; gambling, rivalry. Plot: dispute over a bird, clue in a pit.
- **Hunting:** Upper class; sport and politics. Plot: death during hunt, poacher's grievance.

---

## Travelling Shows

- **Circus:** All classes; equestrian acts, acrobats, animals. Plot: performer with a secret, animal escapes.
- **Magic & Illusion:** All classes; sleight of hand, conjuring. Plot: seemingly supernatural trick, magician's secret.
- **Fortune‑tellers:** All classes; at fairs, private. Plot: fortune reveals hidden truth, charlatan exposed.

---

## Reading & Intellectual Entertainment

- **Circulating Libraries:** Middle/upper; novels, travelogues. Plot: hidden note in a book, library holds records.
- **Newspapers:** Middle/upper; political news, gossip. Plot: article hints at conspiracy, letter with hidden meaning.
- **Debating Societies:** Middle/upper, mostly male; political/philosophical. Plot: debate becomes conflict, secret society.
- **Lectures:** Middle/upper; science, history, philosophy. Plot: lecturer with a secret, dangerous idea spread.
