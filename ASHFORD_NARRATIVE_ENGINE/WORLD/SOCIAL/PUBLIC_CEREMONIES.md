# Public Ceremonies — Ashford, 1816

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be confirmed as canon after review.

## Overview

Public ceremonies in Ashford serve to reinforce community identity, display power, and commemorate important events. They are opportunities for mass gatherings, political theater, and information exchange.

---

## Civic Ceremonies

### Mayoral Election
- **Occurrence:** Annually (if Ashford has a mayor).
- **Process:** Electoral meeting; proclamation; procession; banquet.
- **Public Spaces:** Town Hall, Market Square.
- **Possible Plot:** An election dispute; a candidate with a secret; a bribe.

### Royal Proclamations
- **Occurrence:** Upon the death of a monarch, a new reign, or significant royal events.
- **Process:** Public reading; ringing of bells; celebrations.
- **Public Spaces:** Market Square, church.
- **Possible Plot:** A proclamation that sparks unrest; a royal message that has hidden implications.

---

## Religious Ceremonies

### Rogation Processions
- **Occurrence:** Late April/May.
- **Process:** Procession around parish boundaries; prayers; blessing of fields.
- **Public Spaces:** Fields, roads, boundaries.
- **Possible Plot:** Boundary disputes; discovery of hidden markers; a person who disappears during the procession.

### Easter Services
- **Occurrence:** Easter Sunday.
- **Process:** Church services; communion; special music.
- **Public Spaces:** The church.
- **Possible Plot:** A confession; a secret revealed; a public scandal.

### Harvest Thanksgiving
- **Occurrence:** Late September/early October.
- **Process:** Church service; procession with harvest offerings; feast.
- **Public Spaces:** Church, village green.
- **Possible Plot:** A dispute over tithes; a hidden stash discovered.

---

## Commemorations

### Bonfire Night (Guy Fawkes Day)
- **Occurrence:** 5 November.
- **Process:** Bonfire; effigy burning; fireworks; feasting.
- **Public Spaces:** Fields, common land.
- **Possible Plot:** A plot discovered in the crowd; an act of arson; a political protest.

### Military Returns
- **Occurrence:** When soldiers return from war.
- **Process:** Parade; speeches; toasts.
- **Public Spaces:** High Street, Market Square.
- **Possible Plot:** A soldier who has a secret; a dispute over a war pension.

---

## Charitable and Public Announcements

- **Poor Relief:** Distribution of alms; often on public days.
- **Auctions:** Public auctions of property, livestock, or goods.
- **Readings of Wills:** Sometimes in public to settle disputes.

---

## Functions of Public Ceremonies

- **Worldbuilding:** Show the community's values and structure.
- **Political Theatre:** Display of power and authority.
- **Information Distribution:** Announcements and proclamations spread news.
- **Crowd Scenes:** Large gatherings provide opportunities for characters to meet, observe, or act.
- **Mystery Settings:** Ceremonies can be the backdrop for crime, revelation, or conspiracy.
