## I. CORE PRINCIPLE

Leon operates on **multiple simultaneous layers**, and different characters know different layers. No one outside Leon's immediate secret circle knows the full picture. The layers are:

1. **Leon as ordinary citizen** — public persona
2. **Leon as paranormal investigator** — surface hidden activity
3. **Leon as Leon Ashcroft in the secret organization** — hidden identity
4. **Leon as Aurelius Ashcroft** — full legacy/heir

Each layer is **compartmentalized**. Characters outside a layer cannot see inside it.

---

## II. THE FOUR LAYERS

### LAYER 1 — ORDINARY CITIZEN (Public Persona)

**What Leon appears to be:**
- A newcomer to Ashford
- A gentleman of modest means
- A researcher or student
- A tenant at No. 13 Bellweather Lane
- Friendly with Daniel, Margaret, and Arthur

**What Leon does at this layer:**
- Eats at Margaret's tearoom
- Attends Academy lectures or studies
- Buys books from Arthur
- Walks the town, takes notes
- Corresponds with Pembroke & Clarke
- Pays rent and bills

**Who knows this layer:**
- Everyone in Ashford
- Daniel, Margaret, Arthur (as acquaintances)
- The constable, postmaster, vicar
- All townspeople

**What characters at this layer believe:**
- Leon is a quiet, curious newcomer
- He is researching local history as a hobby
- He has some connection to the Ashcroft name (inheritance)
- He is not dangerous, not powerful, not connected to anything hidden

**Restrictions:**
- Daniel and Margaret must NEVER see evidence that Leon operates on any other layer
- They may suspect something is odd, but they cannot know the truth
- They are **permanent surface contacts** — they will never be initiated

---

### LAYER 2 — PARANORMAL INVESTIGATOR (Surface Hidden Activity)

**What Leon appears to be:**
- A man interested in old houses, local history, and unusual stories
- Someone who asks questions about the bell, the empty house, the symbol
- A person who spends time in the library archives and town hall records
- A tenant who has found a locked door and an old key

**What Leon does at this layer:**
- Investigates Bellweather House through official records
- Researches the symbol, the 1798 key, the address system
- Asks questions about the bell, the lamp, the empty envelope
- Visits the library, solicitor's office, parish records
- Takes extensive notes in his notebook

**Who knows this layer:**
- Arthur Edmund Bell (partial)
- Thomas Wren (partial, before his death)
- Possibly the constable (suspicious)
- Members of the Hidden Circle (they observe but do not reveal themselves)

**What Arthur knows at Layer 2:**
- Leon is investigating something real and significant
- Leon has found the symbol in multiple places
- Leon has evidence of deliberate record tampering
- Leon possesses an old key and a notebook
- Arthur knows Bellweather House history, the symbol's origin, and the Circle's existence
- Arthur does NOT know Leon's full blood connection to Aurelius
- Arthur does NOT know Leon has been contacted by the Circle
- Arthur may suspect Leon is being watched

**Restrictions:**
- Arthur does NOT know the Circle's structure, membership, or current activities
- Arthur does NOT know Leon has been approached by Circle members
- Arthur believes Leon is an independent researcher who stumbled onto something real
- Arthur will not introduce Leon to the Circle — that decision belongs to others

---

### LAYER 3 — LEON AS LEON ASHCROFT IN THE SECRET ORGANIZATION

**What Leon is:**
- An initiated member of the Hidden Circle
- Recognized as an Ashcroft heir
- Given the name "Leon Ashcroft" within the organization
- Granted limited access to Circle archives and contacts
- Tested and observed by senior members

**What Leon does at this layer:**
- Attends meetings (disguised as ordinary business)
- Receives coded messages and dead drops
- Accesses restricted documents through Circle channels
- Meets with correspondents who know his Circle identity
- Uses Circle resources to investigate Bellweather House
- Is evaluated by the Inner Circle

**Who knows this layer:**
- Inner Circle members who deal with Ashcroft matters
- Specific correspondents assigned to Leon
- Senior members who authorized his initiation
- NOT Daniel, Margaret, or any surface contact

**What Circle members know at Layer 3:**
- Leon is an Ashcroft descendant
- Leon has been accepted as a member
- Leon is investigating Bellweather House/Aurelius matters
- Leon has shown unusual deductive ability
- They do NOT know Leon's full capabilities, his connection to Aurelius's legacy, or his ultimate role
- They do NOT know he is being prepared for something beyond normal membership

**Restrictions:**
- Circle members do NOT know Leon is being positioned as Aurelius's successor
- Circle members do NOT know about Layer 4
- Leon must keep Layer 3 completely separate from Layers 1 and 2
- If Daniel or Margaret discover Layer 3, Leon's cover is blown and they are in danger

---

### LAYER 4 — LEON AS AURELIUS ASHCROFT (Full Legacy)

**What Leon becomes:**
- The recognized heir of Aurelius Ashcroft
- Holder of Aurelius's legacy, knowledge, and possibly power
- The person who can access what Aurelius left behind
- The one who must make decisions about the Circle's future

**What Leon does at this layer:**
- Accesses Bellweather House basement and hidden chambers
- Reads Aurelius's private documents and journals
- Understands the symbol's true meaning
- Wields influence over Circle factions
- Makes choices about the legacy: preserve, reform, or destroy

**Who knows this layer:**
- Only the Inner Circle's oldest members
- Possibly one or two people who knew Aurelius personally
- NOT Daniel, Margaret, Arthur
- NOT most Circle members

**Restrictions:**
- Layer 4 is the endgame reveal
- Leon himself does not know he is reaching Layer 4 until it happens
- The reader discovers Layer 4 alongside Leon
- Layer 4 cannot be revealed to anyone outside the immediate circle without catastrophic consequences

## IV. COMPARTMENTALIZATION RULES

### Rule 1: Physical Separation
Leon never brings Layer 2, 3, or 4 materials to Layer 1 locations.
- No Circle documents in his house where Daniel might visit
- No secret messages in his notebook when Margaret can see it
- No Circle symbols displayed publicly

### Rule 2: Temporal Separation
Leon never performs Layer 2, 3, or 4 activities during Layer 1 time slots.
- He investigates at specific times when he is "supposed" to be at the library or walking
- He meets Circle contacts under the guise of ordinary errands
- He keeps strict schedules to maintain cover

### Rule 3: Social Separation
Leon never introduces Layer 2, 3, or 4 contacts to Layer 1 contacts.
- Daniel never meets Arthur in a context where Arthur might reveal Circle knowledge
- Margaret never encounters anyone from the Circle
- If a Circle member appears in public, they are unknown to Daniel and Margaret

### Rule 4: Information Separation
Leon never shares Layer 2, 3, or 4 information with Layer 1 contacts.
- He does not tell Daniel about the Circle
- He does not tell Margaret about the locked door
- He does not tell either of them about the envelope sender
- He may share small, safe fragments of Layer 2 with Arthur only

### Rule 5: Emotional Separation
Leon's emotional investment in each layer differs:
- Layer 1: Casual, friendly, surface-level relationships
- Layer 2: Focused, secretive, determined
- Layer 3: Calculated, testing, observant
- Layer 4: Personal, legacy-driven, transformative

## V. CHARACTER-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARIES

### Daniel James Carter — PERMANENT SURFACE CONTACT

**Knows (Layer 1 only):**
- Leon is a newcomer researching local history
- Leon is interested in old buildings and documents
- Leon is friendly and asks questions
- Leon is an Ashcroft by name, with some inheritance

**Does Not Know:**
- The Hidden Circle exists
- Leon has been contacted by unknown senders
- Leon has found a locked door and an old key
- Leon is investigating paranormal/supernatural elements
- Leon has been initiated into any secret organization
- Leon's connection to Aurelius is anything more than genealogical

**Behavior toward Leon:**
- Treats Leon as a friend and fellow history enthusiast
- Helps Leon with academic research
- Shares local knowledge freely
- Would be devastated if he discovered he was kept in the dark
- Cannot be used as a witness or confidant for hidden activities

**Narrative function:**
- Provides access to Academy archives and library
- Represents the "ordinary world" Leon is trying to protect
- His ignorance is his protection — if he knew, he would be in danger

---

### Margaret Whitmore — PERMANENT SURFACE CONTACT

**Knows (Layer 1 only):**
- Leon is a tenant on Bellweather Lane
- Leon asks questions about the town
- Leon is polite and keeps to himself
- Leon is interested in local history

**Does Not Know:**
- The Hidden Circle exists
- Leon has received anonymous letters
- Leon has seen the lamp in Bellweather House
- Leon is investigating the address system, the symbol, the bell
- Leon is anything other than a quiet researcher

**Behavior toward Leon:**
- Treats Leon as a customer and acquaintance
- Shares gossip and local observations
- Provides tea, breakfast, and conversation
- Has sharp intuition but no evidence of hidden activities
- Would be furious if she discovered she was being used as cover
- Cannot be an informant for the Circle or for Leon's hidden activities

**Narrative function:**
- Social hub of Ashford
- Represents the town's ordinary intelligence network
- Her ignorance keeps her safe and keeps Leon's cover intact

---

### Arthur Edmund Bell — BRIDGE CHARACTER (Layer 1 + Partial Layer 2)

**Knows:**
- Layer 1: Leon is a customer and researcher
- Partial Layer 2: Leon is investigating Bellweather House, has found the symbol, has evidence of record tampering
- Arthur knows the symbol's historical significance
- Arthur knows Bellweather House's layered history
- Arthur knows the town's address system has anomalies
- Arthur may suspect Leon is being watched

**Does Not Know:**
- The Hidden Circle's structure, membership, or current activities
- That Leon has been contacted by Circle members
- That Leon has been initiated
- Leon's full blood connection to Aurelius
- The supernatural elements (or believes them to be legend only)

**Behavior toward Leon:**
- Acts as a mentor for historical research
- Provides books, maps, and documents
- Tests Leon's discretion and trustworthiness
- Will not reveal what he knows about the Circle
- May drop hints to gauge Leon's reaction
- Believes Leon is an independent investigator who stumbled onto something real

**Narrative function:**
- The only surface contact with partial hidden knowledge
- Represents the "old guard" who knows history but chooses silence
- His partial knowledge creates tension — he could help more, but won't

---

### Helen Margaret Ashcroft — COMPLEX COMPARTMENTALIZED (All Layers, Partial)

**Knows:**
- Layer 1: She is housekeeper at No. 13 Bellweather Lane
- Layer 2: She knows about the locked door, the house's history, the symbol
- Partial Layer 3: She knows the Circle exists, has watched Leon being observed
- Partial Layer 4: She knows the basement exists and what it contains (partially)

**Does Not Know:**
- The full extent of Leon's initiation
- The Circle's internal politics
- What Leon will ultimately choose to do with the legacy
- Whether Leon is truly Aurelius's heir or a pawn

**Behavior toward Leon:**
- Maintains strict professional distance
- Watches and waits
- Tests Leon's character through small provocations
- Will not reveal her full identity or knowledge
- Her loyalty is to the house and its history, not necessarily to Leon

**Narrative function:**
- The gatekeeper to the physical mystery (the house, the basement)
- Represents the past's hold on the present
- Her partial knowledge creates dramatic irony — she knows more than Leon but less than the Circle

## VI. CIRCLE MEMBER KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARIES

### Outer Circle / Observers / Correspondents

**Knows:**
- Leon is a new Ashcroft who has arrived in Ashford
- Leon has been initiated as a member
- Leon is investigating Bellweather House matters
- Leon shows unusual deductive ability

**Does Not Know:**
- Leon's full capabilities or potential
- That Leon is being positioned as Aurelius's successor
- Layer 4 details
- The full scope of the Circle's history with Aurelius

**Behavior:**
- Treat Leon as a promising but untested member
- Report Leon's activities to superiors
- Follow directives regarding Leon's access
- Do NOT reveal Circle structure or history to Leon beyond what is authorized

---

### Inner Circle

**Knows:**
- Leon's blood connection to Aurelius
- Leon's initiation and testing status
- The nature of Bellweather House and its significance
- The symbol's meaning and history
- The Circle's factions and conflicts (partial)
- That Leon may be the heir to Aurelius's legacy

**Does Not Know:**
- Layer 4 details — they do not know what Leon will become
- The full contents of Bellweather basement
- Aurelius's true fate or purpose
- Whether the Circle should support Leon, use him, or eliminate him

**Behavior:**
- Observe and evaluate Leon
- Test Leon through cases and challenges
- Compete for influence over Leon
- Hide their own factions' agendas from Leon and from each other

## VII. LAYER TRANSITION RULES

### Transition 1→2: When Leon Begins Investigating

**Trigger:** Leon finds the symbol in Arthur's book (Ch 2) and decides to investigate.

**Visible to others:**
- Daniel notices Leon asks more questions
- Margaret notices Leon is "different" — more focused, less casual
- Arthur notices Leon is serious about the research

**Not visible:**
- The full scope of Leon's investigation
- The anonymous letters
- The locked door

**Risk:** If Daniel or Margaret connect the dots, they become threats to the cover.

---

### Transition 2→3: When Leon Enters the Circle

**Trigger:** Leon is contacted by Circle members, tested, and accepted.

**Visible to others:**
- Leon has less time for ordinary activities
- Leon seems more tired, more distant
- Leon receives strange letters or visits unknown people
- Arthur notices Leon is being watched

**Not visible:**
- The Circle's existence
- Leon's membership
- The tests and evaluations

**Risk:** Daniel and Margaret must interpret Leon's absence as ordinary research fatigue. Arthur may suspect but cannot confirm.

---

### Transition 3→4: When Leon Becomes the Heir

**Trigger:** Leon accesses Bellweather basement, reads Aurelius's private journals, and accepts the legacy.

**Visible to others:**
- Leon changes — becomes more confident, more purposeful
- The house at No. 13 changes — the locked door opens, the basement is accessible
- The town's hidden structures react to Leon's new status

**Not visible:**
- The full meaning of the transition
- Leon's new powers or responsibilities
- The Circle's reaction to Leon's ascension

**Risk:** This is the point where compartmentalization may break. Daniel and Margaret will see that Leon is no longer the man they thought he was. Arthur will realize he underestimated the situation.

## VIII. WHAT EACH LAYER "SEES" OF THE MYSTERY

### Layer 1 Sees:
- A quiet town with old buildings
- A newcomer who asks questions
- Strange local stories (the bell, the empty house)
- A man who is polite but distant

### Layer 2 Sees:
- Evidence of deliberate historical concealment
- A symbol that appears in multiple places
- Records that have been altered or erased
- An empty house that is not empty
- A key that opens something unknown
- An anonymous correspondent who knows Leon's movements

### Layer 3 Sees:
- The Hidden Circle as a real, active organization
- Other members, factions, and agendas
- The Circle's resources and archives
- The fact that Leon was chosen, not random
- The Circle's interest in Bellweather House and Aurelius

### Layer 4 Sees:
- Aurelius's true purpose and fate
- The basement and what it contains
- The symbol's origin and power
- Leon's own heritage and capabilities
- The Circle's origins and ultimate goal
- The choice: preserve, reform, or destroy

## IX. WHY DANIEL AND MARGARET CANNOT KNOW

### Practical Reasons:
- If they know about the Circle, they become targets
- If they know about the paranormal, they cannot maintain their ordinary lives
- If they know Leon's true identity, their relationship with him changes permanently
- Their safety depends on plausible deniability

### Narrative Reasons:
- Daniel and Margaret represent the "ordinary world" Leon is fighting to protect
- Their ignorance creates dramatic irony — the reader knows more than they do
- Their surface-level help is more useful than their compromised involvement
- They humanize Leon by reminding him (and the reader) what is at stake

### Thematic Reasons:
- Not everyone needs to know the truth
- Some people are happier and safer in the dark
- Leon's isolation as the sole bearer of the full secret is thematically important
- The cost of knowledge is a central theme — Leon pays it; Daniel and Margaret do not

---

## X. WHAT ARTHUR CAN AND CANNOT DO

### Can Do (Layer 2):
- Provide historical documents and maps
- Explain the symbol's known history
- Show Leon old records about Bellweather House
- Warn Leon indirectly ("some doors are better left closed")
- Test Leon's trustworthiness
- Observe Leon's progress

### Cannot Do (Beyond Layer 2):
- Introduce Leon to Circle members
- Explain the Circle's structure or purpose
- Reveal the full extent of the Ashcroft connection
- Access Circle-only archives
- Participate in Circle activities with Leon
- Tell Daniel or Margaret anything about Leon's hidden activities

### Arthur's Dilemma:
He knows more than he can say. He wants to help Leon, but he also knows that revealing too much will get both of them killed. He must walk the line between mentor and gatekeeper, always holding back the most dangerous knowledge.

---

## XI. COMPARTMENTALIZATION FAILURE SCENARIOS

### Scenario A: Daniel Discovers Layer 2
**How:** Daniel finds Leon's notebook open to the symbol page, or sees Leon entering a restricted area.

**Consequence:** Daniel becomes a liability. He cannot unknow what he has seen. The Circle may decide to silence him, or Leon must reveal Layer 3 to him — breaking the rule.

**Prevention:** Leon keeps his notebook locked and his meetings private.

---

### Scenario B: Margaret Discovers Layer 2
**How:** Margaret sees someone delivering an anonymous letter to Leon, or overhears a conversation about Bellweather House.

**Consequence:** Margaret's gossip network becomes a threat. If she talks, the Circle's cover is blown. She may be intimidated, bribed, or worse.

**Prevention:** Leon meets correspondents away from Margaret's, never discusses the mystery in the tearoom.

---

### Scenario C: Arthur Reveals Too Much
**How:** Arthur, frustrated by Leon's caution, reveals Circle existence or Leon's blood connection.

**Consequence:** Arthur breaks his oaths. The Circle may retaliate. Leon's trust in Arthur is shattered.

**Prevention:** Arthur's caution is his defining trait. He will not reveal what he has promised to keep.

---

## XII. LAYER BLENDING MOMENTS

There are moments when layers temporarily bleed together:

1. **At Bellweather House:** Leon is physically in the mystery space, but must return to Layer 1 persona immediately after.
2. **In dreams:** Leon's subconscious processes Layer 4 knowledge he has not yet consciously accepted.
3. **With Helen:** She knows all layers and can move between them, but chooses what to reveal to Leon.
4. **In crisis:** When Leon is in danger, he may drop Layer 1 or 2 unconsciously, revealing Layer 3 skills or instincts.

These moments create tension and foreshadow the eventual breakdown of compartmentalization.

---

## XIII. THE FINAL UNCOMPARTMENTALIZATION

At the endgame, Leon must choose:

**Option A: Maintain the layers**
- Keep Daniel and Margaret in the dark
- Keep Arthur at arm's length
- Operate the Circle from the shadows
- Become Aurelius in secret

**Option B: Reveal selectively**
- Tell Daniel the truth, risking his life
- Tell Margaret the truth, risking her safety
- Trust Arthur completely
- Share the burden of the legacy

**Option C: Dismantle the layers**
- Reveal everything to everyone
- Expose the Circle publicly
- Destroy the secret structures that have controlled Ashford for generations
- Lose the protection of ignorance but gain authentic connection

This choice is the story's climax. Leon's decision defines who he truly is.
